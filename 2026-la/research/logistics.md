Research date: **Sun 2026-11-08**. Trip: **Thu 11/26 to Sat 11/28/2026** (2 nights). 3 adults (maybe 4), Korean students at UC Berkeley (some may be international students). Own regular 2WD car, Berkeley to LA and back.

## TL;DR

- **Route:** Berkeley → SR-13/I-580 E → Altamont → I-5 S → Grapevine (Tejon Pass) → LA. About **370–375 mi**. Google says about 5.5–6 h with no stops; OSRM's free-flow estimate (~7 h) runs long. There are **no bridge tolls** on this route in either direction.
- **FasTrak is not needed.** Stay out of the "ExpressLanes / FasTrak" lanes: I-580 in Pleasanton/Livermore, and I-10/I-110 in LA. If you enter LA Metro ExpressLanes without a tag, a notice arrives by mail for the **toll + $8 processing fee** [Official, Metro]. With 3+ people you **may** use the regular **carpool (HOV) lanes**. In Southern California these run **24/7 for 2+ occupants**, and a violation costs **at least $490** [Official, Caltrans].
- **Traffic:** Thanksgiving Day itself had "minimal traffic impact" in INRIX's 2025 forecast. On Saturday the worst window was **1–8 pm** and the best was **before 10 am** [Official, AAA/INRIX 2025]. The 2026 forecast isn't out yet; AAA usually publishes it around Nov 17. CHP's holiday Maximum Enforcement Period usually runs **Wed 6:01 pm → Sun 11:59 pm**.
- **The Grapevine (I-5, 4,144 ft)** closes for snow or ice **3–5 times per season (Oct–Apr)**, usually for 4–6 h and sometimes for more than 24 h. **There are no chain controls; CHP closes the road instead.** It closed **twice on Thanksgiving Day 2019**. NOAA's week 3–4 outlook (issued 11/6, valid 11/21–12/4) **favors above-normal precipitation across much of California** (El Niño). Detours: **US-101** (the best one), SR-58/SR-14, or SR-166 [Official, Caltrans].
- **Tule fog** in the Central Valley (Nov–Feb mornings) and **high-wind advisories** at Altamont and the Grapevine are common. Both were in effect on 11/8.
- **Gas** (AAA, 11/8/26): CA regular averages **$5.75** (a year ago: $4.70). LA–Long Beach $5.83, Oakland $5.75, Hanford–Corcoran (Kettleman City area) $5.63, Bakersfield $5.94. Budget about **$140–200 per car** for ~850 mi (≈ $50–65 each). **Costco gas is closed on Thanksgiving Day** [Secondary].
- **Thanksgiving Day closures:** the **Getty Center & Villa, The Broad and the Griffith Observatory building are CLOSED Thu 11/26** [Official]. All are open Fri and Sat.
- **Parking cheat sheet** (11/8 snapshot):
  - **Griffith**: pay-and-display, card only, ~**$10/h** [Secondary]; paid **from noon on weekdays and from 10 am on weekends**. Free if you park near the Greek Theatre and walk ~1 mi uphill, or take the DASH bus for 50¢.
  - **Getty Center**: **$25** per car, **$15 after 3 pm**, $10 after 6 pm, free Sat after 6 pm.
  - **Broad garage**: **$19 for 3 h** with validation.
  - **Hollywood (Ovation garage)**: **$3 for 2 h** with validation (4 h with a TCL Chinese ticket); $25 daily max.
  - **Santa Monica Beach Lot 1 North** (by the Pier): winter **$8 weekdays / $12 weekends** per entry.
- **LA street parking on Thanksgiving Day:** city meters, time limits, timed "No Parking/No Stopping" and street sweeping are **not enforced** unless a sign says "Holidays Enforced". On **Black Friday** only street sweeping is relaxed; meters and tow-away rules apply [Official, LADOT]. Red curbs and "No Parking Anytime" are enforced every day.
- **Car break-ins are the #1 risk** at Griffith, Hollywood, Venice/Santa Monica beach lots and trailheads. **Put everything in the trunk before you arrive** (LAPD tip) and leave nothing visible. Don't leave luggage in the car overnight or while sightseeing.
- **Weather (DTLA normals, 11/26–28):** high **~70 °F**, low **~50 °F**, **~13 % daily chance of rain**. LAX/coast runs 68/51. Bakersfield (Central Valley) runs 63/42. **Sunset 4:44 pm**, sunrise 6:36–6:38 am (USNO). Santa Ana / fire-weather season runs Oct–Mar.
- **Events that touch the trip:**
  - **Hollywood Christmas Parade** is **Sun 11/29**, but **Hollywood Blvd between Orange and Highland (TCL Chinese Theatre block) closes to cars from Sat 11/28 12:01 am**, and parade no-parking enforcement starts Sat.
  - **USC at UCLA, Rose Bowl, Sat 11/28** (kickoff TBD).

## 3. The Grapevine (I-5 over Tejon Pass)

[Official] Caltrans "Operation Snowflake" winter driving guide (rev. 2019) and Caltrans News Flash #218 (Jan 2020):
- **Full closures of I-5 for snow or ice "occur during the winter months an average of 3–5 times between October and April. Typically, closures last from 4–6 hours, however some may last longer than 24 hours."**
- **No chains.** Caltrans explains that snow and ice are usually limited to the summit and there's nowhere to fit or remove chains at that traffic volume (≈73,500 vehicles/day at peak, vs 40,500 on Donner). **CHP closes the freeway instead** (gates at Grapevine Rd for southbound and Lake Hughes Rd/Castaic for northbound). So carrying chains does **not** get you over a closed Grapevine.
- The grade is **6 % for 5 miles** on the north side.
- Operation Snowflake activates when snow is forecast at **≤ 4,500 ft**. Phases:
  - Phase 1: snow falling.
  - Phase 2: CHP pacing or escorting convoys.
  - **Phase 3A:** I-5 closed; detour on SR-58 or SR-166.
  - **3B:** SR-58 also closed; use SR-166.
  - **3C:** SR-58 and SR-166 also closed; use SR-46 and SR-41 to US-101.
  - **3D:** US-101 also closed; no detour.
- "Estimated times for re-opening the Grapevine (I-5) are not forecasted by the CHP or Caltrans."
- Info: 1-800-427-ROAD (7623), [roads.dot.ca.gov](https://roads.dot.ca.gov/), [QuickMap](https://quickmap.dot.ca.gov/), NWS.

**History relevant to this trip:**
- **Thanksgiving Day 2019 (Thu 11/28/2019):** I-5 closed **twice**, around 4:40 am for ~2 h and around 8 pm for ~2 h, with CHP escorts. SR-58 also closed briefly. Caltrans D7 tweeted that I-5 was "FULLY CLOSED at Parker Rd and Grapevine due to snow" and suggested **US-101** as the detour, "since SR-14, SR-2, & SR-33 will likely be impacted by snow as well" [ABC7 11/29/2019; Caltrans D7 on X].
- **Jan 26–27, 2025:** closed **13+ hours** for snow and ice [VC Star / KGET / SFGate, 1/27/2025].
- Other closures happened in Jan 2021 and Jan and Feb 2019 [Caltrans D6 on X; Press-Telegram; SD Union-Tribune].

**What to do:**
- A few days out, if NWS LA or NWS Hanford forecasts **snow ≤ 4,500 ft**, plan on **US-101** for that leg.
- **Fog:** Caltrans tips: slow down, use **low beams**, never drive on parking or fog lights alone, avoid high beams, roll the window down at intersections to listen, don't stop on the freeway [Official, Caltrans D3 news releases 22-177 / 23-137].
- **Wind:** high-wind advisories mostly affect trailers and high-profile vehicles. A regular car is fine, but keep both hands on the wheel near Altamont and the Grapevine.
- **2026 construction:** an **I-5 Grapevine rehab** job had crossover lanes near Lebec and Grapevine Rd in April–May 2026 [Secondary, BakersfieldNow 4/3/2026]. Caltrans lists no I-5 Grapevine construction restriction on 11/8.

### 6.2 Rules of the road worth knowing
- **New LA speed cameras:**
  - 20 cameras went live **Oct 26, 2026**; the full 125 will be up by Dec 31.
  - Each camera sends **warnings only for its first 60 days**. Real citations start in late December.
  - A ticket triggers at **11+ mph over** the limit: **$50** (11–15 mph over), **$100** (16–25), **$200** (26+).
  - First cameras include La Brea & 1st, Fairfax & Clinton, Melrose & Hayworth, and La Cienega & Pico; corridors include Sunset, Olympic, Venice and Highland [Secondary, FOX 11 10/23/2026 citing LADOT].
- **Phones:** California is hands-free. The phone must be mounted, and you may only tap or swipe once. Let the passenger navigate.
- **Dark traffic signal** (power outage, Santa Ana wind shutoffs): treat it as an **all-way stop** (CVC 21800).
- **Earthquake while driving:** pull over, stop, set the parking brake and stay inside until the shaking stops (§11).
- **US-101 Cahuenga Pass** (Hollywood ↔ Universal City): overnight lane and ramp closures, ~10 pm–6 am, during a rehab project that runs to 2029. Closures are announced weekly [Official, Caltrans D7]. **Franklin Ave eastbound** (Vine to Argyle) is down to 3 lanes through **12/5/26** for US-101 bridge work [Official, Caltrans D7].

### 7.2 LA City street-parking rules
- **Holiday enforcement** [Official, [LADOT](https://ladotparking.org/parking-enforcement/holiday-parking-regulation-exemptions/)]:
  - On **Thanksgiving Day**, these are NOT enforced unless the sign says "Holidays Enforced": **time limits, parking meters, timed "No Parking", timed "No Stopping"** (incl. peak-hour tow-away) and **street sweeping**.
  - **Street sweeping is also not enforced the day after Thanksgiving.** Nothing else is relaxed on Friday.
  - Always enforced: **red zones** (incl. bus zones), yellow/white zones, alleys, sidewalks, disabled zones, **"No Parking Anytime" / tow-away**, temporary no-parking signs, blocked driveways, and **within 15 ft of a hydrant**.
  - The city "does not have authority to waive state parking laws."
- **Santa Monica** [Official, [holiday enforcement](https://www.santamonica.gov/holiday-parking-enforcement)]: meters are not enforced on Thanksgiving Day, except meters signed as enforced daily. Street cleaning is not enforced on Thanksgiving Day **or the Friday after**.
- **Curb colors** (CA Vehicle Code 21458): **red** = no stopping. **Yellow** = commercial loading (posted hours). **White** = passenger loading, ~5 min. **Green** = time-limited parking (posted). **Blue** = disabled placard only.
- **Read the whole sign stack.** LA signs combine several layers: meters, time limits, street sweeping (e.g., "No Parking 8–10 AM Tue") and peak-hour "Tow-Away No Stopping 7–9 AM / 4–7 PM".
- **Pay apps:** LA City meters take coins and cards, and app payment at most meters via **ParkMobile / Park Smarter**. "Only the app(s) listed on the parking meter can be used." [Official, [LADOT](https://ladotparking.org/parking-meters/la-express-park-and-parking-in-la/), [LA Express Park](https://www.laexpresspark.org/apps/)]. Meter rates in DTLA, Hollywood, Venice and Westwood are demand-based and printed on each meter. Santa Monica uses its own parking app (powered by ParkMe) [Secondary, Downtown SM]. Garage-booking apps such as SpotHero and ParkWhiz work for DTLA and event days.
- **Typical fines** (2026): street sweeping ~$73, expired meter ~$68, most others $68–93 [Secondary]. A tow costs far more.

## 8. Car break-ins ("smash-and-grab")

**How big the risk is:**
- LAPD warned in Aug 2022 that **Griffith Park** had 121 vehicle break-ins that year, concentrated in **Western Canyon, Vermont Canyon and Crystal Springs, and around the Observatory and Greek Theatre**.
- Thieves can finish a break-in in about **10 seconds**. Organized crews also worked **Venice Beach** and targeted rental cars and **cars with luggage** [Secondary, LA Magazine 8/25/2022].
- In 2024 Santa Monica PD used a drone to catch break-ins at the **Pier/beach lot** [Secondary, Sacramento Bee 7/18/2024].
- Hollywood Division also posts break-in alerts for the Walk of Fame and Hollywood Sign viewpoints [Secondary, LAPD Hollywood Instagram 9/2025].
- No 2026 statistics found: **UNVERIFIED** current levels.

**What to do** (LAPD's "Lock it, Hide it, Keep it" plus its 10-tip list) [LAPD via lapd.com snippet and LAPD Nextdoor 8/27/2024]:
1. **Never leave anything in plain view.** "Thieves know that you hide your property under towels, sweaters and packages."
2. **"Secure items in your trunk prior to arriving at locations."** Thieves watch parking lots for people stashing bags.
3. Better still, leave nothing in the car: take passports, I-20/DS-2019, laptops and cameras with you, or leave them in the hotel safe.
4. Always lock doors and close windows. Park in well-lit, open, busy spots, or in staffed garages rather than remote trailheads or dark side streets.
5. **Luggage is the jackpot.** On the arrival day go to the hotel first, or keep luggage out of sight and stop only briefly. On check-out day, plan sights so the car isn't left full of bags at Griffith, Hollywood or the beach. Leave a gap between check-out and the first stop, or check out last.
6. Remove dash mounts, chargers and cables, since a visible mount suggests a phone or GPS inside. Leave the glove box empty.
7. **If it happens:**
   - Call **911** if it's in progress or someone is hurt. Otherwise call **1-877-ASK-LAPD** (Santa Monica: SMPD dispatch **310-458-8491** [Secondary]) or file an online report.
   - Take photos. Tape the window.
   - Glass shops with mobile service exist all over LA. Your insurance comprehensive coverage covers the glass, minus the deductible.
   - For a stolen passport or I-20/DS-2019, contact the consulate and the Berkeley International Office (Korean Consulate General in LA: **UNVERIFIED** contact, look it up only if needed).

## 9. Personal safety by area (common sense, not alarmism)

- **Hollywood Blvd / Walk of Fame:**
  - Crowded and touristy, but generally fine by day.
  - Known hassles: **"free CD" hustlers** who then demand money [Secondary, Eye of the Flyer 4/2024], and **costumed characters** who expect tips for photos [Secondary, Tripadvisor].
  - Say "no thanks" and keep walking. Keep phones and wallets in front pockets in crowds.
  - Late at night the boulevard gets rowdier; stay in groups.
- **DTLA:**
  - The Broad, Grand Central Market, Bunker Hill and Little Tokyo are busy and fine by day.
  - **Skid Row** is ~50 blocks bounded by **3rd St (N), 7th St (S), Alameda St (E), Main St (W)** [Wikipedia]. Visible homelessness is concentrated there, just east of the historic core and south of Little Tokyo.
  - Don't route walks through it at night. Drive between areas instead.
- **Santa Monica / Venice:**
  - The Pier and 3rd St Promenade are busy and fine.
  - **Venice Boardwalk is best by day.** Thin out at dusk.
  - Beach parking lots are the break-in hotspots.
- **Griffith / trailheads:** the people are fine; the parked cars are the risk. Bring water and a light if you're staying for sunset. The park's inbound gates close at 10 pm.
- **Everywhere:** keep phones out of reach of passing bikes and scooters at outdoor tables. Use rideshare or valet rather than walking long dark stretches. Don't engage with aggressive panhandling.
- **Fire-area neighborhoods** (Pacific Palisades, Altadena, Malibu): these are recovering communities. Don't sightsee in burned residential streets or block construction. Check for Red Flag restrictions (e.g., Caltrans let only residents onto Topanga Canyon Blvd during the Oct 15–16, 2026 Red Flag Warning) [Official, Caltrans D7].

## 11. Earthquake basics

- **Phone alerts:** California's earthquake early warning (USGS ShakeAlert with Cal OES) reaches phones through:
  - the **MyShake** app (UC Berkeley), for **M4.5+** quakes
  - **Android**'s built-in alerts, for M4.5+ quakes where weak shaking (MMI III) or more is expected
  - **Wireless Emergency Alerts** (no app needed)

  [Official, [MyShake FAQ](https://myshake.berkeley.edu/faq.html), [USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards/science/earthquake-early-warning-overview)]. iPhone users should install MyShake.
- **What to do** [Official, [Earthquake Country Alliance](https://www.earthquakecountry.org/step5/)]:
  - **Indoors:** "Drop, Cover, and Hold On", away from windows and tall furniture. Don't use elevators.
  - **In a car:** "Pull over to the side of the road, stop, and set the parking brake. Stay inside the vehicle until the shaking stops." Avoid stopping under overpasses or power lines (common guidance).
  - **On the beach** (Santa Monica/Venice): after strong shaking, **walk quickly to high ground or inland**, because "a tsunami may arrive within minutes."
  - **In bed:** stay there and cover your head with a pillow.
- Afterwards, expect aftershocks and follow WEA alerts. LA's alert systems: **NotifyLA** (city) and **Alert LA County**. Visitors get WEA automatically.
