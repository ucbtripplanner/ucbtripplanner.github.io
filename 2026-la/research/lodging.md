# Lodging research: Los Angeles, Thanksgiving 2026 (Thu 11/26 → Sat 11/28, 2 nights)

**Trip:** Check in Thu Nov 26 (Thanksgiving), check out Sat Nov 28, 2026. 3 adults, maybe a 4th. Driving our own car from Berkeley. Budget-conscious, but open to one nice meal.
**Research date:** Sun 2026-11-08. Every live rate below was observed between **02:10 and 03:05 PST on 2026-11-08**:
- Booking.com (headless Chrome, USD, en-us)
- Hotel Normandie's own SynXis engine (02:44)
- Airbnb (02:27–02:45)

Holiday prices change hourly, so treat these as a snapshot.

Legend:
- **[OBS]** observed live for these dates.
- **[SITE]** stated on the hotel's or agency's own site.
- **[BKG]** stated on the hotel's Booking.com listing (property-supplied, not independently verified).
- **[EST]** my arithmetic from sourced numbers.
- **UNVERIFIED** not confirmed.

How the prices below work:
- **Prices are the 2-night total for 1 room, before tax**, as Booking.com shows them. They already include any mandatory resort, destination or service fee. Booking's room table lists those fees under "Included" and the taxes under "Excluded".
- **"All-in" = room × (1 + tax %) + per-night flat charges + overnight parking × 2** [EST].
- Rates are the **free-cancellation** option unless noted. Nonrefundable "pay online" rates were usually 5–10 % cheaper.

## 1. TL;DR

### What matters most

- **Parking can cost as much as a cheap room.** Over two nights it ranges from **$0** (Hometel, Dunes Inn, Ramada Wilshire, Pasadena Rose & Crown) to **$120–150** (Hollywood valet-only, WeHo, The LINE) and **$143** at Embassy Suites LAX North ($71.50/night). Always compare all-in prices (§4).
- **Taxes add 12–17 %.** City of LA charges **14 % TOT plus 2 % LA Tourism Marketing District (LATMD)** at hotels with 50+ rooms, so ≈16–16.2 %. Santa Monica is 15 % + $2/night. WeHo is 12.5 % + 4 % + $7/night. Pasadena is 17 %. Glendale is 12 %. Burbank is ≈11.75 %.
  - LA's June 2026 **Measure TT** (14 → 16 %) was **defeated**.
  - LA **Measure TC** passed. It makes resort and booking fees taxable (§2).
- **Resort and destination fees are already in the price shown.** California SB 478 and the FTC fee rule require that. Examples: The LINE $38/night, Loews $35, Freehand $28, Magic Castle $22, Andaz WeHo $40. **Parking and taxes are not included.**
- **Late Thanksgiving arrival.** `routes-stops.md` suggests a Big Sur coast drive Thursday, arriving LA around 9 pm. Some budget hotels **stop checking in at 11 pm** (Dunes Inn Wilshire), 11:30 pm (Ramada) or midnight (Koxie, Freehand). Hometel's cutoff is 12:30 am [BKG]. If you book one of these, message the hotel about a late arrival.
- **Booking requirements.**
  - The booker must show a **photo ID + a credit card** at check-in. Damage or incidental deposits are typically $100–300 [BKG]. Miyako's Booking listing says **$999**.
  - **21+ to check in:** Holiday Inn Express Walk of Fame, Days Inn Santa Monica, Hotel Burbank, Four Points Westside, Hotel Calle Joaquin (SLO) and The Grove (Pismo) [BKG]. Most others are 18+.
- **Koreatown is the best base for this group.**
  - It's central: free-flow drive times are ~10–20 min to Hollywood, Griffith, DTLA and LACMA, and ~20–25 min to Santa Monica and the Getty (§3).
  - It has the most budget hotels with free or $15 parking.
  - It has walkable Korean food, which matters on Thanksgiving night when much of LA is closed (see `dining.md`).
  - **Hollywood** is the more "classic tourist" base, but costs ~$150–250 more all-in for 2 nights. Most of the difference is valet parking.
- **For 4 people:** most 2-queen rooms accept 4 adults [OBS, "Max. people: 4"].
  - **No surcharge:** Koxie, Hometel, H by H, Trylon, Rose & Crown, BW Pasadena Inn, Hotel Burbank, Four Points.
  - **Surcharge:** Dunes Inn **+$77**, The LINE +$40, BW Mid-Town +$30, Days Inn SM +$18.
  - For more space, look at Magic Castle's 1BR suite (2 doubles + sofa bed + kitchen, same price for 4) or a Koreatown 2BR Airbnb with free parking ($664–876 + 14 % tax, strict cancellation).

### Booking strategy (short version; details in §7)

1. **Now:** book **one** refundable, pay-later room at the #1–3 pick. Don't take nonrefundable rates yet.
2. **Fix the headcount (3 or 4) by ~Nov 20** and update the reservation. Some hotels charge per person, and some hosts cancel for undisclosed guests.
3. **Re-check prices Nov 18–22**, before the earliest free-cancel deadlines (Nov 19–25). Rebook if there's a cheaper refundable rate.
4. If you take the Thursday coast route (late arrival), avoid hotels with an 11 pm check-in cutoff or confirm the arrival time with them.

### Fees and price display rules
- **California SB 478** (in force since July 1, 2024) requires the advertised lodging price to include **all mandatory fees**. Only government taxes and fees may be left out. ([CA DOJ "Hidden Fees"](https://oag.ca.gov/hiddenfees); [CHLA compliance guide](https://calodging.com/member_benefits/compliance-guide-california-fee-transparency-laws/))
- **FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees** (effective May 12, 2025) applies to short-term lodging. The only charges that may be excluded from the total are government charges, shipping, and optional items. ([FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/05/ftc-rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-take-effect-may-12-2025), [FTC FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions))
- **So:** resort, destination, amenity and service fees are already in every price below. **Parking is optional, so it is extra.** Taxes are extra.
- **Airbnb (US):** "Guests in the US will see a fee-inclusive total price **before taxes**" ([Airbnb Help: Pricing display in the US](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3610)). Add ~14 % LA TOT.

## 7. Booking strategy (detailed)

1. **Book refundable and pay-later now (Nov 8–10).** Most LA candidates offer free cancellation to Nov 22–25, many with "pay nothing until" 2 days before. The nonrefundable discount is only ~5–10 % (e.g., HGI $373 vs $404; DoubleTree $296 vs $320).
   - **Avoid "free cancellation for 24 hours, pay in advance" rates.** Hometel $221, BW Mid-Town $328 (4 ppl) and Loews $597/$689 all show this type: "Free cancellation before 02:xx on November 9". It's effectively nonrefundable from Nov 9.
   - **"Pay in advance" rates with a real deadline are fine, and sometimes cheaper.** Examples:
     - Miyako Superior Room, 2 doubles: **$400**, free cancellation before 23:59 Nov 24.
     - Ramada: $349 (4 ppl), to Nov 24.
     - H by H: $371 (4 ppl), to Nov 24.
     - Sandpiper Lodge Fri night: **$226**, to Nov 24.
     - Hotel Calle Joaquin Fri night: $157, to Nov 25.
     - Madonna Inn Fri: $336, to Nov 23.
     - These are charged up front and refunded if cancelled in time [OBS].
2. **Book with the booker's own credit card.**
   - Every hotel requires "a photo ID and credit card at check-in" [BKG].
   - Deposits are $100–300 (Koxie $200, HIE $300, Freehand $300; Miyako lists $999). Debit cards may be refused or held longer **[UNVERIFIED per hotel]**.
3. **Mind the age rules.** If anyone booking is under 21, skip HIE Walk of Fame, Days Inn Santa Monica, Hotel Burbank, Four Points Westside, Hotel Calle Joaquin and The Grove Pismo [BKG].
4. **Mind the check-in cutoffs for the Thursday plan.**
   - If the group takes the coast route and arrives after ~10 pm, prefer a hotel with a 24-hour desk. Of those checked: Loews, HGI, DoubleTree, BW Mid-Town and The LINE list no end time [BKG], but confirm.
   - Avoid or call ahead to: **Dunes Inn (11 pm)**, Ramada (11:30 pm), Koxie and Freehand (midnight), Hometel (12:30 am).
5. **Headcount.** Decide 3 vs 4 by ~Nov 20 and update the reservation occupancy.
   - Some rates rise with a 4th adult: Dunes +$77, The LINE +$40, BW +$30, HGI +$18.
   - Unregistered extra guests can void a booking. This applies especially to Airbnb, whose hosts explicitly cancel without refund.
6. **Re-shop Nov 18–22.** That's before HIE's Nov 19 deadline and HGI/DoubleTree's Nov 22. Re-run the Booking search (URLs below) and the hotel's own site. Cancel and rebook only after the new booking is confirmed.
7. **Parking specifics to confirm:**
   - in/out privileges (Loews self-park has none);
   - whether spots are guaranteed (Koxie and Hometel say "reservation is not possible");
   - garage height (Ramada: no large vehicles);
   - attended or gated, for break-in risk.
   - Never leave luggage or valuables in the car (`logistics.md`).
8. **Direct vs Booking.com.** I did **not** compare Booking prices against each hotel's own engine. The only direct-engine price checked is Hotel Normandie's, which isn't on Booking for these dates. Cancellation terms matched where both were visible (The LINE). Booking direct earns loyalty points (Hilton, IHG, BW, Marriott) and is worth a 2-minute price check. Either way, read the cancellation line on the final page.
