# Berkeley ⇄ Los Angeles — routes, traffic & stops worth making (Thanksgiving 2026)

Research date: **Sun 2026-11-08**. Trip: **Thu 11/26 (Thanksgiving) → Sat 11/28/2026**, 2 nights in LA, 3 adults (+maybe 1), own 2WD car, from Berkeley.

Legend: **[Official]** = fetched from the operator / agency page on 2026-11-08. **UNVERIFIED** = could not confirm for 2026 (latest info + its date given). Drive times: **Valhalla** (valhalla1.openstreetmap.de) and **OSRM** (router.project-osrm.org) free-flow routing, no traffic, no stops, computed 2026-11-08. Valhalla's Berkeley→LA I-5 time (6.2 h) is close to the ~5.5–6 h Google gives, so it's the main figure here. OSRM runs ~10–15 % slower.

## TL;DR

- **All three corridors are open as of 2026-11-08.**
  - **CA-1 through Big Sur is fully open.** Regent's Slide reopened **Jan 14 2026** (first full Carmel–Cambria access in ~3 years). There was a short storm closure **Feb 17–20 2026**. Right now there's **one-way signal control** at Rocky Creek Bridge (through 11/30/26) and at spots between Point Sur and Big Sur (through 12/31/26).
  - **US-101:** no restrictions.
  - **I-5:** open, with temporary high-wind and dense-fog advisories on the Grapevine.
- **Thanksgiving Day closes buildings, not scenery.** These are **closed Thu 11/26**: Hearst Castle, Old Mission Santa Barbara, Mission San Juan Bautista, the Gilroy and Camarillo Premium Outlets, and the Getty Villa. Nepenthe closes at 5 pm. These are **open Thu**: Bixby Bridge, McWay Falls (8 am–sunset), the elephant seals (sunrise–sunset, every day of the year), the Pismo monarch grove (sunrise–sunset), Morro Rock, and the Santa Barbara Courthouse (10–5, court-holiday hours). **Madonna Inn serves a Thanksgiving dinner Thu 12–10 pm ($55 adult).**
- **Traffic (AAA/INRIX 2025 pattern; the 2026 forecast isn't out yet):**
  - **Thanksgiving Day = "minimal traffic."** Saturday is worst **1–8 pm** and best **before 10 am**.
  - LA's worst holiday corridor in 2025 was **LA→Bakersfield via I-5 N**: +147 %, 4 h 10 m, at the Wednesday-evening peak.
- **Daylight is short.** Sunset is about **4:44 pm in LA and 4:54 pm at Bixby Bridge**. Drive Big Sur in daylight only.
- **My pick ("Combo 1"):**
  - **Thu:** coast down. CA-1 Big Sur → Morro Bay → SLO (Madonna Inn Thanksgiving dinner) → US-101 → LA, arriving about 9 pm.
  - **Sat:** I-5 back, leaving LA by about 7:30 am, lunch at Harris Ranch, home about 3–3:30 pm.
  - Why: Big Sur on the lightest-traffic day of the week, when holiday closures don't matter for an outdoor drive, then the fastest road home before the Saturday afternoon peak.
  - **Weather swap:** if heavy rain is forecast for Big Sur Thu, do the reverse (Combo 2: I-5 down Thu, Santa Barbara/Solvang/SLO up Sat). If the Grapevine is snowy or closed Sat, come home on 101.
- **Weather risk is above normal.** NOAA CPC's Week 3–4 outlook (issued 11/6, valid 11/21–12/4) **favors above-normal precipitation for much of California** (strong El Niño) and above-normal temperatures on the coast.
  - **Grapevine:** CHP closes I-5 for snow/ice **3–5 times a season** (Oct–Apr), usually 4–6 h, sometimes more than 24 h. The **Thanksgiving 2019 storm closed it.**
  - **Big Sur:** rain can trigger rockfall and full closures (e.g. Feb 2026).
- **Gas:** CA average regular is **$5.75** (AAA, 11/8/26). The cheapest corridor metro is Hanford–Corcoran on I-5 at **$5.63**; the priciest are Bakersfield at $5.94 and SLO–Paso at $5.92. Big Sur has only ~6 stations, all expensive (Gorda is notorious), so **fill up in Monterey/Carmel.**

### 1.2 Grapevine (I-5) — the main weather risk

- **Who closes it:** CHP, with Caltrans concurrence. Closures are based on road conditions, and **chains are not an option** on the Grapevine: it simply closes. Closures happen "an average of 3–5 times between October and April. Typically closures last from 4–6 hours, however some may last longer than 24 hours," and "estimated times for re-opening … are not forecasted." [Caltrans Operation Snowflake flyer, rev. 2019]
- **Late-November precedent:** the **Nov 26 – Dec 3, 2019** storm "was disruptive to Thanksgiving travelers … and closed I-5 at Parker Road and the Grapevine." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/November_26_%E2%80%93_December_3,_2019_North_American_blizzard
- **When it's closed:** wait, or detour. The **Midway Route (SR-58 + SR-14 via Tehachapi/Mojave)** is the primary alternate (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_5_in_California). Tehachapi can get snow too, so **for us the practical fallback is US-101.**
- **Wind:** high-wind advisories (Santa Ana) on the LA side are common in late fall. One is active today.
- **Normals at Lebec (~3,500 ft, near the pass), Nov 1991–2020:** high 62 °F, low 39 °F, record low 15 °F. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebec,_California
- **Before driving:** check https://quickmap.dot.ca.gov, https://roads.dot.ca.gov/?roadnumber=5 or 1-800-427-7623 the night before and the morning of Sat 11/28.

### 1.3 Big Sur (CA-1) practical notes

- **Drive it southbound.** You're in the ocean-side lane, so every turnout is a right turn (simple geometry: the ocean is west and the US drives on the right). Northbound works but needs left turns across traffic.
- **Daylight on Thu 11/26 at Bixby Bridge** [Official, USNO]: civil dawn 6:28, sunrise 6:56, **sunset 4:54 pm**, civil dusk 5:22. At Piedras Blancas, sunset is 4:53. **Be south of Ragged Point by ~4 pm.** https://aa.usno.navy.mil/api/rstt/oneday?date=2026-11-26&coords=36.37,-121.90&tz=-8
- **Expect patchy or no cell signal** for long stretches (advice, not a sourced fact). Download offline maps and a playlist. Keep the Caltrans QuickMap page cached.
- **Restrooms are scarce.** There are none at Bixby or at the elephant seal viewpoint (elephantseal.org). Use them in Carmel, at Pfeiffer Big Sur, Nepenthe, Ragged Point, or the Hearst Castle visitor center area.
- **Weather normals, Nov 1991–2020** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Sur, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterey,_California):
  - Big Sur: high 65 °F, low 45 °F, **4.16 in rain on ~7 days.**
  - Monterey: 62/46 °F.
- **Signal delays:** the Point Sur and Rocky Creek signals add a few minutes each. The exact delay isn't stated (UNVERIFIED).

### 2.2 What this means for us

- **Going down Thu 11/26:** any departure time is fine traffic-wise. The constraint is **daylight on CA-1**, so leave Berkeley **6:30–7:00 am** for the coast route, or ~8:00 am for I-5. Getting into LA Thu evening should be light, but this is inferred (no corridor data for Thu evening; UNVERIFIED).
- **Coming back Sat 11/28:** **leave LA by 7:30 am** (INRIX: best before 10 am, worst 1–8 pm).
  - The I-5 N Grapevine climb out of LA is the corridor most exposed to holiday congestion, so clear Santa Clarita and the Grapevine early.
  - Arriving Bay Area by ~3 pm beats most of the Saturday peak. Expect slowdowns on I-5 N near the I-580/I-205 split and on I-580 W over the Altamont (no 2026 data; UNVERIFIED).
- **If you return on 101:** you'll be on the road 1–8 pm whatever you do. 101 has no single choke point like the Grapevine, but the Bay Area approach (San Jose → Berkeley) will be the slowest part Saturday evening.
- **Avoid Sunday 11/29 entirely** (not in our dates anyway).

|---|---|

**Why this one:**
1. Big Sur happens on the one day INRIX calls "minimal traffic," and the stops are outdoors, so Thanksgiving closures don't matter.
2. It includes a proper Thanksgiving dinner (Madonna Inn) on the way.
3. The return is the fastest road, beating the 1–8 pm Saturday peak.
4. Only one long day.

**Risks:**
- A rainy Thu means rockfall/closures on CA-1. Check QuickMap Wed night; if CA-1 is closed or heavy rain is forecast, switch to **Combo 2**.
- A snowy Sat means the Grapevine closes. Return on 101 instead (as in Combo 3, about +1.5 h).

### Verdict

**Go with Combo 1.** It's the classic first-timer move (Big Sur southbound), puts the long day on the emptiest roads of the week, adds a real Thanksgiving dinner, and makes Saturday the easy day. Decision rule for the morning of Wed 11/25:

- **Big Sur forecast heavy rain, or CA-1 shows a closure on QuickMap** → Combo 2.
- **Grapevine snow/closure forecast for Sat** → swap Saturday to the Combo 3 return (101).
- Want Santa Barbara too and OK with two long days → Combo 3.
