## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Winner: the "Balanced Classic" candidate (I-5 down Thursday → 2 nights Koreatown → US-101 home Saturday via Santa Barbara + Solvang).** I grafted in a few pieces from the runners-up:
- the Madonna Inn break on the way home
- the Big Sur coast-down and I-5-home versions as weather/energy variants
- the Getty/Venice/Endeavour swaps
- the Park's BBQ and Tam O'Shanter backups
- the booking traps found in lodging research

I also fixed several timing and fact errors (see §8).

Why it wins:
1. **It puts every stop on a day it's actually open.** Thanksgiving closes buildings but not beaches. So Thursday gets the outdoor icons that are open on the holiday: the Santa Monica Pier sunset, Beverly Hills/Rodeo, Urban Light, and a verified Thanksgiving dinner. Friday gets the buildings that are closed Thursday: the Griffith Observatory building, The Broad. Saturday gets the Central Coast sights that are closed Thursday: the Santa Barbara Mission, and Solvang's Julefest.
2. **Hollywood lands on the only day it works.** That's Friday: the TCL/Ovation block closes **Sat 12:01 AM** for the parade (verified 11/8).
3. **Thursday takes the fastest road on the emptiest travel day of the week.** It puts us in LA by about 2 PM with daylight left.
4. **Saturday uses the snow-free coast road.** It avoids the Grapevine on its riskiest day and direction, and turns the drive into the "stops on the way" the user asked for.

## 2. Rationale (the key choices, checked against the research)

1. **I-5 down on Thanksgiving.**
   - AAA/INRIX (2025 pattern) expects "minimal traffic" on Thanksgiving Day.
   - I-5 is the fastest road (6.2 h free-flow), so we're in Koreatown by ~14:10 with 2.5 h of daylight.
   - What a coast-down would gain is mostly closed that day: SB Mission and Hearst Castle are closed, Nepenthe closes at 5 PM, and Café Kevah and Ragged Point hours are UNVERIFIED.
   - Big Sur Thursday is a ~15-h day with zero LA time. It also has one-way signal zones (Rocky Creek, Point Sur–Big Sur) and rockfall risk under an "above-normal precipitation" El Niño outlook (NOAA CPC, 11/6).
   - Big Sur stays available as Variant V1 for a dry forecast.
2. **Thursday only uses things that lose nothing on Thanksgiving.**
   - Santa Monica Pier sunset: Pacific Park 11–9, verified.
   - Beverly Hills sign and Rodeo: outdoors.
   - Lawry's: 11–9, verified.
   - Urban Light: 24 h.
   - The loop is ~75 min of light holiday driving, and LA and Santa Monica meters aren't enforced on the holiday.
   - Griffith moves to Friday, when the building, planetarium and telescopes are open, not just the terrace.
3. **Friday clusters the east side.**
   - Hollywood is forced to Friday by the Sat 12:01 AM parade closures (verified).
   - The order Hollywood → DTLA (Broad, Disney Hall, Angels Flight, GCM) → hotel rest → Griffith (sunset + night) → Koreatown BBQ keeps total driving to ~90–110 min on Black Friday.
   - The Black Friday malls (The Grove, Farmers Market) are left as alternates, because the crowds are "extreme".
4. **Saturday home on US-101.**
   - It's the only day the Central Coast stops are all open: the SB Mission (Mon–Sat 9:30–5), the Courthouse (weekends 10–5) and Solvang's Julefest (from 11/27).
   - It avoids the Grapevine. The Grapevine closes 3–5×/season for snow with no chain option, and I-5 N out of LA was LA's most congested 2025 holiday corridor.
   - The Madonna Inn break turns the obligatory mid-drive stop into a sight.
   - It costs ~4.5 h more than I-5 direct (Variant V3 keeps that option).
5. **Two nights in Koreatown, with no hotel switch.**
   - It's central: 9–23 min free-flow to everything.
   - It has the cheapest all-in hotels with cheap or free parking.
   - It has walkable Korean food.
   - Not switching hotels means luggage is never in the car at Griffith or Hollywood.
6. **Exactly one Thanksgiving dinner** (Lawry's, Thu 18:00), with two cheaper or Korean alternatives (§4). Everything else is cheap: Bravo Farms, Grand Central Market, Porto's, Lilly's, Solvang bakeries.
7. **Theme parks are skipped on purpose.** Universal or Disney would eat the only full LA day at peak holiday prices (Universal ~$109–149 + $35 parking; Disneyland $104–224).
