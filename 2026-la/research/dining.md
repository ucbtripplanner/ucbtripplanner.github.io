# LA Thanksgiving Road Trip 2026: Food, Thanksgiving Dinner, En-route Eats & Groceries

Research date: **2026-11-08**. Trip: **Thu Nov 26 (Thanksgiving) → Sat Nov 28, 2026**, 2 nights, 3 adults (+ maybe 1), UC Berkeley students, budget-conscious but OK with one nice meal. Driving Berkeley ⇄ Los Angeles (I-5 ≈ 5–6 h; US-101 / CA-1 alternatives).

**How to read this**
- **TOP PICK / GOOD / BACKUP** = recommendation for *this* group (first-timers, budget, Korean students who already live near Bay Area chains like In-N-Out / Din Tai Fung / Salt & Straw).
- **VERIFIED** = read on the venue's own site (or its own social post) during this research. Even "verified" regular hours are the *currently posted* hours, not a guarantee for Thanksgiving week.
- **UNVERIFIED** = could not confirm for 2026, or source is third-party / older. The source and its date are given.
- Coordinates: `[lat, lng]` with source: **OSM** = OpenStreetMap object (Overpass API), **Nominatim** = OSM address geocode. Address-level points can be ~10–30 m off the door.
- Prices are copied from the menu/source shown, **before tax and tip** unless noted. Where no menu price could be confirmed, only a tier is given and marked *(tier = estimate)*: `$` < ~$15/person · `$$` ~$15–35 · `$$$` ~$35–70 · `$$$$` > $70.
- **Sales tax changed on Oct 1, 2026** (CDTFA Special Notice L-1037): LA County / City of LA / Beverly Hills **10.25%** (was 9.75%), Pasadena · Glendale · Burbank · West Hollywood · Long Beach **11.0%**, Santa Monica · Culver City **11.25%**, Inglewood **10.75%**. Add ~18–20% tip at sit-down places → menu price × ~1.3 is a realistic all-in. Source: https://cdtfa.ca.gov/formspubs/L1037.pdf (via https://spidell.com/news/hot-news-and-notes/new-sales-and-use-tax-rates/, Aug 31 2026)
