# Los Angeles: Sights and First-Timer Course (Thanksgiving weekend 2026)

**Researched:** 2026-11-08 · **Trip:** Thu Nov 26 (Thanksgiving) – Sat Nov 28, 2026, 2 nights · 3 adults (+ maybe 1) · own 2WD car from Berkeley

**How to read this:** Facts come with a source link. Anything I could not confirm for 2026 is marked **UNVERIFIED**, along with the most recent information I found and its date. ## 0. TL;DR

- **Thanksgiving Day (Thu 11/26) closes most museums.** All of these are closed:
  - Griffith Observatory *building* (the grounds stay open)
  - Getty Center and Getty Villa
  - LACMA, The Broad, Petersen
  - Natural History Museum, California Science Center
  - Warner Bros. Studio Tour, LA Zoo Lights, Descanso lights
  - Academy Museum and The Huntington: likely closed too (they were in 2025)
- **Open on Thanksgiving:**
  - Outdoor icons: Walk of Fame, TCL Chinese forecourt, Lake Hollywood Park, Griffith grounds and terrace, Urban Light (24 h), beaches
  - Santa Monica Pier, with Pacific Park rides 11 AM–9 PM
  - Universal Studios (9 AM–9 PM) and Disneyland
  - **Treat Thursday as the driving day plus an evening sunset or city-lights stop.**
- **Fri 11/27 is the one full LA day.** It is Black Friday: malls like The Grove (8 AM–10 PM) are packed and traffic is heavy. Museums keep normal Friday hours.
- **The classic first-timer core (MUST):**
  - Griffith Observatory: sunset or night view; also the best Hollywood Sign view
  - Lake Hollywood Park: the closest legal Hollywood Sign photo
  - Hollywood Walk of Fame and TCL Chinese Theatre forecourt
  - Santa Monica Pier at sunset
  - LACMA *Urban Light*: free, 24 h
- **Strong second tier (RECOMMENDED):**
  - Venice Boardwalk and Canals
  - Beverly Hills sign and Rodeo Drive (city garages: first 2 h free)
  - The Grove and Original Farmers Market: holiday tree, plus "snow" nightly at 6 and 8 PM
  - Getty Center: free entry with timed reservation; parking $25
  - Downtown cluster: Grand Central Market, The Broad (free, book), Walt Disney Concert Hall, The Last Bookstore, Union Station and Olvera Street
  - Koreatown for dinner
  - **NEW:** the Space Shuttle *Endeavour* standing upright at the California Science Center. Its new hall opened 2026-11-13; entry is free, but book a timed reservation now ($4 fee)
- **Fire-recovery and closure notes for Nov 2026:**
  - **PCH is open.** Sunset Blvd → Carbon Beach Terrace (Malibu) is still an active Caltrans work zone: 25 mph, with weekday single-lane closures 9 AM–3 PM possible.
  - **Getty Villa reopened** on 2025-06-27.
  - **Point Dume:** the beach road and lower parking lot are closed (erosion, since Aug 2026). The beach is still reachable on foot.
  - **Japanese American National Museum** is closed for renovation until 2027-01-23.
  - **La Brea Tar Pits museum** is closed for construction; the outdoor park is open.
  - **Runyon Canyon:** the West Trail is closed.
- **Theme parks: not recommended for this trip** *(analysis)*.
  - Thanksgiving week is one of the busiest of the fall.
  - Prices: Universal 1-day about $109–$149 + $35 parking; Disneyland $104–$224 by tier, and it is about an hour away in Anaheim.
  - A park day would use up the only full LA day. Universal is OPTIONAL only if the group cares more about it than about the classic sights. Disneyland is a SKIP.
- **Light:**
  - Sunrise about **6:36–6:38 AM**; sunset about **4:45–4:47 PM**; civil dusk (city lights on, "blue hour") about **5:12–5:14 PM**.
  - Plan any sunset spot for ~4:15–5:15 PM.
- **Not in conflict with the trip:**
  - The Hollywood Christmas Parade is **Sun 11/29, 6 PM**, the day after you leave.
  - The Grove tree lighting (11/16) and Rodeo Drive lighting (11/19) happen before the trip, but the decorations will be up.
  - **UCLA hosts USC at the Rose Bowl on Sat 11/28**: expect Pasadena traffic that day.

### 3.1 Hollywood & Griffith Park

#### 1. Griffith Observatory: **MUST**
- **Area / type:** Los Feliz, in Griffith Park. Free observatory with terraces overlooking the LA basin, plus a straight-on view of the Hollywood Sign.
- **Time needed:** 1.5–2.5 h (building, terraces, maybe a planetarium show).
- **Cost:**
  - Admission to the building, grounds and public telescopes is "always free" ([getting here](https://griffithobservatory.lacity.gov/visit/getting-here/)).
  - Samuel Oschin Planetarium shows: adults 13–54 **$12**, students and teachers with ID **$10**. Tickets are sold **on-site only, same day only**, cash or card ([planetarium](https://griffithobservatory.lacity.gov/explore/samuel-oschin-planetarium/)).
  - Weekday shows run 12:45–~4:45 PM; first weekend show 10:45 AM.
- **Parking:**
  - Paid pay-and-display, **credit card only**, at the Observatory lot and along W. Observatory Rd and Western Canyon Rd.
  - Paid hours are Mon–Fri 12–10 PM and Sat–Sun 10 AM–10 PM ([getting here](https://griffithobservatory.lacity.gov/visit/getting-here/)). Rec & Parks: "enforced every day of the year … fees increase over peak season/holidays" ([RAP plan your trip](https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/griffithpark/planyourtrip)).
  - **Rate:** official pages don't print it. 2026 third-party guides say **about $10/hour** ([washedup](https://washedup.app), [griffithparktours.com](https://griffithparktours.com)). **UNVERIFIED**, and could be higher on the holiday.
  - **Cheap option:** park free in the Greek Theatre area (no concerts in November; the season runs Apr–Oct) and walk up about 1 mile uphill, or ride **DASH Observatory/Los Feliz**.
  - DASH runs daily 10 AM–10 PM, every 20–25 min. Fare **50¢, or 35¢ with TAP**; exact change; it runs even on days the building is closed ([getting here](https://griffithobservatory.lacity.gov/visit/getting-here/)).
  - Drive up **West** Observatory Rd; East Observatory Rd is one-way down. The left lane goes to the lot.
- **Hours:**
  - Thu 11/26 **building CLOSED**; grounds open 5 AM–10:30 PM.
  - Fri 11/27 building 12 PM–10 PM.
  - Sat 11/28 10 AM–10 PM.
  - Inbound park gates close at 10 PM.
- **Best time:** sunset (4:45 PM) through ~7 PM for the lights, or after dinner (7:30–9:30 PM) when the sunset crowd has left *(analysis)*. The official site: busiest "on weekends, during summer and holiday periods, and at sunset". It uses capacity control with 5–15 minute waits, and "road closures are likely when traffic becomes congested".
- **Crowds:** very high at sunset on a holiday weekend.
- **Coords:** [34.11833, -118.30033] (WP). Greek Theatre lot: [34.11949, -118.29643] (OSM).
- *(analysis)* On Thanksgiving the terrace view is still available even though the building is closed. Restrooms are next to the parking lot. On Friday you can do both: the building in the evening, plus a planetarium show if you arrive before ~4:45 PM.

#### 2. Lake Hollywood Park (Hollywood Sign close-up): **MUST** (or swap for the Griffith sign view if short on time)
- **Area / type:** Hollywood Hills residential area. Small grassy park directly below the Sign; the classic "Sign behind you" photo.
- **Time:** 20–40 min. **Cost:** free.
- **Parking:** street parking only on Canyon Lake Dr and nearby streets; obey posted restrictions. This is a residential area with frequent enforcement. Exact rules are **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Hours:** sunrise–sunset daily ([LA Rec & Parks](https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/park/lake-hollywood)). Same on all 3 days.
- **Best time:** morning (the sun is behind you, from the east or southeast, lighting the Sign) *(analysis)*.
- **Crowds:** moderate; tour vans at midday.
- **Coords:** [34.12689, -118.32592] (OSM, "Lake Hollywood Park"). The Sign itself: [34.13406, -118.32159] (WP).
- **Note:** the Hollywood Sign Trust's official list of viewpoints names only Ovation Hollywood and Griffith Observatory ([hollywoodsign.org/viewpoints](https://www.hollywoodsign.org/viewpoints)). Lake Hollywood Park is a city park, but neighbors are sensitive to tourists. Hiking to the Sign itself is not worth it for this trip.

#### 3. Hollywood Walk of Fame: **MUST**
- **Area / type:** Hollywood Blvd (La Brea → Gower) and Vine St. Public sidewalk; free; always open.
- **Time:** 45–90 min, combined with #4 and #5.
- **Parking:**
  - **Ovation Hollywood garage** (entrances on Highland Ave and Orange Ct): **$2.50 per 20 min, $25 daily max** ([Hollywood Partnership, 2025-10](https://hollywoodpartnership.com/go/ovation-parking-hollywood-and-highland)).
  - With validation, reportedly **$3 for up to 2 h** (participating shops), or **4 h with TCL Chinese Theatre validation** ([Starline parking page](https://starlinetours.com/parking/)). **UNVERIFIED** for Nov 2026.
- **Best time:** 8:30–10:30 AM, before costumed characters, ticket touts and tour crowds peak. Or at night for the lights *(analysis)*.
- **Crowds:** high from midday into the evening.
- **Coords:** [34.10166, -118.34253] (OSM, "Walk of Fame" near Orange Dr).
- *(analysis)* Hold on to bags and phones. Ignore people pushing CDs or "free" photos. The stretch between Highland and Orange (the TCL/Dolby block) is the part to see; east of Vine is thinner.

#### 6. Hollywood Bowl Overlook (Mulholland Dr): **OPTIONAL**

- **Hours:** **UNVERIFIED.** Some Mulholland overlooks post no-stopping rules after dark.
- **Best time:** late afternoon.

#### 7. Runyon Canyon Park: **OPTIONAL**
- **Type:** short, steep, very popular hike (about 2–3 mi loop) with Hollywood and downtown views.
- **Time:** 1–1.5 h. **Cost:** free.
- **Parking:** street parking on Fuller Ave and the surrounding residential streets (posted limits).
- **Hours:** sunrise–sunset. "**Runyon Park is now open, however the West Trail is closed**" ([RAP](https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/park/runyon-canyon), checked 2026-11-08).
- **Best time:** early morning.
- **Crowds:** high on weekends and holidays.
- **Coords:** [34.11204, -118.3501] (OSM).
- *(analysis)* Only worth it if the group wants exercise. Griffith and Lake Hollywood give similar views with no effort.

### 3.2 Westside: Beverly Hills, Mid-Wilshire, The Grove

#### 13. LACMA + *Urban Light*: **MUST** (Urban Light) · museum **OPTIONAL**
- **Type:** Chris Burden's 202 restored street lamps, LA's most famous photo spot. It is "**open 24 hours a day**" ([LACMA](https://www.lacma.org/guides/urbanlight)). The museum is the largest in the western US. The new David Geffen Galleries have opened and the LACMA Store has moved into them ([LACMA visit](https://www.lacma.org/visit)).
- **Time:** Urban Light 15–20 min; museum 2–3 h.
- **Cost:**
  - Non-LA County adults **$30**, students 18+ with ID **$26**. LA County residents pay $25/$21.
  - Advance timed tickets are "highly recommended"; slots sell out ([LACMA tickets](https://www.lacma.org/tickets)).
- **Hours:** museum **closed Thanksgiving**, Fri 11 AM–8 PM, Sat 10 AM–7 PM. Also closed every Wednesday ([hours](https://www.lacma.org/visit/plan-your-visit/hours)).
- **Parking:** Pritzker Parking Garage, 6th St east of Fairfax, open daily 5 AM–10 PM. **$24 flat ($16 after 8 PM entry).** The Academy Museum and Petersen share this cluster ([LACMA parking](https://www.lacma.org/visit/plan-your-visit/parking)). Metro D Line Wilshire/Fairfax station opened 2026-05-08.
- **Best time:** dusk (lamps on, ~4:45–5:30 PM) or night. Mornings are emptiest *(analysis)*.
- **Crowds:** the Urban Light photo line can be busy at sunset.
- **Coords:** Urban Light [34.06305, -118.35922] (OSM); LACMA [34.0633, -118.3592] (WP).
- **Add-on (free):** La Brea Tar Pits outdoor park (Hancock Park) next door. The **museum is closed for construction**; "Many outdoor areas of Hancock Park remain open" ([tarpits.org](https://tarpits.org/)). [34.0628, -118.356] (WP).

## 4. Is a theme-park day sensible? *(analysis)*

**Short answer: no, not on this 2-night trip.**

1. **Time budget:**
   - Thu is mostly the 6+ h drive down; Sat is mostly the drive back.
   - **Fri is the only full LA day.** A theme park would replace Griffith, Hollywood, Santa Monica and Beverly Hills: everything a first-timer comes for.
2. **Crowds:** Thanksgiving week to Black Friday is peak demand. Disneyland sits in high price tiers. Universal is open 9 AM–9 PM all three days, which signals that it expects big crowds.
3. **Cost per person:** about $109–$149 for Universal (+$35 per car parking), or $104–$224 for Disneyland, before food and Express or Lightning Lane.
4. **If the group insists:**
   - Choose **Universal**. It is 11 min from Hollywood, has a Studio Tour that doubles as "Hollywood", and has Super Nintendo World.
   - Go **Thanksgiving Day itself**, but only if you drive down Wednesday night (not the plan), or add a night.
   - Otherwise, the **Warner Bros. Studio Tour** ($79, Fri or Sat, 8:30 AM–3:30 PM) gives the "movie magic" in 3 hours.

## 8. Things to book or check

**Book now:**
1. **California Science Center / Endeavour:** timed reservation ($4 per person). Most likely to sell out.
2. **Getty Center** and/or **Getty Villa:** free timed reservation.
3. **The Broad:** free timed ticket. Also try same-day releases; the Infinity Mirror Room is likely gone for November.
4. **LACMA** (if visiting the galleries), **WB Studio Tour** or **Universal** (if chosen), **Zoo Lights** (if chosen).

**Day before:**
- Griffith Observatory calendar.
- [Caltrans QuickMap](https://quickmap.dot.ca.gov/) for PCH lane closures.
- Point Dume / El Matador status ([LA County Beaches](https://beaches.lacounty.gov/point-dume-beach/), [State Parks](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=633)).
- Hours for: The Last Bookstore, Grand Central Market (Thanksgiving), Academy Museum, Huntington (2026 Thanksgiving notices).
