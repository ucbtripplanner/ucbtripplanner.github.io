## 0. Verdict

**Winner: a graft of both candidates.**

From candidate 1 ("근본 멘도시노 클래식"), I kept:
- the overall skeleton and most of the Korean slot text;
- Thursday's Hwy 128 sequence (Hendy Woods → Apple Farm → Navarro Vineyards → redwood tunnel);
- Mendocino village, then the Headlands low tide and sunset;
- Friday as an all-Fort-Bragg day;
- Saturday: Noyo Harbor morning, Russian Gulch, Mendocino village, Boonville lunch, then home on CA-128;
- Harbor Lite Lodge as the #1 room.

From candidate 2 ("느긋한 근본"), I took:
- **Thanksgiving dinner 2 minutes from the room** (HarborView, Noyo Harbor Inn). This cuts out the after-dinner dark CA-1 drive, and the luggage gets unloaded *before* dinner, not at 19:30.
- **Friday's evening order:** Festival of Lights at the 17:00 doors, when parking is easier and you're well ahead of the 18:45 last shuttle, then dinner at Sea Pal Cove, which is open until 22:00 on Friday.
- **Saturday's south-flowing order** (Fort Bragg → Russian Gulch → Mendocino → CA-128). There's no backtracking, and the village shops are opening when you get there.
- **Super 8 as a second $0 lodging hold.** It's the budget switch, and the plan doesn't change because it's 150 m away.

**Why this shape:**
1. **Thanksgiving closes the indoor things, not the coast.**
   - Closed on Thursday: the Skunk Train (no departures), the Botanical Gardens, the Point Arena Lighthouse, Roederer, the Boonville Hotel, Paysanne and GoodLife.
   - So Thursday is the drive day, using only things that are open or very likely open: Hendy Woods (8:00 to sunset), the self-serve Apple Farm stand, Navarro Vineyards (11/26 is on its tour calendar), the free Headlands, and a verified Thanksgiving menu.
2. **Tides decide the beaches** (NOAA Noyo Harbor 9417426):
   - **Thursday 15:30–17:00 is the trip's only daylight low tide** (+1.7 → −0.7 ft), so the Mendocino Headlands tide pools go there.
   - Friday's falling tide (+3.4 → +1.5 ft) goes to Glass Beach.
   - Saturday's rising tide (toward the 12:53 high of 6.7 ft) goes to the Devil's Punchbowl.
3. **Everything that needs Friday or Saturday gets it:**
   - the Skunk Train, Fri/Sat only;
   - the Festival of Lights, opening night Fri 11/27;
   - the village shops, Black Friday / Small Business Saturday;
   - GoodLife, Disco Ranch and Paysanne, all closed Thursday.
4. **Daylight discipline.**
   - Every curvy CA-128 and CA-1 segment is driven before 16:54.
   - The only after-sunset drives are the 15-minute Mendocino → Fort Bragg hop in civil twilight on Thursday, and 2–10-minute hops inside Fort Bragg.
5. **One base for both nights** at the south end of Fort Bragg, Noyo Harbor.
   - On Friday every leg is ≤ 20 min.
   - The car is empty on Friday.
   - Holds are refundable until 11/24.

## 11. Risks (the site should carry these)

1. **Holiday sell-outs.**

   - NOAA CPC favors above-normal California precipitation 11/21–12/4.
   - CA-128 fully closes at NVRC1 23 ft. It reached that in Jan 2026, Feb 2025, Feb 2024 and twice in 2023. At 17 ft and rising, use CA-20.

3. **Ocean.**
   - Thursday is a King Tide day (11/24–26), with midday highs of 7.2–7.5 ft.
