Legend:
- **[OBS]** observed live for these exact dates and party size.
- **[ENGINE]** read from the property's own booking engine or availability calendar, not from an OTA.
- **[SITE]** stated on the property's or agency's own website.
- **[BKG]** stated on the property's Booking.com listing (property-supplied, not independently verified).
- **[EST]** my arithmetic from sourced numbers.
- **UNVERIFIED** not confirmed.

How the prices below work:
- **Booking.com and webrez prices are the 2-night total for 1 room, before tax.** Booking's room table lists mandatory property fees under "Included" (for example the $4/night service charge at Baymont, 8.75 % at Seabird) and taxes under "Excluded".
- **"All-in" = pre-tax price × (1 + official tax rate) + flat per-night fees** [EST]. I use the **official** rate for the property's location (§2), not the rate the property typed into Booking. Those vary from 13 % to 15 % for the same city.
- **Parking was free at every property checked** [BKG "Free parking" / "Free private parking"]. None of these towns has hotel valet fees, so parking adds $0.
- **Airbnb shows "Prices include all fees"** (cleaning and service). Whether the county lodging tax is also included depends on the host (§2.3).
- Unless noted, rates are the **free-cancellation option**. Where a cheaper non-refundable "Pay online" rate exists, it is listed separately.

**Book one refundable room today.** Either Harbor Lite direct (48 h) or Super 8 / Surf Motel on Booking (free until 11/23–24). Use it as a hold while the group decides Mendocino vs. Yosemite vs. LA.

- **Don't book Agate Cove, Seagull Inn, Stanford Inn or other 14/15/30-day-policy inns until the trip is final.** Their refund windows are effectively closed now.
- If you hold two refundable rooms, **cancel the spare before the earliest deadline** (calendar in §7).

### 2.3 Airbnb and the county tax
- Airbnb's California occupancy-tax page **does not list Mendocino County or Fort Bragg** among the places where Airbnb collects tax automatically (read 11/11) [[Airbnb help 2297](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2297)].
- That means hosts collect the tax themselves. Some build it into the price: the Ten Mile suite host writes "**11% of all booking funds go directly to Mendocino County for Transient Occupancy Taxes**" [listing text]. Others may bill it separately.
- **Ask the host before booking whether the shown total already includes the 11 % / 15 %** (UNVERIFIED per listing).
