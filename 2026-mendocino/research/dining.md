# Mendocino · Fort Bragg Thanksgiving Trip 2026: Food, Thanksgiving Dinner, En-route Eats & Groceries

Research date: **2026-11-10/11**. Trip: **Thu Nov 26 (Thanksgiving) → Sat Nov 28, 2026**, 2 nights, 3 adults (+ maybe 1), UC Berkeley students, budget-conscious but OK with one nice meal. Own 2WD car from Berkeley. Main route: US-101 → Cloverdale → CA-128 (Anderson Valley: Boonville / Philo) → CA-1 → Mendocino / Fort Bragg. Alternatives: US-101 → Willits → CA-20 → Fort Bragg; or CA-1 south along the coast (Point Arena · Gualala · Jenner · Bodega Bay) on the way home.

**How to read this**
- **TOP PICK / GOOD / BACKUP** = recommendation for *this* group (first-timers, budget, one nice meal).
- **VERIFIED** = read on the venue's own website, its own booking page (Resy / Eventbrite / Toast data) or an official agency page during this research (11/10–11). Regular hours are the *currently posted* hours, not a promise for Thanksgiving week.
- **UNVERIFIED** = could not confirm for 2026, or the source is third-party / old. The source and its date are given.
- **Search engines were blocked for this run** (Bing/Brave/DDG rate-limited or captcha'd), so everything below comes from direct fetches of official sites, booking-platform data, OpenStreetMap, Visit Mendocino County's own holiday-hours PDFs (2024 and 2025) and local news. Facebook/Instagram/Yelp/Tock pages could not be read. Anything that normally lives only on Facebook (many small Fort Bragg diners) stays UNVERIFIED.
- Coordinates: `[lat, lng]`. **OSM** = OpenStreetMap object (Overpass API, fetched 11/10); **Nominatim** = OSM address geocode (matched name/address shown in §6). Address-level points can be ~10–30 m off the door.
- Prices are copied from the menu shown, **before tax and tip** unless noted. If no menu price could be confirmed, only a tier is given and marked *(tier = estimate)*: `$` < ~$15/person · `$$` ~$15–35 · `$$$` ~$35–70 · `$$$$` > $70.
- **Sales tax (CDTFA rate lookup, 11/11):** Fort Bragg **9.25%**, Point Arena **9.25%**; **Mendocino village, Little River, Albion, Caspar, Boonville/Philo (unincorporated Mendocino County) 7.875%**. Source: CDTFA rate-by-address service (https://services.maps.cdtfa.ca.gov/api/taxrate/GetRateByAddress, queried for 444 N Main St Fort Bragg, 10483 Lansing St Mendocino, 14050 Hwy 128 Boonville, 185 Main St Point Arena). The Oct 1 2026 district-tax notice (CDTFA L-1037) lists no Mendocino County change.
- **Several places add a mandatory service charge instead of (or on top of) tips:** Little River Inn **20% on all checks**, Boonville Hotel **20% (no tips expected)**, HarborView **20% on the Thanksgiving dinner + 2% card surcharge**, The Wharf **2% kitchen gratuity on all checks**. A realistic all-in is still about **menu price × 1.25–1.3**.

### 1.5 Recommendation

1. **Book now (11/11–11/13):** **Little River Inn** (best price for a real turkey dinner; ~$115 all-in with the 20% service fee) at **5:00 or 5:30 PM**, which leaves time for a sunset walk on Mendocino Headlands first. **Backup:** **Trillium** (turkey $105, walk from village lodging). **If everyone is fine with vegan:** Ravens $95.
