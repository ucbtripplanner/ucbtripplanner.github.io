### Daylight, tides and weather

- **Very short days.** Sunrise is about 7:11 AM and sunset about 4:54 PM on all three days ([USNO](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/data/RS_OneDay)). Plan to be at the coast by about 3 PM.
- **Thu 11/26 falls inside the statewide King Tide window (Nov 24–26, 2026)** ([Coastal Commission](https://www.coastal.ca.gov/kingtides/)).
  - The mid-day highs are about 7.2–7.5 ft every day (NOAA Noyo Harbor). That is good for **storm and wave watching** (Devil's Punchbowl, headlands) and bad for beach walking at midday.
  - **The low tides come after dark** (6:36 PM, 7:27 PM, 8:21 PM).
  - **The only usable low-tide light is late Thursday afternoon**: about +0.8 ft at 4:00 PM, falling to about −0.7 ft at 5:00 PM. Friday is about +1.5 ft at 4:30 PM, falling to +0.7 ft at 5:00 PM. Saturday has nothing usable.
- **Gray whales: November is too early.** State Parks says crowds come "from mid-December to early April" for the migration ([MacKerricher brochure](https://www.parks.ca.gov/pages/436/files/MacKerricherSPFinalWebLayout073117.pdf)). Expect few or no whales and don't plan around them. The **harbor seals at Laguna Point** and the **sea lions at Noyo Harbor** are the dependable wildlife.
- **Weather:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals for November at "FT BRAGG 5 N" are a high of 55.9 °F, a low of 42.6 °F and 4.55 in of rain for the month ([NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&stations=USC00043161&dataTypes=MLY-TMAX-NORMAL,MLY-TMIN-NORMAL,MLY-PRCP-NORMAL&format=json)). Bring rain shells and layers.

### The classic first-timer core

**MUST:**
1. Mendocino Headlands and the village, at sunset
2. Point Cabrillo Light Station
3. Glass Beach plus the Fort Bragg Coastal Trail and the Pudding Creek Trestle. Collecting glass is prohibited.
4. MacKerricher's Laguna Point boardwalk, for the harbor seals
5. Russian Gulch: Devil's Punchbowl and the Headlands Trail
6. The Hwy 128 Anderson Valley and Navarro redwoods drive, which is on the way

**RECOMMENDED:**
- Skunk Train *Pudding Creek Express*
  - Fri and Sat at 11:00 and 1:45 PM, 1.5 h, from $66.65 per person
  - Thanksgiving weekend books up, so reserve now
- Festival of Lights (Fri or Sat)
- Noyo Harbor
- Hendy Woods redwoods
- Philo Apple Farm stand
- Navarro Vineyards (21+ wine; grape-juice tasting for everyone else)
- Point Arena Lighthouse, only if you come home down Hwy 1 on Saturday

### Skip or optional

- **SKIP Bowling Ball Beach for this weekend unless you chase Thursday's 4:15–4:55 PM window.** The balls show only at low tide, and every other daylight hour that weekend has a mid or high tide (see §3).
- **SKIP the "Mr. Skunk" Christmas train.** It leaves from **Willits only**, which is OSRM 55 min inland from Fort Bragg, and it's aimed at families.

### Rules and costs

- **Glass Beach rule (official):** "All features of the park are protected by law and may not be disturbed or collected, including glass found at Glass Beach" ([MacKerricher infosheet](https://www.parks.ca.gov/pages/436/files/MacKerricherSP_Infosheert_092226_FINAL.pdf)).
- **Budget tip:**
  - Russian Gulch, Van Damme and Hendy Woods each charge **$10 vehicle day use**; MacKerricher charges **$8**.
  - All four accept the free **California State Library Parks Pass**, which you can check out with a California public-library card. It covers one vehicle (≤9 people) for day use, but not museums or tours ([State Parks](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=30806)).
  - Everything else on the core list is free or donation-based.
- **Road notes, Caltrans as of 2026-11-11 00:06** ([SR 1](https://roads.dot.ca.gov/roadscell.php?roadnumber=1)):
  - Hwy 1 has one-way traffic control for a **rock slide 14.8–16.5 mi north of Point Arena** (up to 10 min delays).
  - It also has 24/7 one-way control at **Westport** through 12/31.
  - Several daytime utility-work zones around Little River, Mendocino and Fort Bragg end 11/13.
  - Hwy 128: "No traffic restrictions" in Northern California.
  - Re-check [QuickMap](https://quickmap.dot.ca.gov/) the day before. Hwy 128 near the Navarro River can flood in big storms *(analysis)*.

What this means *(analysis)*:
- **Tide pools** at Laguna Point, Glass Beach and below the Mendocino Headlands:
  - Thursday from about 3:45 PM to sunset is the one good window.
  - Friday from about 4:15 PM is marginal.
  - Saturday is no good.

- **Storm and wave watching.**
  - The king-tide highs fall around 11 AM–1 PM all three days. They are best at Russian Gulch's Devil's Punchbowl ("At high tide, it's a roiling cauldron of water" — [State Parks](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=432)), the Mendocino Headlands blowholes and arches, Point Cabrillo, and the Glass Beach bluffs (watch from the trail).
  - State Parks: "Large rogue waves can sweep you out to sea during ALL seasons… Bluff- and ocean-related deaths are common" ([Russian Gulch](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=432)). Stay on bluff trails at high tide, and never turn your back on the surf.
  - Check the NWS Eureka forecast for Beach Hazards / High Surf statements: [weather.gov/eka](https://www.weather.gov/eka/).

### A. Mendocino village and the southern parks

**1. Mendocino Headlands State Park: MUST**
- **Type:** Bluff-top trails around the village on three sides; sea arches, grottos, blowholes.
- **Time:** 45–90 min.
- **Cost and parking:** "Admission … is Free." Park along Heeser Dr and Main St. Village street parking gets tight Fri/Sat midday *(analysis)*.
- **Hours:** Day use, all three days.
- **Best time:** Sunset (4:54 PM). Thursday 3:45 PM to sunset also has falling tide pools (about +1 → −0.7 ft). Wave watching at the 11 AM–1 PM king-tide highs.
- **Crowds:** Moderate; spread out.
- **Coords:** [39.3041, -123.80251] OSM.
- **Notes:**
  - State Parks: "Miles of trails wind along the cliffs… spectacular views of sea arches and hidden grottos."
  - Stay back from edges.
  - Source: [State Parks](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=442).

**2. Mendocino village (Main St, water towers, Victorian streets): MUST**
- **Type:** Historic village walk, galleries, bookshops.
- **Time:** 1–2 h.

- **Crowds:** Busy Fri/Sat.
- **Coords:** [39.30778, -123.79944] WP.
- **Notes:** Pair it with #1 and #3. Dining is covered in `dining.md`.

### Variant for tide-chasers (Bowling Ball Beach on Thursday)

Drive up Hwy 1 along the coast on Thursday:
1. Berkeley → Jenner → Point Arena. The OSRM fastest route to Point Arena is about 3 h 17 m, and that route isn't necessarily the coastal one. The Hwy 1 coast route is slower in practice.

**Not recommended** for first-timers on Thanksgiving: it costs the Anderson Valley stops and means night driving on Hwy 1.
