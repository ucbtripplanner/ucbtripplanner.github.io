Own 2WD car from Berkeley (route A in `logistics.md`: I-80 → Carquinez → SR-37 → SR-29 through Vallejo / American Canyon → Napa → Yountville → St. Helena → Calistoga).

**How to read this**
- **TOP PICK / GOOD / BACKUP** = recommendation for *this* group: first-timers, budget, Korean students who already live near Bay Area chains.
- **VERIFIED** = read on the venue's own site, or on the venue's live **Resy** calendar, during this research. "Verified" regular hours are the *currently posted* hours. They are not a promise for Thanksgiving week.
- **Resy live** = the venue's own Resy booking calendar, checked **11/11 09:20–10:03 PST** for a party of 3 (and 2 or 4 where noted). On that calendar, `closed` means the restaurant has marked the day closed. `sold-out` means no tables are left. Availability changes by the minute.
- **UNVERIFIED** = not confirmed for 2026, or the only source is older or third-party. The source and its date are given.
- **Research limits (be aware):** general web search (Brave, Bing, DuckDuckGo) was rate-limited or CAPTCHA-blocked for most of this session, and OpenTable, Tock, Toast and Four Seasons pages block automated reading. So holiday status here comes from venue sites and Resy. Anything that books on OpenTable or Tock shows availability as **UNVERIFIED**.
- Coordinates are `[lat, lng]` with a source tag. **OSM** = an OpenStreetMap object (Photon/Overpass). **Nominatim** = an OSM address geocode. **Resy** = the venue's Resy listing. Address-level points can be off by ~10–30 m.
- Prices are copied from the menu or source shown, **before tax and tip**, unless noted. Where no menu price could be confirmed, only a tier is given, marked *(tier = estimate)*: `$` < ~$15/person · `$$` ~$15–35 · `$$$` ~$35–70 · `$$$$` > $70.
- **Sales tax** (CDTFA rate lookup, 11/11/2026, https://maps.cdtfa.ca.gov/):

  | Area | Rate |
  |---|---|
  | **Napa city** | **8.75%** |
  | **St. Helena** | **8.25%** |

  | **Vallejo** · **Glen Ellen** | **9.25%** |
  | **Sonoma city** | **10.25%** |

  At sit-down places, tip ~18–20% of the pre-tax bill. Menu price × ~1.28–1.30 is a realistic all-in.
- **Alcohol:** 21+. California accepts a **US or foreign passport** as proof of age but **not a foreign driver's license** ([CA ABC](https://www.abc.ca.gov/education/licensee-education/checking-identification/), via `sights.md`). ## 5. Rough food budget (per person, incl. tax & ~20% tip at sit-downs)

Arithmetic from the menu prices above (tax 7.75% Calistoga/Yountville, 8.25% St. Helena, 8.75% Napa).

| Meal | Budget pick (≈ all-in) | Comfortable pick (≈ all-in) |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | Bakery/diner (Model, Butter Cream): **$8–15** *(tier = estimate)* | Café Sarafornia / Sam's ($22 two-egg breakfast → ~$28) |
| Lunch | Buster's tri-tip sandwich $16.35 → **~$18** (counter, small tip) · Gott's **$18–25** *(estimate)* | Calistoga Inn pizza $25 → ~$32 · Hog Island $30–45 *(estimate)* |
| Dinner | Buster's tri-tip dinner $20.15 → **~$22** · Daikon AYCE $35.99 → **~$47** | **Ad Hoc $69 → ~$88** · Bouchon poulet rôti $45 + frites $12 → ~$74 |
| **Thanksgiving dinner** | **Evangeline $89 → ~$114** · **Calistoga Inn $95 → ~$121** (+3% if paid by card ≈ $124) | Sam's $110 → ~$141 · Lovina $125 incl. service → **~$135** · Harvest Table $125 → ~$160 · La Toque $148++ → ~$190+ |
| Snacks / coffee / water | **$8–12/day** | $15–20/day |

**Per-day totals**
- **Casual day (Fri or Sat): ~$60–90 pp.** Or ~$110–130 if that day includes the Ad Hoc dinner.
- **Thanksgiving day: ~$130–150 pp** (light breakfast + V. Sattui deli lunch ~$20–25 *(estimate)* + Calistoga Inn $121).
- **Whole trip (Thu–Sat, 3 days): ≈ $250–330 pp** with one Thanksgiving dinner and one nice meal. **≈ $180–230 pp** if you self-cater Thanksgiving from Safeway and skip the nice meal.
- **Ways to save:** Lovina's price already includes service, so it's no worse than Sam's after tip. Calistoga Inn has no card fee for debit/cash. At Ad Hoc and Bouchon, ordering no alcohol saves the most.
