# Napa Valley (Napa → Calistoga) Thanksgiving 2026: routes, logistics, safety and prep

Research dates: **Tue 2026-11-10 to early Wed 2026-11-11** (Caltrans and Spare the Air checks ran on 11/11). Trip: **Thu 11/26 to Sat 11/28/2026** (2 nights). 3 adults (maybe 4), Korean students at UC Berkeley (some may be international students), driving their own regular 2WD car from Berkeley.

Legend:
- **[Official]**: a page from the agency or venue itself (Caltrans, MTC/BATA, CA DMV, CA ABC, CHP, NOAA/NWS/CPC, USNO, CAL FIRE, Vine/NVTA, the town or city, the hospital, the business).
- **[Secondary]**: news or third-party page. It is cited, but no agency page confirmed it.
- **UNVERIFIED**: I could not confirm it for 2026. The newest info I found is given with its date.

## TL;DR

- **Route (fast, the default):** Berkeley → **I-80 E** → **Carquinez Bridge** → **SR-37 W** (1.5 mi) → **SR-29 N** through Vallejo and American Canyon → Napa. **38.5 mi / ~55 min to downtown Napa** (OSRM). Calistoga is **65 mi / ~85 min** (OSRM). Visit Napa Valley's own estimate from SF is "about 1 to 2 hours (depending on traffic and day of the week)" [Official].
- **Alternative:** I-80 E → **SR-12 W (Jameson Canyon)** → SR-29/SR-221. 45 mi / ~64 min (OSRM). Use it when SR-29 through Vallejo and American Canyon is jammed. **Scenic:** Richmond–San Rafael Bridge → US-101 → SR-37 → **SR-121 (Carneros)** → Sonoma or Napa. 47 mi / ~69 min to Sonoma Plaza (OSRM).
- **Inside the valley:** **SR-29** is "the main road" on the west side. The **Silverado Trail** on the east side is "less traveled" [Official, Visit Napa Valley]. Napa → Calistoga: SR-29 is **28 mi / ~39 min**; Silverado Trail is **31 mi / ~50 min** (OSRM). A good plan is to drive up one and back down the other.
- **Toll: one $8.50 toll for the whole trip.** The Carquinez Bridge toll is collected "**One way, eastbound in Vallejo**", so you pay going north and the drive home is free [Official, MTC]. Booths are cashless (no cash since 2020). Without FasTrak, **an invoice is mailed to the car's registered owner** within 21 days; pay it within 21 days of receipt, or a $5 penalty is added [Official, Bay Area FasTrak]. In 2026 the toll is **$8.50 whether you pay by FasTrak, license-plate account or invoice**. The surcharge for invoice and plate-account payment only **starts in 2027** [Official, MTC 12/1/2025]. **FasTrak never asks for payment by text** [Official, MTC], so any toll text is a scam.
- **Traffic:** in INRIX's 2025 pattern, **Thanksgiving Day had "Minimal Traffic Impact"**. **Saturday was worst 1–8 pm and best before 10 am** [Official, AAA 11/2025]. The 2026 AAA/INRIX forecast isn't out as of 11/11. Inside the valley, expect Black-Friday and weekend crawl on SR-29 (St. Helena's Main St is SR-29).
- **CHP Thanksgiving enforcement:** 2025 ran **Wed 11/26 6:01 pm → Sun 11/30 11:59 pm**. During the 2024 Thanksgiving period CHP made **1,100+ DUI arrests**, and **36 people died** in crashes statewide [Official, CHP]. The 2026 window is **UNVERIFIED**; it will likely be Wed 11/25 6:01 pm → Sun 11/29.
- **Drinking and driving is the #1 risk of this trip.** California's limit is **0.08 % BAC at 21+**, **0.01 % under 21**, and **0.04 %** for anyone driving passengers for hire [Official, DMV]. A typical tasting flight is about **2 standard drinks** (my arithmetic, §7.2). Two wineries ≈ 4 drinks, which puts many people near or over the limit. **Pick a 0.00 driver each day**, or don't drive (§7.4). Napa County prosecutors charged three 2025 DUI crashes that killed **nine people** as **murder** [Secondary, Napa Valley Register 3/5/2026].
- **Open bottles go in the trunk.** Passengers may not drink in the car [Official, DMV].
- **ID for tastings:** **21+ only**. An international student needs a **passport**. A **foreign driver's license or ID card is not acceptable ID** in California, and neither is a school ID or a temporary paper DMV license [Official, CA ABC]. A business may set a stricter policy. Some wineries admit **no one under 21 at all**: Domaine Carneros says "All visitors must be 21 or older" [Official].
- **Car-free options:**
  - **Vine Route 10** runs Napa ↔ Yountville ↔ St. Helena ↔ Calistoga hourly for **$2**. Friday 11/27 runs a **Saturday schedule** [Official, Vine].
  - Free **Yountville Bee** and **$1 Calistoga / St. Helena on-demand shuttles**.
  - Small-group tours such as Platypus (**$139/person**, tasting fees discounted but not included).
  - A hired **"your car, our driver"** service (quote needed).
  - Uber/Lyft exist but are scarce in the evening up-valley [Official, Downtown Napa Association].
  - **Thanksgiving Day: no Vine buses and no community shuttles** [Official, Vine].
- **Closed on Thanksgiving Day (examples, all [Official]):** Castello di Amorosa, Domaine Carneros (Wed 11/25 last reservation is 3 pm), Bale Grist Mill SHP (open weekends only, so Sat 11/28 is its only open day), and Vine transit. **Many wineries close Thursday.** Use Thursday as the drive/scenery day.
- **Parking:**
  - **St. Helena**: free, **3-hour limit**, enforced Mon–Sat 8 am–6 pm, **not enforced on Thanksgiving Day** [Official].
  - **Yountville**: free public lots; **no overnight parking** in them [Official].
  - **Downtown Napa**: 3 city garages plus lots and street parking. Fees and limits are **UNVERIFIED**; check the city map.
  - Wineries generally have their own lots.
- **Weather (NOAA 1991–2020 normals, 11/26–28):**
  - Napa: high **~62 °F**, low **~42 °F**.
  - Calistoga: **~61 / 40 °F**.
  - Chance of measurable rain on any given day: **~30–35 %**.
  - NOAA CPC's week 3–4 outlook (issued 11/6, valid 11/21–12/4) **favors above-normal precipitation for much of California** (El Niño) and above-normal temperatures on the West Coast.
  - **Sunset ~4:50 pm, sunrise ~7:02–7:06 am** (USNO).
  - **King tides are Nov 24–26** [Official, CA Coastal Commission]. They only matter on low bay-shore roads during a storm.
- **Fire/air:**
  - Every 2026 CAL FIRE incident in Napa County was small (≤ 98 acres) and is 100 % contained. **No active CAL FIRE incidents statewide on 11/11** [Official, CAL FIRE API].
  - PG&E fire-weather power shutoffs (PSPS) can still happen in late fall (there was one on **Nov 20, 2019**) [Secondary, Wikipedia]. Sign up for PG&E **Address Alerts** for the lodging address [Official, PG&E].
  - On a **Spare the Air** alert day, burning wood (fireplaces, fire pits) is illegal [Official].
- **Nearest ERs (24/7):**
  - Calistoga has none. The closest is **Adventist Health St. Helena** (10 Woodland Rd, ~9 mi / 13 min from Calistoga).
  - **Providence Queen of the Valley** (1000 Trancas St, Napa) is a **Level III trauma center**.
  - **Sonoma Valley Hospital** (347 Andrieux St, Sonoma).
- **Gas (AAA, 11/10/26), regular:** Napa **$5.70**, Vallejo–Fairfield **$5.58**, Oakland $5.70, CA average $5.71. The whole trip is ~200–250 mi, so **~$40–60 per car**. Fill up in Berkeley or Vallejo/American Canyon.

### 1.2 Inside the valley: SR-29 vs Silverado Trail

[Official, Visit Napa Valley "Where is Napa Valley"]: "There are two roads that run north to south in the valley floor – Highway 29 is the main road from the city of Napa to Calistoga on the west side of the valley. On the east side is the less traveled Silverado Trail. Several roads run west to east between the two main roads". The valley floor "is just 35 miles long and 5 miles across at its widest point."

| Leg (OSRM) | Distance / time |
|---|---|
| Napa → Yountville (SR-29) | 9.3 mi / 14 min |
| Yountville → St. Helena (SR-29) | 9.5 mi / 12 min |
| St. Helena → Calistoga (SR-29) | 8.5 mi / 10 min |
| **Napa → Calistoga via SR-29** | **28.0 mi / 39 min** |
| **Napa → Calistoga via Silverado Trail** | **30.7 mi / 50 min** |

- **SR-29**: the towns (Yountville, Oakville, Rutherford, St. Helena) and most big-name wineries are on it, plus Vine Route 10. It slows down most in **downtown St. Helena (one lane each way on Main St)** and at the **Oakville Cross Rd and Rutherford Rd (SR-128) intersections**. MTC/NVTA have a $14 M "Napa Valley Forward" project to "enhance safety and improve traffic flow" at exactly those two intersections, with construction "TBD (Funding Dependent)" [Official, MTC, updated 10/1/2025]. **Tip (my synthesis):** where a left turn across SR-29 traffic has no signal, turn right and loop back via a cross road.
- **Silverado Trail**: fewer lights and calmer, with vineyard views (Stags Leap area). It is a county road with bicyclists. A cyclist died after a collision on **Dunaweal Ln near Silverado Trail (Calistoga) in April 2026** [Secondary, Napa Valley Register 4/13/2026]. Give cyclists **3 ft** when passing.
- **Cross roads** (south → north): Trancas St / Oak Knoll Ave, Yountville Cross Rd, Oakville Cross Rd, Rutherford Rd (SR-128), Zinfandel Ln, Pope St, Larkmead Ln, Dunaweal Ln, Lincoln Ave (Calistoga).

### 3.1 What the data says (2025 pattern; the 2026 forecast isn't published yet)

- The Bay Area's worst 2025 corridor was **SF → Santa Rosa on US-101 N, Tuesday 7:15 pm** (2 h 34 m, +114 %). That's wine-country-bound traffic, but on Tuesday and not on this route.
- AAA tips: "Filling the tank is a good idea the night before a road trip". Check battery and tire pressure. For impaired driving: "have a plan in place" (rideshare, sober driver, transit).
- **CHP Holiday Enforcement Period** [Official, CHP 11/24/2025]: 2025 ran Wed 11/26 6:01 pm → Sun 11/30 11:59 pm. During the 2024 Thanksgiving HEP, "36 people died in crashes across California" and CHP made "more than 1,100 arrests for driving under the influence during the same 102-hour holiday enforcement effort." The 2026 dates are **UNVERIFIED**.
- AAA's new forecast usually comes out the week before Thanksgiving. Re-check https://newsroom.aaa.com/ around 11/17.

### 3.2 Suggested windows (my synthesis)
- **Thu 11/26 going up:** leave Berkeley **~9–10 am**. Roads are light, and you arrive with ~6 h of daylight (sunset 4:50 pm). Many wineries are closed Thursday, so this is the drive/scenery/Oxbow/Silverado day.
- **Fri 11/27 (Black Friday):** start tastings or sightseeing early. Expect slow SR-29 in St. Helena and Napa in the afternoon. The Vine runs a Saturday schedule.
- **Sat 11/28 going home:** best before 10 am, worst 1–8 pm. Practical option: end the day at the **south end** (Napa/Carneros) and **leave by ~1–2 pm**. You're home (66 mi from Calistoga, ~40 mi from Napa) before the 4:51 pm Berkeley sunset. If you leave after dark, the driver must be fully sober (§7).
  - **Pitt at Cal is in Berkeley on Sat 11/28**, kickoff **TBD** (ESPN API, 11/11). With an afternoon or evening kickoff, expect jams near campus, Piedmont Ave and Tunnel Rd at the very end of the drive.
- No 49ers, Raiders or Warriors home games turned up for 11/26–28 in the ESPN API (11/11). Low confidence; re-check.

## 7. Drinking, driving and IDs (read this one)

### 7.1 The law [Official, CA DMV Driver Handbook §9 "Alcohol and Drugs"]
- "It is illegal for you to drive if you have a BAC of":
  - "0.08% or higher if you are over 21 years old."
  - "0.01% or higher if you are under 21 years old."
  - "0.04% or higher if you are driving a passenger for hire."
- "Even if your BAC is below legal limits, that does not mean it is safe for you to drive… you may be arrested and convicted of a DUI even without a BAC measurement."
- **In the car:** "It is illegal to drink any alcohol… while you are driving or riding as a passenger in a vehicle. If you are carrying any alcohol… the container must be sealed and unopened. If it is open, you must keep the container in the trunk or place where passengers do not sit. It is also illegal to keep an open container of alcohol in your glove box." The same applies to a re-corked restaurant bottle: **trunk**.
- **Under 21:** "You may not carry any alcohol beverage inside a vehicle unless an individual who is 21 years old or older is with you. The container must be full, sealed, and unopened." Penalties: impound up to 30 days and a 1-year license suspension.
- **Refusing a test** → "DMV will suspend or revoke your driving privilege for one year."
- **A DUI conviction** brings suspension, a DUI program, SR-22 insurance, a possible ignition interlock, "up to six months in jail", fines and impound, and it "remain[s] on your driver's record for 10 years."
- **Bicycles too:** riding a bike under the influence is also a DUI offense in California (CVC 21200.5). Under-21 riders can lose or delay their driving privilege (DMV handbook). So "we'll bike between wineries" is not a loophole.
- For international students, a DUI arrest can create **immigration and visa complications** on top of the criminal case. Talk to Berkeley International Office before anything else if it ever happens. (General caution; not researched in detail.)

### 7.2 How fast tasting adds up (my arithmetic; check against the DMV BAC chart)
- **One US standard drink = 14 g of alcohol = "a 5-ounce glass of wine at 12% alcohol"** [Official, NIAAA].
- Napa reds often run **14–15 % ABV**. A 2-oz pour at 14.5 % ≈ 0.58 standard drinks. **A 4–5-pour flight ≈ 2–2.5 drinks.** Pour sizes vary by winery (UNVERIFIED as a fixed number).
- **Two wineries ≈ 4–5 drinks in ~3–4 hours.** For many adults, especially lighter ones, that's near or above 0.08. This is my estimate and is **UNVERIFIED**: the DMV handbook's BAC-by-weight table is an image I couldn't read, so check it yourself. Either way it's far above the 0.01 limit for anyone under 21.
- "**BAC can continue to rise even when a person stops drinking**" and "cold showers, hot coffee, and walking do not reverse the effects" [Official, NIAAA]. Only time helps.
- Visit Napa Valley itself suggests **2–3 winery visits per day** (each ~90 min) and to "Spit into a 'dump bucket' if you don't want to get inebriated" [Official].

### 7.3 Why this matters in Napa specifically
- "Nine people died in three Napa County crashes last year in cases prosecutors have charged as murder" under California's Watson doctrine (DUI fatalities charged as second-degree murder) [Secondary, Napa Valley Register column, 3/5/2026].
- A 2023 op-ed by a victim's father points to "DUI's, particularly on the rural, winding, and scenic Highway 29… Getting from one winery to the next requires too many to drive after they drink" [Secondary, opinion, Napa Valley Register 7/18/2023].
- CHP targets impaired driving statewide over Thanksgiving (§3.1). If you see a suspected impaired driver, "call 9-1-1" with make, model, plate and direction [Official, CHP].

### 7.5 Wine-tasting ID and age rules [Official, CA ABC "Checking Identification"]
- **Acceptable ("bona fide") ID:** a "California Real ID/driver license/identification card", an "Out-of-state driver license/identification card", U.S. military ID, and "**Both U.S. and foreign passports** with photograph, but without a physical description are acceptable".
- **Not acceptable:** "**Foreign driver license/identification card**", "School or work identification card", "Interim or temporary driver license or receipt for license from DMV", birth certificate, Social Security card, expired or borrowed IDs. "A person may not combine two unacceptable identifications to make one acceptable identification."
- "A business may have a company policy that is stricter than the law."
- **So:** each person brings a **passport** (international students) or a **CA/US license or ID**. The Korean driver's license and the Cal student ID won't work. Keep a photo of the passport on your phone, and lock the original in the hotel safe when you're not tasting.
- **Under 21?** They can't taste anywhere. Some wineries admit **nobody under 21** (Domaine Carneros: "All visitors must be 21 or older"). Others allow minors with a non-alcoholic option. Check each reservation.
- **Reservations:** "some of the more popular wineries and tasting rooms fill up their reservations weeks in advance". The average basic tasting is "about $40… as low as $20 and go as high as $200 or more" [Official, Visit Napa Valley]. Tipping guide from the same page: $10–15 per couple at a bar, $15–20 seated, 20 % for large groups.

## 8. Other safety

### 8.1 Night and rain driving
- **Sunset ~4:50 pm** (§9.2). Dinners and most drives back are in the dark. SR-29 and the Silverado Trail are mostly unlit two-lane rural roads with cyclists, deer and turning traffic (general advice, **UNVERIFIED** as a measured hazard).
- In rain: slow down and use **low beams**. Expect standing water on the Silverado Trail and SR-121 shoulders. The first rain after a dry spell makes roads slick (general).
- **Valley fog** on clear, cold mornings is common in the valleys in late fall (general, **UNVERIFIED** for these dates). If you're out early (e.g., a balloon ride), drive with low beams.
- **Phones:** California is hands-free. The phone must be mounted and you may only tap or swipe once (CVC 23123.5). Let a passenger navigate.

### 8.2 Car break-ins
- Napa is calmer than SF or Oakland, but vehicle burglaries do happen there. Examples: an American Canyon police **hotel-parking-lot stakeout** for "catalytic converter thefts and car burglaries" (1/2021), and a **string of vehicle burglaries in St. Helena** (3/2022) [Secondary, Napa Valley Register]. Frequency at winery lots is **UNVERIFIED**.
- Rules (same as the LA trip): **nothing visible in the car**. Put bags in the trunk *before* you arrive, not in the parking lot. Unload luggage at the hotel. Passports, I-20/DS-2019 and laptops stay with you or in the safe.
- **Wine purchases:** sealed bottles in the trunk are both legal and less visible. In late November the trunk won't overheat.

### 8.3 Coast hazards
- **Not applicable** to Napa Valley: no ocean, no sneaker waves. If you add a Bodega Bay or Sonoma Coast leg, use the coast research file's NWS sneaker-wave guidance.

### 8.4 Earthquakes
- Napa had the **M 6.0 South Napa earthquake on Aug 24, 2014** [Official, USGS event nc72282711]. If you feel shaking indoors: drop, cover, hold on. In the car: pull over away from overpasses and power lines, stop and set the parking brake. The **MyShake** app gives early warnings in California.
