### 2.4 Seasonal factors for late November 2026

- **Post-harvest, fall color:**
  - "November is a more relaxed time after the harvest parties end, but this is when the colors of autumn are on full display. Experience fewer crowds" ([Visit Napa Valley](https://www.visitnapavalley.com/seasons/fall/)).
  - *(analysis)* By the last week of November, vine leaves are often past peak and start to drop after the first frosts. Expect yellow-brown rather than vivid red. The mustard bloom is Dec–Feb ([Visit Napa Valley winter](https://www.visitnapavalley.com/seasons/winter/)), so it is not in the window.
- **Wildlife:**
  - I found no notable wildlife spectacle (migrations, salmon runs) tied to these sites for this week; that topic was not researched further.
  - Old Faithful has a small farm with goats ([OFG](https://www.oldfaithfulgeyser.com/plan-your-visit/)).
- **Holiday crowds** *(analysis)*:
  - Thursday is quiet on the roads but limited in what is open.
  - Fri and Sat are a holiday weekend. Mondavi says weekend tastings "typically sell out in advance" ([FAQ](https://robertmondaviwinery.com/pages/faq)), and V. Sattui recommends reservations on weekends "for access to retail and picnicking" ([V. Sattui visit](https://www.vsattui.com/visit/)).
  - **Book every winery 1+ week ahead.**
- **Events, holiday lights, trains:** see §6.
- **Road note** *(analysis, from Caltrans)*:
  - Caltrans is paving **SR-29 (Sonoma Blvd) in Vallejo**, with daytime two-lane closures Mon–Thu 9 AM–6 PM and Fri 9 AM–7 PM ([Caltrans 2026-11-05](https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-4/d4-news/2026-11-05-sr29-lane-closure)).
  - Whether work pauses for the holiday is **UNVERIFIED**.
  - From Berkeley, I-80 → **SR-12 (Jameson Canyon)** → SR-29 avoids that stretch. Routing details belong in `routes-stops.md`.

## 4. Candidates in detail (42)

### 4.1 Calistoga (north end)

#### 1. Calistoga mud bath / hot-spring soak: **MUST** (pick one spa; comparison in 4.2)
- **Type:** Calistoga's signature ritual: volcanic-ash mud bath, mineral bath, steam, blanket wrap. **Time:** 1–2 h plus pool time.
- *(analysis)* For 3 friends:
  - **Golden Haven** has the best value: a private room shared with your booking partner, $128 pp Mon–Thu. The odd third person books a single at $169, or two rooms.
  - **Calistoga Spa** and **Indian Springs** are gender-separated or traditional bathhouses.
  - **Dr. Wilkinson's** is the retro-famous one, with a day pass for pool lounging.
  - Book as soon as the dates are fixed. Holiday weekend slots are limited.

#### 2. Old Faithful Geyser of California: **MUST** (the best non-drinking sight; open Thanksgiving)
- **Area / type:** 1299 Tubbs Lane, just north of downtown Calistoga. A privately run geyser that "erupts about every 20–35 min", plus a geology museum, an animal farm (goats), lawn games and picnic areas.
- **Time:** "about one to two hours".
- **Cost:**
  - Adults **$18**; children 3–12 $13; under 3 free.
  - **Students & teachers $16**; seniors 62+ and military $16.
  - Small online booking fee; tickets are valid 12 months.
  - Picnics *and your own wine* are allowed; they supply glasses.
- **Parking:** free, on-site.
- **Hours:** "Open 365 days a year … 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM … Holiday hours may vary — when in doubt, call ahead" ([plan your visit](https://www.oldfaithfulgeyser.com/plan-your-visit/)). So it is open Thu, Fri and Sat.
- **Best time:** any time; a cold morning makes a bigger steam plume *(analysis)*.
- **Crowds:** moderate *(analysis)*.
- **Coords:** [38.59694, -122.60213] (OSM).
- 4 min from downtown Calistoga (OSRM).

#### 3. Castello di Amorosa: **MUST** (Fri or Sat; closed Thanksgiving)
- **Area / type:** Hwy 29, about 3 mi south of Calistoga. A 136,000 sq ft Tuscan-style castle winery with a drawbridge, chapel, great hall, caves and a "torture chamber".
- **Time:** 75 min (self-guided tasting) to ~1 h 50 min (guided tour).
- **Cost** (price snapshot from the [Wayback 2026-05-16 copy of the tour page](https://web.archive.org/web/20260516054617/https://castellodiamorosa.com/tour-tasting2/); the live page now loads prices in a booking widget, so **re-check**):
  - **Tasting Experience** (self-guided, two main levels; 5 pours chosen from Reserve, Current-Release, or the **"Libero" non-alcoholic flight**): **$60 adult**, **$25 ages 2–20**.
  - **Diamond Estate Tour & Tasting** (guided, adults-only): **$75**.
  - Plus a flat **$4.95 online booking fee** per booking. Full refund up to 24 h before ([live tour page](https://castellodiamorosa.com/tour-tasting2)).
- **Rules:**
  - "Reservations are required for everyone in your party"; no entry past the gate without one.
  - "Guests under 21 are not allowed on our guided tours with other parties" ([FAQ](https://castellodiamorosa.com/faq)).
  - Caves are 58°F, so bring a jacket.
- **Hours:**
  - Daily 10:00 AM–5:30 PM, by reservation.
  - **"Thanksgiving Day — CLOSED"** ([tour page holiday box](https://castellodiamorosa.com/tour-tasting2)).
- **Best time:** first slot at 10 AM, before tour buses *(analysis)*.
- **Crowds:** high on Fri/Sat *(analysis)*.
- **Coords:** [38.55839, -122.54260] (OSM).

#### 4. Sterling Vineyards (aerial gondola): **RECOMMENDED** (choose this *or* Castello if the budget is tight)
- **Area / type:** Dunaweal Ln, between Calistoga and St. Helena. A hilltop winery reached by gondola; "the round-trip ride is approximately 5 minutes" ([visit FAQ](https://www.sterlingvineyards.com/pages/visit-faq)).
- **Cost / experiences** ([tastings](https://www.sterlingvineyards.com/pages/tastings)):

| Experience | Price | Length | Ages |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sterling Stroll** (self-guided, 5 wines) | **$55** | 60 min | under 21 welcome: **$25 for ages 3–20** |
| Tour & Terrace | $85 | 90 min | all ages |
| Hilltop | $95 | 90 min | 21+ |

  - Non-drinkers can buy a **$25 ticket**.
  - "Gondola-only rides are not currently offered."
- **Rules:**
  - The gondola stops at winds ≥34 mph and closes above 100°F; shuttles run instead.
  - No food is sold and no picnics are allowed. No pets.
- **Hours:** "Open Every Day, 10am – Last Sterling Stroll Tasting at 4:30pm"; reservations recommended, walk-ins welcome. Thanksgiving status **UNVERIFIED** (no holiday note on the site).
- **Best time:** the late-afternoon view south over the valley *(analysis)*.
- **Crowds:** moderate to high.
- **Coords:** winery [38.56960, -122.54490] (WP); gondola line midpoint [38.56823, -122.54786] (OSM).
- **Caution:** in June 2026 the SF Chronicle ran "Two famed California wineries face uncertain future as wine giant downsizes", about Sterling's owner, Treasury Wine Estates (headline seen via Google News; I could not open the article to see which wineries). ### 4.3 Up-valley state parks (non-drinking, cheap)

#### 10. Bothe-Napa Valley State Park: **RECOMMENDED** (pair with #11)
- **Type:** redwood and Douglas-fir canyon on Ritchey Creek, between St. Helena and Calistoga. "over 10 miles of scenic hiking trails" ([infosheet PDF](https://www.parks.ca.gov/pages/477/files/BotheNapaValleySP_Infosheet_8.5x14_073025_FINAL.pdf)):

#### 11. Bale Grist Mill State Historic Park: **RECOMMENDED** (Saturday only)
- **Type:** an 1846 water-powered grist mill with a 36-ft waterwheel, "one of the largest in North America". Milling and grinding demonstrations ([brochure PDF](https://www.parks.ca.gov/pages/482/files/BaleGristMillFinalWebLayout060415.pdf)).
- **Time:** 45–60 min.
- **Cost:** vehicle day use **$10**.
- **Hours:** "Open: Weekends and on some Monday holidays. 10am - 4pm. Last Tour Begins at 3pm" ([parks.ca.gov](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=482)).

  - **Sat 11/28: open.**
  - The "Old Mill Holidays" event is Dec 5 (after the trip).

#### 12. Robert Louis Stevenson State Park (Mt. St. Helena / Table Rock): **OPTIONAL** (hikers only)
- **Type:** 5,272 undeveloped acres; the summit of Mt. St. Helena is "the highest peak in the California wine country", with Pacific, Sierra and Mt. Shasta views on clear days ([infosheet PDF](https://www.parks.ca.gov/pages/472/files/RobertLouisStevensonSP_Infosheet_8.5x11_112624_FINAL.pdf)).
- **Trails** ([hike list PDF](https://napaoutdoors.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/RLS-Park-Hike-List-rev9.3.pdf)):
  - Stevenson Memorial Trail: 1.3 mi one-way, to the honeymoon-cabin site
  - Mt. St. Helena Trail (fire road): ~4.5 mi
  - **Table Rock Trail: ~2.2 mi**, to volcanic cliffs
  - Palisades Trail: very rugged, not for this group
- **Cost:** "There are no fees". Dirt parking lot on Hwy 29 (4824 Lake County Hwy). **No water, no restrooms, no dogs**; lock the car.
- **Hours:** sunrise–sunset.
- **Time:** Table Rock out-and-back is ~2–3 h; the summit is a half day (5–6 h) *(analysis)*.
- **Coords:** park [38.63827, -122.59731] (OSM, centroid); summit [38.66935, -122.63332] (OSM); Table Rock [38.64296, -122.57971] (OSM). Calistoga → park entrance ~13 min (OSRM).
- *(analysis)* The summit can't be done in winter daylight together with the classics. Only do it if the group really wants a hike. In late November the trailhead can be near freezing, and after rain the fire road is muddy.

### 4.7 Sonoma side trip (Sonoma Plaza / Glen Ellen)

#### 37. Sonoma Plaza + Sonoma State Historic Park (Mission San Francisco Solano): **RECOMMENDED** (Fri/Sat; Mission closed Thanksgiving)
- **Type:** Sonoma's historic town plaza, ringed with shops and tasting rooms. The park covers several historic sites around it: Mission San Francisco Solano, General Vallejo's home, the Sonoma Barracks. It's the "birthplace of the California Bear Flag".
- **Cost:** museum entry adult **$3**, youth $2, 5 and under free.
- **Hours:** "Open 7 Days a Week, 10:00 am - 5:00 pm. **Closed Thanksgiving Day**" ([parks.ca.gov](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=479)).
- **Holiday lights:** the "Lighting of the Sonoma Plaza 2026" is **Nov 21** ([sonomavalley.com events](https://www.sonomavalley.com/)), so lights are up but the ceremony is before the trip.
- **Coords:**
  - Plaza [38.29281, -122.45770] (OSM)
  - Mission [38.29389, -122.45610] (OSM)
  - Sonoma Valley Visitors Center [38.29274, -122.45700] (OSM)
- Berkeley → Sonoma Plaza ~62 min (OSRM).
- *(analysis)* The Plaza itself (outdoors) is fine to walk on Thanksgiving; just the museums are closed.

#### 38. Jack London State Historic Park (Glen Ellen): **RECOMMENDED** (one of the few sights open on Thanksgiving)
- **Type:** Jack London's "Beauty Ranch": his cottage, the House of Happy Walls museum, the Wolf House ruins, the grave, and "over 29 miles of trails". The park suggests allowing 3 h.
- **Cost:** **$15 per vehicle** ([hours & fees](https://jacklondonpark.com/directions-hours-fees/)).
- **Hours:**
  - Park 9 AM–5 PM; museum 10 AM–5 PM; cottage noon–4 PM.
  - **"Thanksgiving Day: Park and Museum are open"**; cottage not mentioned, so **UNVERIFIED**.
  - State Parks: "Open Daily (Except Dec 25)" ([parks.ca.gov](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=478)).
- **Event:** **Turkey Waddle Hike, Fri 11/27, 10:00 AM–12:30 PM**: a guided ~3.5-mi moderate hike to an old-growth "Ancient Redwood". $15, which includes park entry; register online ([event](https://jacklondonpark.com/events/turkey-waddle-2026/)).
- **Coords:**
  - Park [38.34508, -122.55317] (OSM)
  - House of Happy Walls [38.35666, -122.54094] (OSM)
  - Wolf House ruins [38.35104, -122.53727] (OSM)
- Jack London → Sonoma Plaza ~24 min; Calistoga → Jack London ~48 min (OSRM).

## 7. Two sample day plans

Drive times are OSRM free-flow; add 20–50% for holiday traffic on Hwy 29 *(analysis)*. Every winery and spa needs a reservation; book a week or more ahead. Meals belong in `dining.md`; slots are only placeholders here.

### Plan A: "Classic up-valley" (lodging in or near Calistoga both nights)

**Thu 11/26 (Thanksgiving: use what's open)**
- 08:30 Leave Berkeley → 09:30 **Napa Valley sign** photo (62 min).
- 09:45–11:45 **V. Sattui**: deli shopping, picnic lunch on the lawn, optional Mercato Tasting $45 for the 21+ members (open Thanksgiving; reserve).
- 12:15–13:15 **Bothe-Napa Valley SP**: Redwood Trail walk ($10 per car). The Bale mill is closed today.
- 13:45–15:15 **Old Faithful Geyser** (open 365; students $16). Call ahead for holiday hours.
- 15:30 Check in. 16:00–17:30 **mud bath** at Golden Haven (holiday price $138 pp likely) or Dr. Wilkinson's pools.
- 18:00+ Thanksgiving dinner (see `dining.md`). Calistoga sunset is 4:51 PM.

**Fri 11/27**
- (Optional) 06:15–10:00 **Hot-air balloon** from Yountville. Then reverse the day: Yountville and St. Helena in the morning, Calistoga in the afternoon.
- 10:00–11:30 **Castello di Amorosa** Tasting Experience ($60 / $25 under 21), first slot.
- 11:45–12:45 **Petrified Forest** ($14; 10 min west) *or* the **Sterling** gondola ($55 / $25).
- 13:15 Lunch in St. Helena → 14:00 **St. Helena Main St** → 14:45 **Beringer** Discover Outdoor ($35, reserve) *or* the **CIA Greystone** store, with the 4:30 PM tour.
- 16:00–17:15 **Yountville**: Bouchon Bakery (open to 6 PM Fri), Washington St.
- Evening option: downtown Napa (lit tree at Veterans Park, Riverfront, Oxbow until 9 PM) and/or **Merry Meritage skating**. It's about 35 min back to Calistoga, so skip this if everyone is tired.

**Sat 11/28 (return day)**
- 10:00–11:00 **Bale Grist Mill** (open weekends only; $10 per car; demonstrations), plus the History Trail toward Bothe.
- 11:15 Drive down the **Silverado Trail** → 12:30 lunch at **Oxbow Public Market** (Napa).
- 14:00–15:30 **Domaine Carneros** (21+ only, from $45) *or* **di Rosa** (Sat 11–4) for under-21 groups.
- ~15:45 Head home via Hwy 121 / 37 or 12 → I-80. About 52 min free-flow; Saturday holiday return traffic will be heavier.

### Plan B: "Napa base + Sonoma, non-drinking friendly" (lodging in Napa)

**Thu 11/26**
- 09:00 Leave Berkeley → 10:00 **Sonoma Plaza** (outdoor stroll; Mission museums closed today).
- 10:45–13:30 **Jack London SHP** (open Thanksgiving; $15 per car): museum, Beauty Ranch, Wolf House ruins walk.
- 13:45 **Oakville Grade** over the Mayacamas, in daylight (~41 min to Yountville).
- 14:45 Yountville stroll (Clementine is open 11–4 if you need a snack).
- 16:30 Napa: **Merry Meritage skating** (2–10 PM) → Thanksgiving dinner.
- *Splurge swap:* replace the Sonoma morning with the **Wine Train Thanksgiving Lunch** (10:30–3:00, from $449.69) and move Sonoma to Saturday.

**Fri 11/27 (Calistoga day; ~35 min up-valley)**
- 09:15 **Old Faithful** → 10:30 **Petrified Forest** → 12:00 **Castello** (Libero non-alcoholic flight for under-21s) → 14:30–16:30 **mud bath** in Calistoga → back down the Silverado Trail at sunset (~4:50 PM) → evening at **Oxbow** / downtown Napa.
- *Alternative morning:* the Jack London **Turkey Waddle** hike (10:00–12:30), if Thursday went to the Wine Train.

**Sat 11/28**
- 10:00 **Bale Grist Mill** + Bothe (Saturday only) → 11:30 **St. Helena Main St** + **CIA Greystone** ($15 tour at 11:30 if available) → 12:45 **V. Sattui** picnic (weekend BBQ truck 11:30–3) → **Napa Valley sign** → home.
