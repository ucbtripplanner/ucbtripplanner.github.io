## 0. Verdict

**Winner: candidate A's skeleton, with B's best timing ideas grafted in.**

- **From A:**
  - Sleep in **Calistoga** (UpValley Inn) both nights.
  - **Walk** to dinner both nights.
  - Saturday runs down-valley with stops on the way home: **Bale Grist Mill → Silverado Trail → Yountville → Oxbow**.
- **From B:**
  - **The mud bath moves to Thanksgiving Thursday afternoon.** That's the day the wineries are closed, so the spa isn't competing with anything.
  - **Castello gets the first 10:00 slot on Friday**, before the tour buses.
  - Gott's right at its 11:00 opening, Safeway Napa as the Thursday grocery fallback, luggage loaded in the trunk at home, and the cheap Napa base kept as the budget variant (V1).

**Why this shape:**
1. **Every stop is on a day it's actually open.**
   - Thanksgiving closes Castello, Beringer, Domaine Carneros, Montelena, Schramsberg, Frank Family and the Sonoma Mission, and Oxbow was closed on Thanksgiving 2025.
   - So **Thursday** uses what is confirmed open: V. Sattui ("Open Daily (except for Christmas Day)"), Old Faithful ("Open 365 days"), a Calistoga spa, and the Calistoga Inn turkey menu (12–9 pm).
   - **Friday** has Castello (closed Thursday).
   - **Saturday** has Bale Grist Mill, which is open only on weekends and closed on Thanksgiving, so Saturday is its only open day this trip (parks.ca.gov, re-checked 11/11 10:24).
2. **There is no driving after dark at all.**
   - Thursday: the car is parked by 15:50, and the Thanksgiving dinner is a 15-minute walk.
   - Friday: back at the hotel by 15:30, dinner on foot again.
   - Saturday: leave Oxbow 13:45, home ≈ 14:45–15:05. Sunset is 16:51.
   - This removes the trip's #1 risk (drinking and driving on a CHP holiday-enforcement weekend, on unlit two-lane SR-29) by design rather than by willpower.
   - Candidate B needed a 35-minute dusk drive after Thanksgiving dinner and an after-dark drive on Friday.
3. **The holiday is spent on the spa.** Thursday has little else open. Golden Haven charges the same $138 "Friday, Saturday, Sunday and holiday periods" rate either way, so Friday's prime 10:00 slot goes to Castello.
   - *Risk:* Golden Haven's Thanksgiving operation is **UNVERIFIED**. Booking is by phone, so you find out today, not on the day. If Thursday isn't possible, use variant **V2** (Bothe on Thursday, mud bath Friday 09:15).
4. **Short drives.**
   - Thu ≈ 70 mi / 92 min in total.
   - Fri ≈ 21 mi / 34 min, all within 10 minutes of the hotel.
   - Sat ≈ 69 mi / 100 min, broken by four stops.
5. **Cost is the trade-off, and it's stated openly.**
   - The Calistoga room costs ≈ $283 pp, against ≈ $145 pp at Inn at the Vines in Napa.
   - The extra ≈ $138 pp buys walkable dinners, two evenings in a geothermal pool, ~70 fewer minutes of driving a day, and no night driving.
   - For a group that ranks budget first, **V1 (Napa base)** is fully worked out in §8.3.
   - UpValley also requires a **21+ booker**. If nobody is 21+, use V1.
