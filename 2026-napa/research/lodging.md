How the prices below work:
- **Prices are the 2-night total for 1 room, before tax**, as Booking.com shows them. They already include mandatory resort / service / destination fees (Booking lists them under "Included"); taxes are under "Excluded".
- **"All-in" = room × (1 + tax %) + paid parking × 2 + per-night flat charges** [EST]. Rounded to the dollar.
- Rates are the **free-cancellation, pay-later** option unless noted. Non-refundable "pay online" rates were usually 5–10 % cheaper.
- **Booking's wording "Free cancellation before Nov X"** means free until the property's local midnight starting Nov X. In other words, **the last free day is Nov X−1**. The detailed text says e.g. "until 7 days before arrival". Always check the exact time in your confirmation email.

### Fees and price display rules
- **California SB 478** (in force since July 1, 2024): the advertised lodging price must include **all mandatory fees**. Only government taxes and fees may be left out. ([CA DOJ "Hidden Fees"](https://oag.ca.gov/hiddenfees))
- **FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees** (effective May 12, 2025) applies to short-term lodging. ([FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/05/ftc-rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-take-effect-may-12-2025))
- So resort, amenity and service fees are already in every price below. Taxes are extra. Parking is extra only where stated.
- **Booking "Partner Offer" rates** appeared at Calistoga Motor Lodge ($904), UpValley ($715), Napa Valley Hotel & Suites ($340) and Napa Winery Inn ($630):
  - "facilitated by a Booking.com partner company", "Pay in advance", "**No modifications**", no invoice [OBS].
  - Cheaper, but you can't change names or dates. Cancellation fee steps apply, e.g. CML: "From 00:00 on November 23, the cancellation fee will be $465".
- **Deposits and holds** [BKG / SITE]:
  - DoubleTree American Canyon: $100 damage deposit.
  - Mount View: $100 pre-authorization.
  - Everywhere: the booker shows **photo ID + the credit card** at check-in. A passport is fine as photo ID.
  - Debit cards: banks may hold extra funds (Mount View policy).

- Sleep in **Calistoga** only if the group values the hot-springs-pool evening. If so, at least one night at UpValley Inn or Calistoga Motor Lodge is the way to do it.

## 7. Airbnb & short-term rentals

### The rules (why Airbnb is mostly hotels here)

| Jurisdiction | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Unincorporated Napa County** (most of the valley floor between towns, incl. many "Napa" or "Calistoga" addresses outside city limits) | **Prohibited.** "County ordinance NCC § 18.104.410 prohibits transient occupancies also known as short-term rentals or vacation rentals, in every residential and agricultural area in the unincorporated county. This is true no matter where you list the property, including Airbnb, VRBO, or similar sites." | [Napa County PBES FAQ](https://www.napacounty.gov/m/faq?cat=154) [SITE] |
| **City of Napa** | Capped at "**41 Non-Hosted** Accommodations" and "**60 Hosted** Accommodations". "The Planning Division is not currently accepting any applications…" A list of active permits is published. | [cityofnapa.org Vacation Rental Permits](https://www.cityofnapa.org/325/Vacation-Rental-Permits); [Active non-hosted permits, Fall 2026 (PDF)](https://www.cityofnapa.org/DocumentCenter/View/15918/Active-Permits---Non-Hosted-Fall-2026) [SITE] |
| **St. Helena** | "Ordinance 2012-2 permitting **25** short-term rentals"; waiting list | [cityofsthelena.gov STR](https://www.cityofsthelena.gov/238/Short-Term-Vacation-Rentals) [SITE] |
| **Yountville** | "short term rentals taking place over a span at/under 29 days are not allowed" | [Airbnb Help: Yountville](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2567) (platform's own summary) |
| **Calistoga** | New Municipal Code **Chapter 17.34** "prohibiting Short-Term Rentals" (under 30 days) in residential zones; council-adopted, described as codifying a long-standing ban. Hotels and licensed B&Bs are separate uses. | Secondary: [code mirror (Zoneomics) § 17.34.010](https://www.zoneomics.com/code/calistoga-CA/chapter_21); [Citizen Portal council summary](https://citizenportal.ai/articles/6737508/california/napa-county/calistoga/california/napa-county/calistoga/California/Napa-County/Calistoga/Calistoga-tightens-shortterm-rental-rules-council-adopts-consolidated-zoning-chapter). The official code host (codepublishing) blocked the fetcher, so the primary text is **UNVERIFIED**. |

**Practical rule:** in Napa County, book a whole home only if it is inside the City of Napa **and** its owner appears on the city's active-permit list, or if it is a licensed hotel, inn or B&B. Anything else risks a last-minute host cancellation on a holiday weekend.
