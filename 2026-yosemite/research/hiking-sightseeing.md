# Yosemite: "하이킹은 적당히, 구경 위주" re-plan (Thu 11/26 – Sat 11/28/2026)

**Researched:** 2026-11-11 · **Why:** the group asked for moderate hiking and more sightseeing (views, landmarks).
The old plan had 3 real climbs (Tuolumne Grove, Mist Trail to the top of Vernal Fall, Columbia Rock) plus Mirror Lake.
This note keeps the sourced parts of the 11/11 research (conditions, trails, sights, light) behind the new course in `../data.js`.

**How to read this:** ✅ = read on an official page on 11/11 · **UNVERIFIED** = not confirmable on 11/11 (date given) ·
*(analysis)* = my own calculation or inference. "Fall Guide" = Yosemite Guide 51-8 (Sep 23 – Nov 24, 2026), the current issue.
"Winter Guide 2025" = Guide 51-1 (Nov 26, 2025 – Jan 27, 2026), last year's Thanksgiving issue, used as the closest analog.
**The Guide that covers our dates (from 11/25) was not posted on 11/11** (expected ~11/20–24).

**Definition used for "적당히":** at most **one real hike per day** (about 1–3 h, ≤ ~1,000 ft gain, nothing technical or icy-exposed),
about 5–6 mi of walking per day at most, and the rest of the time on drive-up viewpoints and short walks, with the best viewpoints timed for golden hour.

## 5. Getting around (Fall Guide 51-8, to 11/24)

- **Valley shuttle:**
  - Free, 7 am – 10 pm.
  - Valleywide (green) "every 22–32 min", round trip 1.5 h. East Valley (purple) "every 18–22 min", round trip 50 min.
  - The NPS web page still shows summer intervals (12–22 / 8–12 min); don't use those.
  - **Purple stops only at #1, #2, #12 and #14–19; #3–#11 are "Green Shuttle ONLY"**; #9 is closed.
  - Shuttles run one way in stop-number order, so the course rides east in the morning (purple #2 → #16) and comes back west (green #16 → #3).
  - Curry Village has two stops: **#14 "Curry Village—eastbound"** and **#19 "Curry Village—westbound"**. So Curry → Village is purple **#19 → #1 → #2**, and Village → Curry is **#2 → #12 → #14** (used in the 🏨 Curry variant; "#14 → #2" would ride the whole east loop).
- **One-way roads:** Southside Dr is **eastbound** and Northside Dr is **westbound**.
  - So the south-side sights (Bridalveil, Cathedral Beach, Chapel, Sentinel Bridge) come on the way in, and El Cap Meadow and Valley View on the way out.
  - Driving from Valley Lodge east to Curry or the Ahwahnee loops via El Cap Bridge, ~16–21 min ([OSRM](https://router.project-osrm.org/), *(analysis)*).
  - OSRM 11/11 *(analysis)*: Curry → Tunnel View 9.2 mi / ~23 min (Northside → Pohono Bridge → Southside → Hwy 41; the site says ~25–30 min for holiday traffic); Tunnel View → Curry 6.9 mi / ~16 min (Southside straight); Cathedral Beach → Curry 3.7 mi / ~10 min.
- **Saturday drive home:** AAA/INRIX 2025 Saturday after Thanksgiving: Worst "1:00 PM – 8:00 PM", Best "Before 10:00 AM" ([AAA 2025](https://newsroom.aaa.com/2025/11/aaa-thanksgiving-travel-forecast-2025/), re-read 11/11) ✅. Leaving the Valley at 10:05 and Mariposa at 12:15 puts the I-205/I-580 legs at ~14–16:00 *(analysis)*, inside the worst window; only the park and Hwy 140 part runs before it. The site says so instead of claiming the 10:00 start avoids it.
