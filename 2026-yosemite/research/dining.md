Research date: **2026-11-07**. Trip: 2 nights within **Thu Nov 26 – Sun Nov 29, 2026**, 3 adults (students, budget-conscious, open to one nice meal), driving from Berkeley.

**How to read this**
- **TOP PICK / GOOD / BACKUP** = recommendation for this group (budget, first visit, late November).
- **VERIFIED** = read on the official site on 2026-11-07. Even "verified" hours are the venue's *current posted* hours, not a guarantee for Thanksgiving week. Holiday-specific hours are almost never posted, so call ahead on Thanksgiving Day.
- **UNVERIFIED** = couldn't confirm for 2026, or the source is older or third-party. The source year is given.
- Prices are copied from the venue's menus as posted. They exclude tax and tip unless noted.

## 1. In-park dining (Yosemite Valley + rest of park)

All venues are run by the park concessioner (travelyosemite.com, which calls itself "Yosemite National Park Lodging & Activities"; NPS calls it "Yosemite Hospitality"). Hours below were posted on the official pages on 2026-11-07. Several pages already say "Closed for the Season" for summer-only venues, which suggests the hours are being kept up to date for the current season.

The NPS says dining reservations "are available 366 days in advance for The Ahwahnee Dining Room, the Mountain Room… and are recommended, especially… during holidays."
Source: https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/dining.htm (last updated Oct 22, 2026)

### 1.1 The Ahwahnee Dining Room: **TOP PICK for the one nice meal** (VERIFIED)
- **What:** Grand 1927 dining hall in The Ahwahnee hotel, open to the public. This is the classic Yosemite splurge.
- **Hours (posted):** Breakfast 7:00–10:00 AM · Lunch 11:30 AM–2:00 PM · Dinner 5:30–9:00 PM · Sunday Brunch 7:00 AM–3:00 PM ("starting 4/5/26"). Afternoon tea 4–5 PM is for hotel guests only.
- **Price:**
  - **Dinner is prix fixe: 5 courses $95, 7 courses $125, wine pairing +$40.** Menu includes Dungeness crab, duck confit, roast elk tenderloin and capirotada. 18% gratuity is added for parties of 6+. (Menu PDF linked from the official page. The menu rotates, so dishes may differ.)
  - **Breakfast buffet $32** (pastries, eggs, bacon, sausage, potatoes, coffee, juice and tea included). À la carte breakfast: eggs Benedict $21.50, omelets $19.50–20.50, French toast $18.50.
  - **Lunch** (2026 menu): French onion soup $13, salads $14.50–24, burgers $22.50–24, entrées $27–32, prime rib $38/$48.
  - **Sunday Brunch: $69 adults** (carving stations with prime rib and pork loin, omelet station, raw bar, desserts). Relevant if you are there **Sun Nov 29**.
- **Reservations:** "Highly encourage" OpenTable. A hotel reservation does not guarantee a table.
- **Dress code (dinner only):** Long pants and a collared shirt, or a dress, skirt, or long pants with a blouse. No hiking clothes, shorts, T-shirts, flip-flops or caps. Breakfast and brunch are casual. **Pack one nicer outfit if you plan on dinner or the Thanksgiving feast.**
- **Budget tip:** The breakfast buffet ($32) or lunch gets you the room and the views for a fraction of the dinner price.
- Sources: https://www.travelyosemite.com/dining/the-ahwahnee-dining-room · dinner menu https://assets.milestoneinternet.com/aramark-parent/yosemite-national-park/pdf/ahwahnee-menu-1.pdf · breakfast https://assets.milestoneinternet.com/aramark-parent/yosemite-national-park/pdf/ahwahnee-2025-breakfast-a-la-carte.pdf · lunch https://assets.milestoneinternet.com/aramark-parent/yosemite-national-park/pdf/2026-ahw-lunch.pdf · brunch https://assets.milestoneinternet.com/aramark-parent/yosemite-national-park/pdf/sunday-brunch-ahwahnee-11-x-17-in.pdf

### 5.4 Simple self-catering plan (if your lodging has a kitchen)
This assumes a kitchen-equipped rental (gateway town or Yosemite West) for 2 nights, 3 people. Curry Village tent cabins have **no** kitchens, and food must go in the bear lockers.

**Shop once, on the way in, at the last big supermarket (§5.3). If you drive up Thursday, shop in the Bay Area on Wednesday.** Thanksgiving-Day store hours are reduced or unknown (§2.5).

**Shopping list (3 people, 2 nights):**
- **Breakfasts (×2):** instant oatmeal or granola, milk, bananas, a dozen eggs, bread or bagels, coffee or tea
- **Trail lunches (×2–3):** sandwich bread or tortillas, deli meat or hummus, cheese, apples, trail mix, granola bars, water
- **Dinner 1:** pasta, jarred sauce, Italian sausage or chickpeas, bagged salad
- **Dinner 2: DIY "Friendsgiving"** — rotisserie chicken or pre-cooked turkey breast, boxed stuffing, instant mashed potatoes and gravy, canned cranberry sauce, a bag of green beans, and one store-bought pumpkin pie
- **Extras:** hot chocolate packets, a thermos, foil, zip bags, a small trash bag

**Daily rhythm:**
- **Morning:** breakfast at the rental, and fill a thermos. Alternatively, grab $3–5 coffee at Curry Village Coffee Corner (opens 6:30 AM) or Degnan's.
- **Midday:** packed lunch at a viewpoint (Tunnel View, Cook's Meadow, El Capitan Meadow). Note that Village Grill and Meadow Grill are closed for the season.
- **Evening:** cook. Keep **one** restaurant meal as the splurge:
  - Ahwahnee Thanksgiving ($165)
  - Ahwahnee breakfast buffet ($32) or Sunday brunch ($69)
  - Mountain Room (mains $28–72)
  - Pizza Deck large pizza ($21.75–41) shared three ways

**No-kitchen alternative (in-park, approximate per person per day from posted menus, excluding tax):**
- Degnan's breakfast sandwich ($7.50–9) + coffee ($3)
- Degnan's deli sandwich for lunch ($8–13)
- Dinner: Base Camp Eatery ($8.50–18) or ⅓ of a large Pizza Deck pizza ($7–14)
- That comes to roughly **$25–45 per person per day** before tax, tips and drinks. My arithmetic from the posted menus is in §1.
