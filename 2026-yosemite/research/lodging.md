## 3. Drive-time reference

All figures are from NPS "Driving Distances and Times" ([nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/driving.htm](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/driving.htm)).

**Entrances to Yosemite Valley:**

| From | Time | Distance |
|---|---|---|
| Arch Rock entrance (Hwy 140) | about 25 min | 11 mi |
| Big Oak Flat entrance (Hwy 120 W) | about 45 min | 25 mi |
| South entrance (Hwy 41) | a little more than an hour | 35 mi |

**Towns to Yosemite Valley:**

| From | Time | Distance |
|---|---|---|
| El Portal | about 30 min | 14 mi |
| Mariposa | 1h15m | 44 mi |
| Groveland | 1h15m | 49 mi |
| Fish Camp | 1h15m | 39 mi |
| Oakhurst | 1h30m | 55 mi |
| SF/Bay Area | 4–5 hours | 195 mi |

**Roads and chains:**
- Hwy 41, Hwy 140, Hwy 120 West, and Hetch Hetchy Road are **open all year**. Tioga Road and Glacier Point Road close in November. ([NPS winter roads](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/wroads.htm))
- **Tire chains:** "you must carry and know how to use them, regardless of the type of vehicle you are driving." ([NPS winter](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/winter.htm))
- Yosemite Hospitality adds that rangers can deny entry without chains, with fines up to $5,000. ([winter travel conditions](https://www.travelyosemite.com/winter/winter-travel-conditions))
- My general understanding, **[UNVERIFIED]**: Hwy 140 along the Merced River is the lowest-elevation approach, while Hwy 120 West and Wawona Road / Yosemite West climb to roughly 6,000 ft and are more snow-prone. NPS's page doesn't rank the routes.

## 4. Candidate details

### A. Inside Yosemite Valley (Yosemite Hospitality / Aramark)

**Booking:**
- Online at https://www.travelyosemite.com, or by phone at 888-413-8869.
- Reservations open 366 days in advance ([NPS lodging](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/lodging.htm)).
- Deposit is 100% of the first night.
- A refund requires cancelling ≥7 days before arrival ([policies](https://www.travelyosemite.com/plan/policies-information)).
- Rates: **[UNVERIFIED]**. None are shown on the property pages, and the live search was blocked.

#### 1) Yosemite Valley Lodge
- **Area / drive:** In the Valley, at the base of Yosemite Falls. 0 min drive. Enter at any entrance; from Berkeley, typically Big Oak Flat (Hwy 120) or Arch Rock (Hwy 140).
- **Rooms for 3 adults:**
  - Traditional rooms: one king, **two doubles**, or one queen.
  - **Bunk Room:** queen + bunk bed (double bottom / single top), "perfect for small groups." Fits 3, and fits 4 comfortably.
  - **Family Room:** king + bunk (double/single) + pull-out sofa. Only 4 of these exist. Fits 4–5.
  - Rollaways are complimentary but first-come, first-served.
  - Maximum occupancy and extra-person fees are not stated **[UNVERIFIED]**.
  - Source: [travelyosemite YVL](https://www.travelyosemite.com/lodging/yosemite-valley-lodge/)
- **Fridge:** mini fridge and coffee maker in rooms. No kitchen.
- **Open late Nov 2026:** yes, year-round ([NPS lodging](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/lodging.htm)).
- **Price:** not observed **[UNVERIFIED]**. The "Get a Third Night Free" (3RDFREE) promo covers travel only from **Nov 29, 2026**, so it doesn't help Thanksgiving. It also excludes Bunk and Family rooms. ([offer](https://www.travelyosemite.com/special-offers/specials-packages/linger-longer))
- **Pros:** best location for first-timers (walk to Lower Yosemite Fall, shuttle, Base Camp Eatery); no daily drive over snowy roads. Base Camp Eatery serves a Thanksgiving meal ([Thanksgiving page](https://www.travelyosemite.com/things-to-do/specialty-events/holiday-events/thanksgiving-dinner)).
- **Cons:** likely sold out. Motel-style rooms. Must be booked through the in-park system.
- **Book:** https://reservations.ahlsmsworld.com/Yosemite/Plan-Your-Trip/Accommodations/Yosemite-Valley-Lodge
- **Cancellation:** ≥7 days before arrival for a refund of the deposit.

#### 3) Curry Village
- **Area / drive:** Yosemite Valley, below Glacier Point. 0 min.
- **Open:** yes for Nov 26–28. **Closed Nov 29 – Dec 10, 2026.** [SITE]
- **Unit types:**
  - **Heated canvas tent cabins:** heat runs from the Tuesday after Labor Day to the Friday before Memorial Day. One outlet, no plumbing (shared bathhouse). Bed options "vary by room" **[UNVERIFIED]**.

### B. Private rentals inside the park gates

- **Price:** see §2 [OBS]. Airbnb 2-night totals from $650 all-in. Scenic Wonders listed nightly rates from $255–$355 for studios and lofts.

  - **Scenic Wonders requires a 3-night minimum during holidays**, which may rule out a 2-night Thanksgiving stay [UNVERIFIED whether Thanksgiving counts].

## 6. How in-park booking works, and why cancellations appear

**Who runs it.** All lodging inside the park (Ahwahnee, Yosemite Valley Lodge, Curry Village, and so on) is operated by Yosemite Hospitality (Aramark).
- Book at https://www.travelyosemite.com, which uses the engine at `reservations.ahlsmsworld.com`, or call **888-413-8869**.
- Reservations open **366 days in advance** and are "strongly recommended, especially… during holidays." ([NPS lodging](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/lodging.htm))

**Deposit and cancellation:**
- Deposit is 100% of the first night at booking.
- "Cancellations or changes… must be received 7 days prior to reservation arrival date to assure refund of deposit." ([YVL](https://www.travelyosemite.com/lodging/yosemite-valley-lodge/), [policies](https://www.travelyosemite.com/plan/policies-information))

**What this means for cancellations.** People holding speculative Thanksgiving bookings must cancel by about **Nov 19** (for a Nov 26 arrival) or **Nov 20** (for Nov 27) to get their deposit back. Rooms tend to reappear in the days just before those deadlines. Some also return after the deadline, from no-shows or people willing to lose one night. This timing pattern is the general mechanism; how often it happens at Thanksgiving is **[UNVERIFIED]**.

**Searching the engine.**
- The search form offers "All Yosemite Lodging" or a specific property (Curry Village, Housekeeping Camp, The Ahwahnee, Tuolumne Meadows Lodge, Yosemite Valley Lodge).
- Bookable dining products also appear in the same system, including **"Basecamp Thanksgiving Dinner"** [OBS, form options].

**Campgrounds** are booked separately, through recreation.gov. Late cancellations at Upper Pines show up there at random times.

## 7. Tips for finding Thanksgiving availability about 3 weeks out

1. **Lock a refundable backup today.** Good candidates:
   - Cedar Lodge ($388, 2Q, only 1 left) or Yosemite View Lodge ($680). Both cost $15 to cancel until 7 days out.
   - Rush Creek's **Best Flexible** rate (free cancel until 7 days out).
   - Avoid nonrefundable rates for now: Rush Creek/Evergreen Advance Purchase, AutoCamp "Adventure Secured", and Scenic Wonders' 14-day/100% policy.
2. **Check in-park lodging daily.** Use travelyosemite.com, or call 888-413-8869 when the website shows nothing. Peak days are **Nov 17–21**, just before the 7-day refund deadlines, and again 1–2 days before arrival.
3. **Search one night at a time and accept split stays.** For example, night 1 at Curry Village and night 2 at Yosemite Valley Lodge, or night 1 in the Valley and night 2 in El Portal. A 2-night search can hide single-night openings.
4. **Try both date patterns.**
   - Fri–Sun was cheaper at Rush Creek ($575 vs $694/night for a King), Evergreen, and Tenaya.
   - Fri–Sun had more Redwoods cabins (14 vs 7).
   - Thu–Sat avoids any question about Curry Village closing on Nov 29, and includes Thanksgiving dinner night (Ahwahnee $165/adult; Base Camp Eatery).
5. **If a 4th adult joins:**
   - The observed El Portal, Rush Creek, and Evergreen room rates didn't change for 4 adults.
   - Tenaya rose about $22/night.
   - In the Valley, aim for a YVL Bunk Room or Family Room, or a Curry Stoneman room with 3 double beds.
   - Rentals (Yosemite West, Redwoods, Airbnb) charge flat rates up to max occupancy.
6. **Camp 4 (only if the group has winter gear).** Sites release on recreation.gov one week ahead: for Nov 26, around Nov 19. The release time of day wasn't stated on the NPS page **[UNVERIFIED]**. Hodgdon Meadow is first-come, first-served in winter.
7. **Airbnb or VRBO checks:**
   - Read "Prices include all fees".
   - Check the cancellation tier.
   - Confirm the real location on the map (some listings labelled "Yosemite Valley" are in Yosemite West or Foresta [UNVERIFIED]).
   - Ask about snow access and the driveway.
   - Confirm that a 2-night holiday stay is allowed.
8. **Chains:** everyone must carry chains in winter conditions. Hotels on the low Hwy 140 corridor reduce the snow-driving exposure [UNVERIFIED that 140 is less chain-prone].
