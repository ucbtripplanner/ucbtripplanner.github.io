## Corrections from the cross-check (don't reintroduce)
- **Thursday starts at 07:30, not 08:00.** OSRM free-flow is 4h20m and the stops add 4h35m, so an 08:00 start arrives about 16:59 with no slack. With the site's own +10–25% curve rule, that becomes ~17:22–17:55, after dark on unlit Hwy 1.
  NPS says to allow 1h45m from the northern East Bay to the lighthouse lot. New times: Cypress 09:05, lighthouse 09:45 (stairs open at 10:00), Drakes 11:35, Goat Rock 14:15, Shell Beach 15:10. Leaving Shell Beach at 16:55 is fixed.
- **Drakes Beach lunch** is ~1 h after the 7.1 ft king-tide high (10:48). Use the picnic tables "near the visitor center and along the northern edge of the parking lot" (NPS), not the sand.
- **Minus tide wording:** the tide is +1.3 ft at 15:30, crosses 0 ft around 16:20, and reaches −0.6 ft at 16:50 (NOAA 9415625 hi/lo + cosine interpolation; low −1.51 ft at 18:26). Don't call 15:30–16:50 "the minus tide".
- **Friday stargazing is not at Bodega Head.** Sonoma Coast SP is open "dawn until 1 hour after sunset" (~17:53 on 11/27), and full darkness (end of astronomical twilight) is ~18:25, not 17:50.
  Stargaze from the hotel instead: Hotel Dega's 2nd-floor lounge "The Lookout" has a telescope and binoculars. Darkest window ~18:25 until moonrise at 20:18 (USNO).
