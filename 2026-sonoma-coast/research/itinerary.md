## 0. Verdict

**Winner: a graft of both candidates.**
- **Skeleton from candidate 1 ("근본 보데가 클래식"):**
  - Point Reyes Lighthouse on Thanksgiving Thursday.
  - Friday's one-way inland → river → coast loop.
  - Tomales Bay oysters on Saturday morning, then an early trip home.
- **Timing ideas from candidate 2 ("저스트레스"):**
  - Thursday afternoon goes to **Goat Rock seals + Shell Beach tide pools at the trip's only daylight minus tide**.
  - **Bodega Head moves to Friday sunset.** It's 6 minutes from Spud Point, and the drive back is a harbor road, not a cliff road.
  - A one-hour hotel rest is built into Friday.
  - Spud Point chowder becomes a late-afternoon stop on the way to Bodega Head, not a second breakfast.

**Why this shape:**
1. **Thanksgiving closes the shops, not the coast.**
   - On Thursday the only "paid-for" things are free NPS/State Parks sites and one dinner that is verified open: Bay View, 3–7 pm, $39 turkey (re-checked 11/11).
   - The lighthouse is open **10–2 on Thanksgiving** (NPS API 11/10). That is the only day it fits without pushing Saturday's drive home into the 1–8 pm peak.
2. **Tides decide the beach order.**
   - NOAA Bodega Harbor entrance (9415625) high/low, cosine-interpolated by me:

     | | 15:00 | 15:30 | 16:00 | 16:30 | 16:50 |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | Thu | 2.1 ft | 1.3 ft | 0.5 ft | −0.2 ft | **−0.6 ft** |
     | Fri | 3.6 ft | 2.8 ft | 2.0 ft | 1.2 ft | 0.7 ft |

   - **Thursday 15:30–16:50 is the only daylight minus tide of the trip**, so Shell Beach goes there.
   - Midday king-tide highs (Thu 6.8 ft at 11:00) are spent on cliff tops: the lighthouse and Drakes Bay.
3. **Long drive on the emptiest day, short drives after.**
   - Thu ≈ **136 mi / 4 h 20 m** free-flow. AAA 2025: Thanksgiving Day had "Minimal Traffic Impact".
   - Fri ≈ **61 mi / 2 h 00 m**, all in daylight.
   - Sat ≈ **70 mi / 2 h 03 m**. Leave Point Reyes Station at 13:20, home around 14:40–15:10.
4. **Every day's last cliff or river drive ends by ≈17:15.** Civil dusk is 17:22 (USNO). On Friday, the only after-dark drive is 9 minutes on harbor roads.
5. **One hotel for both nights** (Hotel Dega, Bodega Bay harbor).
   - No repacking.
   - Friday has an empty car.
   - No Russian River flood exposure.
   - Refundable until Nov 22.
