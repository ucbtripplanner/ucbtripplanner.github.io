How the prices work:
- "Room" = the **2-night total for 1 room, before tax**, as Booking.com shows it. That figure already includes mandatory destination, resort or service fees: Booking lists them under "Included" and taxes under "Excluded".
- **"All-in" = room × (1 + tax %)** [EST]. Parking is free at every shortlisted property, so it adds $0.
- The rate shown is the **free-cancellation** rate unless noted. Several properties also sell a slightly cheaper "pay online" rate. At this point those rates are refundable **only for 24 h after booking** (see §1).

## 1. TL;DR

### What matters most (as of 11/11)

- **The free-cancel clock is the #1 issue.** Today is 15 days before check-in.
  - Every property with a **14-day** policy loses its free-cancel window on **Nov 12**: boon, The Woods, R3, Boho Manor, The Highlands, Valley Ford, Hotel Dega's cheaper "pay online" rate, and Best Western Garden Inn's "pay online" rate.
  - Booking now therefore gets only Booking.com's **"Cancel without fees within 24 hours of booking"** grace [OBS].
  - **Book only the 72-hour (free cancel before Nov 23) or 7-day (before Nov 19) rates.**
  - On Airbnb, "Limited" (14-day) and "Firm" (30-day) listings are already past their refund date. Only "Flexible" (24 h) and "Moderate" (5 days) still refund ([Airbnb policies](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/475)).
- **Holiday availability is thin. Point Reyes is a day trip, not a base.** Sold out or showing no rate for 11/26–28 [OBS]:
  - **Inn at the Tides:** Thanksgiving night is sold out; only Friday 11/27 is left.
  - **No 11/26–28 availability on Google Hotels:** Olema House, Inn at Occidental, Occidental Lodge, Fairfield Inn Sebastopol and Bodega Harbor Inn. Google offers them only for other dates (Dec 3–5 or Nov 19–21).
  - **"Contact this property":** Point Reyes Station Inn, Chanslor Ranch and Creekside Inn.
  - **Booking.com "Point Reyes Station" search:** the only nearby hotel it returned was Tomales Bay Resort, which has only 2-person rooms. Everything else it listed was in Novato or Petaluma.
- **21+ check-in is common here.** Check how old the person booking is:
  - **21+ to check in [BKG]:** Hotel Dega, Sonoma Coast Villa, La Quinta Santa Rosa, Valley Ford Hotel.
  - **21+ adults-only:** boon hotel + spa [SITE].
  - **Adult-only:** The Highlands [SITE].
  - **18+:** Casa Secoya, Lodge at Bodega Bay, Jenner Inn, AutoCamp, Sandman.
  - The booker must show **photo ID + a credit card in their name** at check-in [BKG]. A passport is fine as photo ID.
  - Several properties take a **$100–250 damage deposit**. Quality Inn Petaluma takes **$250 in cash** [BKG].
- **Taxes add about 14 %.**
  - **Unincorporated Sonoma County** (Bodega Bay, Jenner, Guerneville, Monte Rio, Occidental): **12 % TOT** plus a **2 % Tourism BIA** at lodging with more than $350k/yr in rent, so **14 %**. Some hotels also pass on the 0.2 % California tourism assessment, which gives **14.2 %** ([Sonoma County TOT/BIA](https://sonomacounty.gov/acttc-tot/bia)).
  - The 2021 West County "Measure B" (12 → 16 %) **failed**. There was **no** Sonoma or Marin hotel-tax measure on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot (§2).
  - **West Marin** (Point Reyes, Inverness, Olema, Dillon Beach): **14 %**.
  - **Santa Rosa:** Booking shows 16–16.2 %.
- **Parking is free at every shortlisted property.** At Hotel Dega it's part of the $15/night amenity fee, which is already in the price.
- **Flood risk is real this year.**
  - Sonoma County **proclaimed an emergency on Oct 6, 2026** ahead of a possibly historic El Niño winter.
  - **Thanksgiving week 2024** brought an atmospheric river. The Russian River crested at **34.7 ft at Guerneville on Nov 23, 2024** (flood stage is 32 ft).
  - Riverside cabins and Hwy 116/River Road access can be hit. Bodega Bay hotels are not on the river (§7).
- **Base recommendation: Bodega Bay, with Monte Rio or Guerneville as a cheaper backup.**
  - The classic first-timer course is mostly coastal: Bodega Head, Sonoma Coast beaches, Goat Rock/Jenner.
  - From Bodega Bay, Jenner is 16 min, Occidental 22 min, Monte Rio 26 min and Guerneville 32 min.
  - Point Reyes Station is 52 min away, so do Point Reyes on the way up on Thursday.
  - (OSRM free-flow drive times; holiday traffic adds time.)

## 7. Flood-zone cabins: read before booking anything on the river

**Why it matters this year:**
- **Sonoma County proclaimed an emergency on Oct 6, 2026** "due to the developing El Niño season, which is predicted to cause unusually heavy rains this winter". Forecasters give a 75 % chance it reaches historic levels ([County of Sonoma](https://sonomacounty.gov/sonoma-county-proclaims-emergency-ramps-up-preparations-ahead-of-potential-winter-storms); [SoCo Emergency: Winter Storms](https://socoemergency.org/emergency/winter-storms/)).
- **Thanksgiving-week precedent:**
  - An atmospheric river on **Nov 20–23, 2024** dropped nearly 13 in of rain on Santa Rosa in three days.
  - The Russian River **crested at Guerneville at 34.7 ft early Sat Nov 23, 2024** (minor flood). It "caught some residents by surprise". Guerneville, Monte Rio and Forestville were affected and multiple county roads closed ([Press Democrat, 2024-12-02](https://www.pressdemocrat.com/2024/12/02/lower-russian-river-residents-advocates-criticize-sonoma-countys-response-to-recent-flooding/)).

**NWS flood categories, Russian River at Guerneville (GUEC1)** ([NWPS gauge](https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/guec1); data via [api.water.noaa.gov](https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/GUEC1), fetched 11/11):
- **Action 29 ft · Minor 32 ft · Moderate 35 ft · Major 40 ft.**
- Record: 49.5 ft (Feb 1986). Feb 2019: 45.38 ft.
- **Current (11/10 23:00 PST): 4.31 ft, "no flooding".** NWS forecast to 11/15: 4.58 ft.

NWS impact statements (stage → impact), filtered to places in this file:

|---|---|

**What this means for us:**
- **Bodega Bay hotels** (Hotel Dega, Lodge, Inn at the Tides) are not on the Russian River, so river flooding doesn't apply. Storm-surf and road issues on Hwy 1 still can.
- **River properties** are a risk only in a big storm. If a storm hits, cabins *and their access roads* can be cut off, sometimes with little warning, as in 2024. Exposure by property:
  - Casa Secoya (Northwood): the nearby road floods at 34–35 ft.
  - Cottages on River Road: River Road floods at 37 ft.
  - West Sonoma Inn (Brookside Ln): possibly 35 ft (UNVERIFIED).
  - Creekside Inn (Neeley Rd): 32.5–34 ft.
  - R3: 39 ft.
  - Forestville/Mirabel Airbnbs: 33–36 ft.
- **Rule for Airbnb on the river:** before booking, paste the address into the [FEMA Flood Map Service Center](https://msc.fema.gov/portal/home). Avoid riverfront or "beach access" homes on Mirabel, Vacation Beach, Northwood and Monte Rio flats. Prefer hillside listings, such as Rio Nido hills or Armstrong Woods Rd. Also ask the host whether they refund if the county orders an evacuation. Most standard Airbnb policies do **not** refund for weather; the host's answer is UNVERIFIED until asked.
- **Check before the free-cancel deadlines:** [CNRFC forecast for GUEC1](https://www.cnrfc.noaa.gov/graphicalRVF.php?id=GUEC1) on Nov 20–22, plus [SoCoEmergency](https://socoemergency.org/emergency/winter-storms/). If a crest of 29 ft or more (action stage) is forecast for Nov 26–28, cancel the river room before **Nov 22** and switch to Bodega Bay or Santa Rosa.
