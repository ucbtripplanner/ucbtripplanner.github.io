## 0. TL;DR

- **Thursday 11/26 is a King Tide day.** The California Coastal Commission lists King Tides for **Nov 24–26, 2026**.
  - High tide ≈ **6.8 ft at 11:00 AM** at Bodega Harbor entrance, **7.1 ft at 10:48 AM** at Point Reyes (NOAA).
  - With winter swell, beaches narrow at midday and sneaker-wave risk goes up. Stay off rocks and well above the wet sand.
  - **The afternoon is the opposite:** the tide falls to **about +0.3 ft by 4 PM and −1 ft by 5 PM** on Thu.
  - So **Thu 3:30–4:50 PM is the trip's best tide-pool window** (Shell Beach). It runs right up to **sunset at 4:53 PM**.
- **Daylight:** sunrise 7:05–7:07 AM, sunset 4:53 PM, civil dusk 5:22 PM (USNO, all three days). The usable day is short, so plan one "sunset spot" each day for 4:15–5:15 PM.
- **Wildlife in late November** *(analysis from sources)*:
  - **Harbor seals** at Goat Rock / Russian River mouth are present year-round. This is the most reliable marine-mammal sighting.
  - **Tule elk** at Point Reyes are present year-round (Pierce Point Rd / Tomales Point; along Sir Francis Drake Blvd near Drakes Beach).
  - **Gray whales:** the southbound run is mainly **Dec–Feb, busiest in January**, so late November is early. You *might* see one. Bodega Head whale docents only work **weekends Jan–May**.
  - **Elephant seals:** the bulls **arrive in December**, so the Chimney Rock overlook will likely be sparse. NPS says *some* elephant seals can be present any day.

- **Tide-pooling:** NPS recommends a **minus tide** and arriving about 1 h before low.

### 1.4 Weather and ocean

- **NPS (Point Reyes):** "The strongest winds occur in November and December during occasional southerly gales." Heaviest rain is in winter. In between storms it is "often sunny, calm, and cool."
- **bodegabay.com (11/2/2026):** storm-watching season "usually starts in November," with the biggest swells Dec–Feb. "Sneaker waves can catch people off guard, so stay off the rocks."
- **Recent example:** State Parks closed **Wright's Beach Campground Nov 13–17, 2026** for "forecasted high ocean swells." Swell events do happen this month.
- **Goat Rock** — State Parks: "Periodically the Goat Rock gate closes to vehicle traffic during day use hours when dangerous ocean conditions prevail." You may still walk in.
- **Check 1–2 days before** (live pages):
  - NWS Bay Area (MTR) **High Surf Advisory / Beach Hazards Statement**. MTR covers the Sonoma and Marin coast.
  - The NWS point forecast for Bodega Bay.
  - The **CNRFC Russian River at Guerneville** gauge (winter storms can flood River Rd and Guerneville).
  - Links are in §7.
- **Cell coverage:** State Parks notes coverage around Tomales Bay "is very limited." Expect the same gaps on Hwy 1 north of Jenner and on the Point Reyes headlands *(analysis)*. Download offline maps.

### A. Bodega Bay and Bodega Head

**1. Bodega Head (bluff trails, whale and storm watching)**
- Rocky granite headland forming the harbor entrance, on the west side of the San Andreas Fault. It moved about 15 ft in 1906. PG&E's nuclear plant here was famously stopped (WP).
- "The high cliffs offer excellent vantage points for observing migrating gray whales" (State Parks).
- Time: 45–90 min of bluff loops from the West lot *(analysis)*. Exact trail mileage is **UNVERIFIED**.
- Cost: free *(the lots are not listed as fee areas; "many locations are free of charge," Stewards)*.
- Parking: West and East lots. **Bodega Head East Day Use has "reduced services (pack it in/pack it out and no restroom facilities)"** (Stewards, undated).
- Best time: whale-scanning on clear, calm mornings. Storm-watching when a swell is running. Around the 11 AM–1 PM highs the surf is most dramatic, so stay on trail. Sunset over open ocean at 4:53.
- Hours: dawn to 1 h after sunset, all three days.
- Crowd: moderate; lots can fill midday on holiday weekends.
- Coords: `[38.31103, -123.06611]` OSM (Bodega Head point, by the West lot). WP `[38.3111, -123.0658]`.
- Whale docents: **weekends Jan–May only** (Stewards). None during the trip.
- **MUST.**

**2. Bodega Bay harbor town: Tides Wharf and the "The Birds" in-town sites**
- The Tides Wharf & Restaurant (rebuilt in the 1990s) is where restaurant, gas-station-fire and dock scenes were filmed.
- **Taylor Street**, about half a mile north of the Visitor Center: the "children running from the schoolhouse" shots.
- **Bay Hill Road**, about 1.5 mi north: Tippi Hedren's drive into town.
- The Brenner house (on Westshore Rd) has been demolished; the site is now part of the UC marine lab grounds (bodegabay.com "The Birds").
- **Sonoma Coast Visitor Center**, 913 Hwy 1, (707) 377-4459, hands out "maps for an Alfred Hitchcock tour." Hours **UNVERIFIED**.
- Time: 30–60 min plus a meal.
- Cost: free. Parking: free lots by the wharf *(analysis)*.
- Hours: shops and restaurants vary. Thanksgiving hours: see dining.md.
- Crowd: busy at lunch on Fri/Sat.
- Coords: Tides Wharf `[38.3287, -123.04509]` OSM; Taylor St `[38.33387, -123.04733]` OSM.
- **RECOMMENDED** (combine with lunch).

**3. Bodega village: St. Teresa of Avila Church and Potter School ("The Birds")**
- The **church** (1860) is "the oldest church in continuous use in Sonoma County." It appears in *The Birds*, and "Hitchcock attended services in the church" (WP). It was also photographed by Ansel Adams (bodegabay.com).
- The **Potter School** is behind the church (the film's schoolhouse). It is a **private residence**, "occasionally open for tours" (bodegabay.com). **View from the road only.**
- Time: 15–20 min.
- Cost: free.
- Parking: roadside on Bodega Hwy *(analysis)*.
- Best time: any. Soft afternoon light on the white church *(analysis)*.
- Hours: exterior anytime. The church is an active parish, so be respectful during services *(analysis)*.
- Crowd: low.
- Coords: church `[38.34624, -122.97237]` OSM (WP `[38.34625, -122.97239]`); Potter School `[38.34593, -122.97185]` OSM; Bodega Country Store `[38.34577, -122.97388]` OSM.
- **MUST** (quick and iconic; on the way from Valley Ford / Occidental).

### B. Sonoma Coast State Park: Hwy 1 from Bodega Bay to Jenner

The park "spans 17 miles from Bodega Head to Vista Trail" with "more than a dozen" beach access points off Hwy 1 (State Parks). The whole drive is the experience.
- Plan **2–3 h with 3–4 stops** *(analysis)*.
- Most lots are free.
- **Day-use restrooms are limited** ("REDUCED SERVICES … no restroom facilities" at several day-use areas, Stewards).
- **Dogs are banned** on Goat Rock and Salmon Creek beaches (seals, plovers).

**9. Salmon Creek Beach**
- "Two-mile sandy beach with lagoon." **No dogs, no fires** (snowy plover habitat).
- Time: 20–40 min. Parking: North and South lots; fee **UNVERIFIED** (likely free).
- Coords: South Salmon Creek Beach `[38.33911, -123.06817]` OSM.
- **OPTIONAL.**

**10. Arched Rock / Portuguese / Schoolhouse beaches (pull-outs)**
- "Beautiful sandy beaches surrounded by rocky headlands" (State Parks).

## 4. Safety (the public site should repeat this)

- **Sneaker waves and King Tides.**
  - State Parks: "Strong rip currents, heavy surf and sudden ground swells make surf play dangerous… **Never turn your back to the ocean**… Always stay well above the highest water marks in the sand."
  - At **Duncan's Landing** "people have been swept off the rocks."
  - Thursday 11/26 is a King Tide day with 6.8–7.1 ft highs around 11 AM. **Never climb on wet rocks or tide-pool shelves while the tide is rising**, and stay out of the water.
- **Bluffs:** Chimney Rock and other Point Reyes headlands have undercut, unstable edges (NPS). Stay on official trails and behind fences.
- **Wildlife distance:** harbor seals 50 yd (State Parks); elk "rule of thumb" (NPS). No dogs on Goat Rock or Salmon Creek.
- **Wind:** the lighthouse stairs close when winds exceed 40 mph. November and December bring the strongest southerly gales (NPS). Bring a windproof layer and a hat.
- **Short days:** sunset is 4:53 PM. Hwy 1 north of Jenner and the road to the lighthouse are curvy and unlit; avoid unfamiliar coast driving after dark when possible *(analysis)*.
- **No signal:** around Tomales Bay and much of Hwy 1 north of Jenner. Download offline maps and share the plan *(analysis + State Parks note)*.
- **Russian River:** winter storms can raise the river quickly. Check the CNRFC Guerneville gauge if heavy rain is forecast.
- **Marine Protected Areas:** Bodega Head SMR prohibits all take. Leave shells, rocks and tide-pool animals.
