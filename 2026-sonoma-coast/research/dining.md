# Bodega Bay · Sonoma Coast · Russian River, Thanksgiving 2026: Food, Thanksgiving Dinner, En-route Eats & Groceries

Research date: **2026-11-10/11**. Trip: **Thu Nov 26 (Thanksgiving) → Sat Nov 28, 2026**, 2 nights, 3 adults (+ maybe 1), UC Berkeley students, budget-conscious but OK with one nice meal. Driving their own 2WD car from Berkeley. Area covered: Bodega Bay, Bodega, Valley Ford, Jenner, Duncans Mills, Guerneville, Forestville, Occidental, Freestone, Sebastopol, plus optional Point Reyes / Tomales Bay (Marshall) on the way.

**How to read this**
- **TOP PICK / GOOD / BACKUP** = recommendation for *this* group (first-timers, budget, want the classic "근본" course).
- **VERIFIED** = read on the venue's own website (or its own menu PDF) during this research. Regular hours are the *currently posted* hours, not a promise for Thanksgiving week.
- **UNVERIFIED** = not confirmed for 2026, or the source is third-party / older. Source and date are given.
- Coordinates `[lat, lng]`: **OSM** = OpenStreetMap object (Overpass API, pulled 2026-11-10), **Nominatim** = OSM address/name geocode. Some points can be 10–30 m off the door.
- Prices are copied from the source shown, **before tax and tip**. Where no menu price was found, only a tier is given and marked *(tier = estimate)*: `$` < ~$15/person · `$$` ~$15–35 · `$$$` ~$35–70 · `$$$$` > $70.
- **Sales tax (CDTFA rates page, accessed 2026-11-10):** Sonoma County unincorporated (Bodega Bay, Bodega, Valley Ford, Jenner, Guerneville, Forestville, Occidental, Freestone) **9.25%** · Sebastopol **10.5%** · Santa Rosa **10.0%** · Petaluma **10.25%** · Marin County unincorporated (Point Reyes Station, Marshall, Tomales) **8.25%**. Add ~18–20% tip at sit-down places, so menu price × ~1.3 is a realistic all-in. Source: https://cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/rates.aspx
- Booking widgets (OpenTable, Resy, Toast) are JavaScript or bot-blocked, so **sold-out status could not be checked for any Thanksgiving dinner.** Treat every holiday table as "book now".
- **Re-checked 2026-11-11** on the venues' own pages, unchanged: Bay View Thanksgiving (Nov 26, 2026, 3–7 pm, turkey $39, desserts $10) · Farmhouse Inn (Thanksgiving dinner 3:00–7:30 pm, prix fixe $125) · Spud Point (daily 9–5, closed Thanksgiving) · Hog Island Boat Oyster Bar (Fri–Mon 11–4, closed Thanksgiving, Resy up to 90 days ahead, $10/person no-show fee). ## 2. Local classics, cafés, bakeries, casual spots (≈50 entries)

### 2.0 ⚠️ Crab reality this year

- **CDFW, Oct 30, 2026:** "The commercial Dungeness crab fishery will be delayed … from the California/Oregon border to Point Conception **until at least January 1, 2027**" (humpback-whale entanglements). Recreational crabbing opened Nov 7 with trap restrictions. Next risk assessment is mid-December. Source: https://wildlife.ca.gov/News/Archive/recreational-dungeness-crab-fishing-begins-in-most-areas-nov-7-with-trap-restrictions-commercial-season-delayed
- So over Thanksgiving weekend, **"fresh local whole cooked crab" will not be available.** Spud Point's menu lists whole cooked crab "11/15 thru 6/30" at market price, and that depends on the commercial opener. Crab sandwiches and cocktails may use stored or out-of-area crab. Ask at the counter. **Order the clam chowder, the guaranteed classic.**
