Research date: **2026-11-10/11**. Trip: **Thu Nov 26 (Thanksgiving) → Sat Nov 28, 2026**, 2 nights, 3 adults (+ maybe 1), UC Berkeley students (Korean; some international), budget-conscious but OK with one nice meal. Driving Berkeley → Monterey Peninsula (Monterey, Pacific Grove, Carmel-by-the-Sea, 17-Mile Drive, Point Lobos; optional Big Sur day trip; Moss Landing / Castroville / Santa Cruz on the way).

**How to read this**
- **TOP PICK / GOOD / BACKUP** = recommendation for *this* group (first-timers who want the classic "근본" Monterey food: wharf clam chowder, calamari, cioppino, Carmel bakeries, plus a Korean meal option).
- **VERIFIED** = read on the venue's own website (or its own ticketing page) during this research, 11/10–11/11. "Verified" regular hours are the *currently posted* hours; Thanksgiving-week exceptions are only verified where the venue says so.
- **UNVERIFIED** = third-party source (Restaurantji listing, See Monterey event calendar, OpenStreetMap tags, older posts) or no 2026 holiday info. Source + date given. **Call ahead for anything UNVERIFIED on Thu 11/26.**
- **Coordinates** `[lat, lng]`: **OSM** = OpenStreetMap object (Overpass API, 2026-11-11). **Nominatim** = OSM address geocode (house-number level; can be ~10–30 m off the door). **approx** = estimated from neighbouring mapped objects; re-geocode before pinning.
- **Prices** copied from the venue's menu/page, **before tax and tip**. Where no price was found, only a tier is given and marked *(tier = estimate)*: `$` < ~$15/person · `$$` ~$15–35 · `$$$` ~$35–70 · `$$$$` > $70.
- **Sales tax (CDTFA tax-rate API, queried 2026-11-11):** Monterey, Pacific Grove, Carmel-by-the-Sea, Seaside, Marina, Sand City **9.25%**; unincorporated Monterey County (Castroville, Big Sur, Moss Landing, the Crossroads/Carmel Valley area) **8.75%**; Capitola **9.25%**. No Monterey-County rate change in CDTFA's Oct 1 2026 notice (L-1037 lists none). With ~18–20% tip at sit-down places, **menu price × ~1.29** is a realistic all-in. Sources: https://services.maps.cdtfa.ca.gov/api/taxrate/GetRateByAddress , https://cdtfa.ca.gov/formspubs/L1037.pdf
- **Water is served on request only** on the Peninsula (printed on the Fish Hopper and Monterey's Fish House menus, "per Monterey Peninsula Water Management"). Just ask.

### 1.3 Notes per main option

- **Old Fisherman's Grotto**: family-run since 1950 (owner Chris Shake), self-described "originators of the 'Monterey Style' Clam Chowder". The Thanksgiving plate *and* the regular chowder/calamari menu are served the same day, so one person can get turkey and another chowder. On a normal day it is walk-in heavy with lines; on Thanksgiving, use OpenTable. Address 39 Fisherman's Wharf. **Coords [36.60438, -121.89293] (OSM)**.
- **Peter B's**: the cheapest verified turkey plate on the Peninsula, with no reservation stress. Downside: it's a sports bar showing football. It's in the Portola Hotel complex next to the wharf, so you can walk the wharf and Custom House Plaza after. **[36.60235, -121.89479] (OSM)**.
- **Asilomar**: the most "Thanksgiving-y" setting (historic conference grounds in the dunes; architect Julia Morgan, built 1913, per https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asilomar_Conference_Grounds). The dinner is early (last seating 4:30 PM). It fits a Thursday plan of **arrive → 17-Mile Drive / Asilomar beach at sunset → bonfire 6–9 PM**. The bonfire/live music is listed for "Thursday–Saturday 6–9 PM" at Social Hall. Only the Friday movie night is explicitly "open to the public", so whether non-guests can join the bonfire is UNVERIFIED. S'more kits are sold at Phoebe's Café. **[36.61857, -121.93845] (OSM "Crocker Dining Hall")**.
- **Sardine Factory**: the Cannery Row institution (opened Oct 2, 1968; "Best Wine List in America" award per the site). $73 for 4 courses is reasonable for this level. Business-casual dress is safer (dress code not stated). **[36.61564, -121.90162] (OSM)**.
- **Kids/teens pricing** is irrelevant here, but note the **senior** rates if anyone brings a parent.

### 1.5 Recommendation

1. **Default plan:** Thanksgiving **lunch/early dinner at Old Fisherman's Grotto ($51)**. Reserve on OpenTable now; if no table, walk in at **11:00 AM opening**. It covers both "Thanksgiving turkey" and "Monterey chowder" in one stop.
2. **If budget matters most:** **Peter B's $39.95 plate**, walk-in, any time 12–8 PM.
3. **If you want one special meal:** **Sardine Factory $73** (noon–8) *or* **Asilomar $95** (2–4:30 seatings, then the bonfire). Book Asilomar on Eventbrite **before Mon 11/23 4 PM PT**. It is non-refundable, so decide on the 4th person first.
4. **Fallback if everything is full:** Toribashi ramen (open) or Dametra (Carmel, open all holidays). Safeway (until 6 PM) for picnic food.
