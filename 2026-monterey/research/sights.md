# Monterey · Carmel: Sights & Activities (Thanksgiving weekend 2026)

**Researched:** 2026-11-10/11 · **Trip:** Thu Nov 26 (Thanksgiving) – Sat Nov 28, 2026, 2 nights · 3 adults (+ maybe 1), budget-conscious, own 2WD car from Berkeley

**How to read this**
- Every non-obvious fact has a source link (official/primary where possible). Anything I could not confirm for these exact dates is marked **UNVERIFIED** with the latest info found and its date.
- Advice I worked out myself (timing, crowds, sequencing) is labeled *(analysis)*.
- Drive times marked "OSRM" are free-flow road-router estimates. Big Sur has 24/7 one-way signal controls and holiday traffic, so add 30–60 min on a Big Sur day.
- Coordinates are `[lat, lng]`: **WP** = Wikipedia coordinates API/infobox; **OSM** = OpenStreetMap (Nominatim via `tools/geocode.py --any`, or Photon/komoot which searches OSM), matched name checked.
- Verdicts for this group: **MUST** / **RECOMMENDED** / **OPTIONAL** / **SKIP**.

### 2.3 Weather (normals)

- November at Monterey (NOAA 1991–2020 normals): mean high **62.5 °F**, mean low **46.0 °F**, **1.64 in** of rain over **7.7 days** with ≥0.01 in ([Wikipedia climate table, sourced to NOAA/NWS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterey,_California)).
- *(analysis)* Bring a rain shell and layers. A rainy day is a good aquarium/museum day.

### 2.4 Wildlife

- **Humpback whales: excellent right now.**
  - Monterey Bay Whale Watch's list since Nov 1, 2026 shows **4–12 humpbacks on every trip 11/1–11/7**, often with hundreds of dolphins.
  - Other sightings: 2 blue whales (11/5), mako sharks, and a rare Olive Ridley turtle (11/7) ([MBWW Nov list](https://www.montereybaywhalewatch.com/sightings/slstcurr/)).
  - **Orcas** were seen 10/23/26 ([Oct list](https://www.montereybaywhalewatch.com/sightings/slst2610/)).
  - Last year's Thanksgiving week (11/24–11/30/25) had **6–20 humpbacks per trip** ([Nov 2025 list](https://www.montereybaywhalewatch.com/sightings/slst2511/)).
  - KSBW (10/6/26) reported "more than 50 to 100 whales" feeding on anchovies ([MBWW homepage, "In the news"](https://www.montereybaywhalewatch.com/)).
  - Discovery Whale Watch's typical calendar: humpbacks Apr–Dec, **gray whales Dec–May**, orcas Apr/May & Sep/Oct ([Discovery](https://discoverywhalewatch.com/)). Gray whales are borderline for late Nov.
- **Sea otters (year-round)**
  - The best bets are Moss Landing / Elkhorn Slough (rafts of 2–100), Fisherman's Wharf, the Cannery Row shoreline, Lovers Point and Point Lobos ([See Monterey](https://www.seemonterey.com/things-to-do/animals-and-wildlife/top-5-places-to-spot-sea-otters/)).
  - Elkhorn Slough has "more than 100 Southern sea otters" ([CDFW](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Lands/Places-to-Visit/Elkhorn-Slough-ER)).
- **Monarch butterflies (peak season, but low numbers)**
  - PG says "Peak population will be around the end of November, but we predict the numbers will remain low in Pacific Grove" (updated 11/06/26).
  - Clusters show before 11 AM or on cool overcast days below 55 °F; on sunny afternoons they fly at the nectar beds ([PG Museum](https://www.pgmuseum.org/monarch-viewing)).
  - The 2025 Western Monarch Count (late Nov–early Dec 2025) found PG Sanctuary **188**, Natural Bridges **2,500** (highest in Santa Cruz County), and 12,260 statewide, the third-lowest ever ([Xerces, 2026-01-29](https://xerces.org/press/western-monarch-numbers-remain-at-historic-low)).
  - Natural Bridges' own page says 10,000 overwintered there in 2024 ([parks.ca.gov](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=541)).
- **Sea lions / harbor seals:** Old Fisherman's Wharf lists "Observing Sea Lions" as a top activity ([montereywharf.com](https://www.montereywharf.com/)). The Coast Guard Pier breakwater next to it is the usual haul-out *(analysis)*.
- **Brown pelicans** are leaving: Elkhorn Slough Safari ran a "Pelican Fall Farewell" tour on 11/7 because they "start their November journey south" ([Elkhorn Slough Safari](https://elkhornslough.com/fall-2026-specialty-tours)).

### A. Monterey (Cannery Row · Wharf · downtown)

**1. Monterey Bay Aquarium (MUST)**
- **Type:** world-class aquarium: sea otters, Kelp Forest, Open Sea, jellies, the deep-sea "Into the Deep" exhibit.
- **Time:** 3–4 h.
- **Cost:**
  - Adult (18–69) **$65**, youth (5–17) $50, senior (70+) $50. **No student price listed.**
  - "Tickets now on sale through December 31, 2026. Purchase your tickets in advance" ([tickets page](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/visit/admission-tickets)).
  - Re-entry the same day with a hand stamp; last entry 1 h before close ([FAQ](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/visit/visitor-faqs)).
  - Optional behind-the-scenes tours: $29–40 + admission ([schedule](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/visit/schedule)).
- **Parking:** no aquarium lot ([directions & parking](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/visit/directions-parking)). City options ([City rates](https://monterey.gov/your_city_hall/departments/public_works/parking/public_garages_and_lots.php)):
  - Cannery Row Garage (Foam & Hoffman): seasonal flat rate **$15–22 daily max, $5 after 5 PM**
  - CR7 (Foam & Irving): $20 daily max, $5 after 5 PM
  - Lot 21 (Cannery Row & Foam): $2/h, $14 max
- **Best time:** right at opening, or after ~2:30 PM; Thanksgiving Day is likely the calmest of the three *(analysis)*.
  - Feedings 11/26–28: sea otters 10:30 AM & 3:30 PM, Open Sea 11:00 AM, Kelp Forest dive 12:30 PM ([schedule](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/visit/schedule)).
- **Thu / Fri / Sat:** all open. Hours **UNVERIFIED**: 10–6 per [See Monterey](https://www.seemonterey.com/destinations/monterey/monterey-bay-aquarium/).
- **Crowds:** very high on Fri/Sat of a holiday weekend; expect a security-screening line ([FAQ](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/visit/visitor-faqs)).
- **Coords:** [36.6181, -121.9019] WP.

**2. Cannery Row (RECOMMENDED)**
- **Type:** historic sardine-cannery street (Steinbeck), shops, tasting rooms, ocean views.
- **Time:** 45–60 min (walk).
- **Cost:** free.
- **Parking:** same garages as the Aquarium.
- **Best time:** combine with the Aquarium. **Fri 11/27 evening: tree lighting 5:45–6 PM** ([See Monterey](https://www.seemonterey.com/event/cannery-row-tree-lighting/)).
- **Thu / Fri / Sat:** street always open; shop hours vary.
- **Crowds:** high Fri/Sat, very high on Fri evening.
- **Coords:** Steinbeck Plaza [36.61612, -121.89997] OSM; Cannery Row [36.6165, -121.9006] WP.

**3. Old Fisherman's Wharf (RECOMMENDED)**
- **Type:** historic wharf (est. 1845, 181st anniversary in 2026), clam chowder, whale-watch and boat departures, sea lions ([wharf](https://www.montereywharf.com/), [See Monterey](https://www.seemonterey.com/events/christmas/)).
- **Time:** 45–75 min.
- **Cost:** free to walk.
- **Parking:** Waterfront Lot #1 (245 Washington) is attended, **$15 max/day**; Wharf II lot is $2/h, $14 max ([City](https://monterey.gov/your_city_hall/departments/public_works/parking/public_garages_and_lots.php)).
- **Best time:** lunch, or around a whale-watch departure.
- **Thu / Fri / Sat:** open (restaurants vary on Thanksgiving).
- **Crowds:** medium–high.
- **Coords:** [36.60456, -121.89265] OSM (WP infobox 36.6044, -121.8928).

**4. Coast Guard Pier / breakwater + San Carlos Beach (RECOMMENDED, quick)**
- **Type:** sea lions on the breakwater, harbor seals, otters, kelp; a dive-entry beach.
- **Time:** 15–30 min.
- **Cost:** free.
- **Parking:** San Carlos Beach Lot $2/h, $14 max ([City](https://monterey.gov/your_city_hall/departments/public_works/parking/public_garages_and_lots.php)).

- **Thu / Fri / Sat:** open.
- **Crowds:** low–medium.
- **Coords:** pier [36.60903, -121.89184] OSM; San Carlos Beach Park [36.61033, -121.89653] OSM.

**5. Whale-watching trip from Fisherman's Wharf (RECOMMENDED: the trip's "one splurge" activity)**
- **Type:** 2.5–4 h boat trip into the Monterey Canyon.
