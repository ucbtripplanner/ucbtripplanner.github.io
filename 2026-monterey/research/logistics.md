# Monterey · Carmel Thanksgiving 2026: routes, logistics, safety and prep

Research date: **Tue 2026-11-10 to Wed 2026-11-11**. Trip: **Thu 11/26 to Sat 11/28/2026** (2 nights). 3 adults (maybe 4), Korean students at UC Berkeley (some may be international students). Own regular 2WD car, Berkeley → Monterey Peninsula and back, with an optional Big Sur day.

Legend:
- **[Official]**: page from the agency or venue itself (Caltrans, CA State Parks, NOAA/NWS/USNO, City of Monterey, City of Carmel, Pebble Beach Co., 511/MTC, AAA newsroom, CAL FIRE, EPA), fetched 11/10–11/11/2026.
- **[Secondary]**: news or third-party page. It is cited, but the agency's own page didn't confirm it.
- **UNVERIFIED**: I could not confirm it for the trip dates. The newest info I found is given with its date.
- Distances/times are **OSRM free-flow** (router.project-osrm.org, queried 11/10) from the UC Berkeley campus. Real holiday traffic is slower.

Companion topics (other files in this folder) cover sights, food and lodging. This file covers the practical side: how to get there, where to stop, how to drive, park and stay safe, and what to check before leaving.

## TL;DR

- **Best route (fast, boring, safest):** Berkeley → SR-13/I-580 → **I-880 S → US-101 S → SR-156 W → CA-1 S** → Monterey. **~116 mi / ~2 h 25 min** to the Aquarium, ~119 mi to Carmel. **No tolls.** Scenic alternatives:
  - **SR-17 via Santa Cruz:** ~118 mi / ~2 h 35 min, no tolls. It's curvy and an official **"high-collision corridor" since 1998** [Official, SCCRTC]. Only take it **in daylight and dry weather**.
  - **CA-1 coast via Half Moon Bay:** ~134 mi / ~3 h 15 min without stops, realistically 5–7 h with stops. **Bay Bridge toll $8.50** going west.
- **Traffic:** in AAA/INRIX's 2025 pattern, **Thanksgiving Day had "minimal traffic"**. On **Saturday the worst window was 1–8 pm and the best was before 10 am** [Official, AAA 2025]. AAA hadn't published the 2026 forecast as of 11/8 (it usually comes out around 11/17). **Thu-down / Sat-early-up is the right shape.** Black Friday: **Gilroy Premium Outlets (on US-101) opens at 6 am and stays open until 9 pm**. It's closed on Thanksgiving [Official].
- **Big Sur Highway 1 is open end to end.** It reopened Carmel–Cambria on **Jan 14, 2026** (Regent's Slide) and fully again on **Sep 14, 2026** after the Timber Fire. Caltrans (11/10, 11:57 pm) lists **24/7 one-way traffic control at Rocky Creek Bridge (through 6 am 11/30)** and at **various points from Point Sur Lighthouse to Big Sur (through 12/31)**. Expect signal waits [Official].
  - **Timber Fire (25,352 ac, 97 % contained):** every trail east of Hwy 1 is still closed. **Pfeiffer Big Sur SP reopened 10/16** with six trails closed (Pfeiffer Falls, Valley View and others). The Forest Service closure runs through **1/31/2027** [Official].
- **No parking at Bixby Bridge:** Monterey County banned parking on both sides of Hwy 1 from **~900 ft north to ~700 ft south of the bridge**. The ban started Aug 2026 and lasts 12 months [Secondary, CBS/County].
- **17-Mile Drive:** **$12.50 per car**, open **sunrise to sunset**, **motorcycles prohibited**. The fee is **refunded with a $35+ purchase at Pebble Beach Resorts restaurants** (not the Pebble Beach Market) [Official, Pebble Beach Co. via archived page 11/1/2026]. Sunset is **4:53 pm**, so enter by about 3:30 pm.
- **Parking cheat sheet:**
  - **Monterey Cannery Row Garage:** **$15–22 per day**, $5 after 5 pm. Cannery Row meters **$1.75–2.25/h**, enforced daily 9 am–8 pm. Wharf lots **$2/h ($14 max)**. Downtown garages: **first hour free**, then $1 per 30 min, **$12 max** [Official, City of Monterey].
  - **Carmel-by-the-Sea:** parking is **free**, with **2-hour limits downtown**. Unlimited spots: **Vista Lobos lot** (Torres & 3rd), **Sunset Center north lot** (8th Ave), and Scenic Rd 5 am–midnight. Paid parking is still only proposed [Official].
  - **Point Lobos:** **$10 per car, ~150 spaces, open 8 am–5 pm (last entry 4:30 pm)**. Rangers ticket illegal shoulder parking on Hwy 1 (**$51**).
  - **Pacific Grove:** mostly free.
- **Carmel now has street numbers.** The "no street addresses" fact is out of date. Carmel adopted numbered addresses in **2026** (ordinance adopted 1/13/2026, numbers installed Sep 2026). Google Maps already has them and other apps are catching up. **Mail still goes to P.O. boxes** [Official timeline + Secondary, KSBW 9/11/2026]. Navigate by business name or cross-streets ("Dolores St & 7th Ave").
- **Ocean is the #1 safety risk, not crime.**
  - **King Tides Nov 24–26** [Official, CA Coastal Commission]. Monterey high tide is **6.8 ft at 10:26 am on Thanksgiving**, then **−1.5 ft at 6:09 pm** [Official, NOAA 9413450].
  - **Strong El Niño** (CPC "strong-to-very strong").
  - In **Nov 2025, two separate sneaker-wave incidents at Garrapata SP killed a father and daughter, and a man went missing a week later.**
  - **Monastery Beach (Carmel River SB) is notoriously deadly.** **Carmel Beach has no lifeguards.**
  - Stay off wet rocks, stay above the berm, and never turn your back on the waves.
- **Car break-ins:** standard California coastal-trailhead risk at Hwy 1 pullouts, Point Lobos shoulders, beach lots and Cannery Row garages. **Put everything in the trunk before you arrive**, and don't leave luggage in the car on check-out day. I found no Monterey-specific 2026 statistics (**UNVERIFIED** level).
- **Weather (NOAA 1991–2020 normals, Monterey airport, 11/26–28):** high **~63 °F**, low **~44 °F**, **~28 % chance per day of measurable rain** (~14 % of ≥0.10″). **Sunrise 6:57–6:58 am, sunset 4:53 pm**, civil twilight ends 5:21 pm (USNO). The CPC week 3–4 outlook (issued 11/6, valid 11/21–12/4) **favors above-normal precipitation across much of California**.
- **Gas** (AAA, 11/10/26): CA avg **$5.71**. Oakland $5.70, San Jose $5.66, Santa Cruz–Watsonville $5.80, **Salinas/Monterey Co. $5.81**. Fuel for ~300–360 mi is **≈ $50–85 per car**. Fill up in Monterey/Carmel before Big Sur, where gas is scarce and expensive. **Costco is closed Thanksgiving Day** [Secondary].
- **Medical:**
  - **Nearest ER:** **Community Hospital of the Monterey Peninsula (Montage Health), 23625 Holman Hwy, Monterey**, 24/7 [Official, 211 Monterey Co.]. It's ~5 min from Carmel and ~45 min from Big Sur village.
  - **Trauma:** Natividad in Salinas, the only Level II trauma center in Monterey County [Secondary].
  - **Big Sur has no ER.** The Big Sur Health Center clinic is open Mon–Fri only.

### 1.2 Route A details (I-880 → US-101 → SR-156 → CA-1)
- **I-880 Express Lanes** run from Hegenberger Rd (Oakland) to Dixon Landing Rd (Milpitas). On weekdays, stay out of them unless you have a FasTrak Flex (see §4).
- **US-101 through south San Jose, Morgan Hill and Gilroy** is the main holiday choke point. On **Black Friday** the outlets at the **Leavesley Rd exit (Gilroy)** draw extra traffic, since Gilroy Premium Outlets opens at 6 am [Official, premiumoutlets.com].
- **SR-156 W (Prunedale → Castroville)** is partly a two-lane road. The **traffic signal at Castroville Blvd is "the only stop light on the heavily-traveled Route 156"**, a known bottleneck [Official, TAMC]. Caltrans/TAMC's SR-156/Castroville Blvd interchange project was going to construction bids in late Sep 2026 [Official, TAMC slide deck]. The Caltrans conditions on 11/10 showed **no restrictions on SR-156**. Construction-related lane closures over Thanksgiving are **UNVERIFIED** but unlikely.
- **CA-1 from Castroville to Monterey** is a 4-lane freeway past Marina, Seaside and Sand City. Moss Landing is **~3 mi north of the SR-156/CA-1 junction**, a ~10-minute detour each way.

### 1.3 Route B: SR-17 (Santa Cruz Mountains)
- **Why people warn about it:** the Highway 17 corridor "was identified as a **high-collision corridor in 1998**". The multi-agency **Safe on 17** task force funds **extra CHP enforcement (~$150k/yr)** and targets the top crash causes: **unsafe speed, right-of-way violations, improper turning, improper lane change**. It also works on **wet-weather and run-off-road collisions** [Official, SCCRTC fact sheet updated Oct 2026].
  - A law-firm blog citing crash data claims **690 collisions in 2024 (237 injury)** [Secondary, low-confidence].
- **Character:** tight blind curves, sudden slow-downs, tailgating locals and a posted ~50 mph limit over the summit [Secondary; exact posted limits **UNVERIFIED**]. It's slippery in the first rain of the season.
- **Rule of thumb for this group:** use it **southbound on Thu morning in dry daylight** if you want Santa Cruz. **Avoid it at night, in rain, or with a tired or inexperienced driver.** Don't make it the Saturday return in the dark.
- Caltrans (11/10): **no restrictions on SR-17** [Official].
- After SR-17, **CA-1 through Santa Cruz → Watsonville** is often slow on weekend afternoons (**UNVERIFIED** for these dates).

### 1.4 Route C: coastal CA-1 via Half Moon Bay
- **Toll:** the Bay Bridge westbound toll is **$8.50** (2026, all state-owned bridges, collected one way). There are **no cash lanes** [Official, 511]. See §4.
- **Driving it:** a two-lane road with few passing spots, cyclists, and fog or low sun. **Southbound is better**: the ocean-side turnouts are on your right.
- **Caltrans (11/10):** one-way control at **Waddell Creek** (Santa Cruz/San Mateo line), 7 am–4 pm Tue–Fri, **through 11/13 only**, so it ends before the trip [Official].
- **Stops worth knowing (late-Nov status):**
  - **Pigeon Point Light Station SHP:** **free** admission and parking. Grounds open 8 am–sunset; visitor center Thu–Tue 10 am–4 pm. The **lighthouse tower is closed** for restoration (since 2/2024) [Official, parks.ca.gov, checked 11/10/2026].
  - **Año Nuevo SP:** $10 per vehicle. Day use 8:30 am–sunset; seal viewing 8:30 am–3:30 pm. **Sep 1–Nov 30 is the "Juvenile Seal Haul-out" season: a free same-day visitor permit is required**, and you must exit by 5 pm. The **Wildlife Viewing area closes Dec 1–14**, and guided walks start 12/15 [Official, parks.ca.gov].
    - The walk out takes a few hours, so it doesn't fit a drive-down day well.
    - Note the 2/25/2026 State Parks alert about **avian influenza (H5N1) confirmed in elephant seals**: keep your distance.
  - Half Moon Bay, Pescadero, Davenport and Wilder Ranch (sights and food: see the other files).
- **Time reality:** 3 h 15 min of free-flow driving + 2–3 stops = **leave Berkeley by ~8 am to reach Monterey by ~2–3 pm**, still before the 4:53 pm sunset.

### 6.2 17-Mile Drive rules [Official, pebblebeach.com, archived 11/1/2026]
- "Admission to 17-Mile Drive is **$12.50 per vehicle**." It's "reimbursed with a purchase of **$35 or more** at all Pebble Beach Resorts restaurants, **excluding Pebble Beach Market**." Keep the gate receipt.
- "The 17-Mile Drive gates are open to the public from **sunrise to sunset**. **Motorcycles are prohibited.**" Overnight guests of the Lodge, Inn at Spanish Bay or Casa Palmero get in free.
- Gates [Secondary, carmellife.com; names vary between sources]:
  - **Pacific Grove Gate** (Sunset Dr, near Asilomar): the natural entry from Monterey/PG.
  - **Carmel Gate** (N. San Antonio Ave, Carmel): the natural exit into Carmel.
  - Plus the Country Club Gate and the gates off Hwy 1 / Hwy 68 (Holman Hwy).
  - Payment is "cash or card" [Secondary, **UNVERIFIED**].
- My tips:
  - Drive it **PG Gate → Carmel Gate** (ocean on your right).
  - Park only in the marked turnouts.
  - Finish by **4:53 pm** sunset. On Thanksgiving weekend, a visit right after the 6:57 am sunrise or before ~3:30 pm entry beats the midday tour-bus peak (**UNVERIFIED** crowd pattern).

### 6.3 Big Sur Highway 1: status and rules
- **History in 2026:**
  - **Regent's Slide** (north of Lucia) reopened **Jan 14, 2026**, restoring Carmel–Cambria [Official, gov.ca.gov / Caltrans D5 release].
  - **Feb 2026 storms:** about 4 days of closures, then reopened with one-way control 2/20 [Secondary, SLO Tribune].
  - **Aug–Sep 2026 Timber Fire:** closures; "As of Sep. 14, Caltrans has reopened all of Highway 1 through Big Sur" [Secondary, KAZU]. The Big Sur Chamber says "Highway One is now fully open through Big Sur" [Secondary, bigsurcalifornia.org, 9/14/2026].
- **Now:** open, with 24/7 signal-controlled one-lane sections at **Rocky Creek Bridge** (just north of Bixby) and **between Point Sur and Big Sur** (§1.5). Budget **+10–20 min** each way (**UNVERIFIED** wait times).
- **Timber Fire aftermath** [Official, CAL FIRE incident page updated 10/20/2026; parks.ca.gov checked 11/10/2026]:
  - 25,352 acres, 97 % contained. All evacuation orders lifted 10/7.

## 8. Car break-ins

**How big the risk is:** I found **no Monterey-, Carmel- or Big Sur-specific 2026 break-in statistics or official warnings** (**UNVERIFIED** level).
- Carmel PD reported steep declines in burglary and vehicle theft in 2025 [Secondary, NewsBreak 3/19/2026].
- Unattended cars at **remote trailheads and scenic pullouts** are the classic California smash-and-grab target, and Bay Area and LA police repeat the same advice ("Lock it, Hide it, Keep it").
- A Cannery Row Garage reviewer: "I still would not recommend leaving anything valuable visible in your car" [Secondary, Yelp].

**Highest-exposure spots on this trip** (my judgment):
- Hwy 1 roadside pullouts: Garrapata/Soberanes, Big Sur turnouts, Point Lobos shoulder parking
- Sunset Dr at Asilomar and Ocean View Blvd at Lovers Point
- The Carmel Beach / Scenic Rd area after dark
- Busy Cannery Row lots

**What to do:**
1. **Nothing in plain view**, not even a jacket, bag, charging cable or phone mount.
2. **Move items into the trunk *before* you arrive**, not in the parking lot where someone can watch.
3. **Luggage day = risk day.**
   - **Thu:** go to the hotel and drop the bags first, or keep the bags hidden and stops short.
   - **Sat (check-out):** plan to drive straight home, or only make stops where someone stays with the car.
4. Keep passports and I-20/DS-2019 in the hotel safe or on your body, never in the car.
5. **If it happens:**
   - Call **911** if it's in progress or someone is hurt.
   - Otherwise call the local police non-emergency line: Monterey PD (831) 646-3914; Carmel PD (831) 624-6403; Pacific Grove PD (831) 647-7900 (§12). Big Sur and unincorporated areas are covered by the Monterey County Sheriff.
   - Take photos and file a report for insurance.

## 9. Ocean safety: sneaker waves, king tides, rip currents

### 9.1 Why this trip's dates are higher-risk than usual
- **King Tides:** the California Coastal Commission's statewide windows are "**November 24-26, 2026**", Dec 23–25 and Jan 21–22, 2027. The Commission warns of "actual water heights [exceeding] tide predictions as well as significant dangers, including landslides, flooding, and unsafe ocean waters" [Official, coastal.ca.gov/kingtides].
- **Strong El Niño:** "a strong-to-very strong El Niño likely through January-March 2027", with a decent chance of a historic-strength event [Official, CPC ENSO discussion 10/8/2026]. Higher sea levels and bigger winter swells are typical.
  - **This week's evidence:** NWS Bay Area issued a **Coastal Flood Advisory for Southern Monterey Bay/Big Sur Coast through Fri 11/13**, with tides forecast **1.1–1.4 ft above predicted (6.9–7.2 ft MLLW)**. It warned: "Waves can sweep across the shoreline without warning, pulling people into the sea from beaches, jetties, and rocks" [Official, NWS MTR CFW 11/10/2026 12:11 pm].
  - Early November also brought a Beach Hazards Statement for "Southern Monterey Bay and Big Sur Coast" with **breaking waves of 12–16 ft** [Official snippet via NWS WWA summary; Secondary, CBS SF ~11/6/2026].
- **Recent deaths at the same time of year:**
  - **Nov 2025, Garrapata SP:** a 39-year-old father and his 7-year-old daughter were swept off by **15–20 ft waves** and died.
  - **One week later (Nov 22, 2025, ~3 pm):** three people were swept off the rocks at **Soberanes Point**. Two women self-rescued and one man was missing [Secondary, CSLEA 11/25/2025 citing Monterey County Sheriff; State Parks headline "Three People Swept into Ocean at Garrapata State Park" (11/22/25) on parks.ca.gov].

**How to use this:**
- **Late mornings (≈ 9 am–1 pm) are king-tide high water.** Beaches are at their narrowest and rock ledges are awash, and an El Niño anomaly could push the water above 7 ft. **Don't walk out on rocks or under bluffs then.**
- **Thu and Fri late afternoons are on a falling tide.** Tidepools open up ~3:30–4:50 pm (Asilomar, Lovers Point, Pacific Grove shoreline). Point Lobos closes at 5 pm (last entry 4:30), so Thursday is the better tidepool day there.
- **The lowest tides come after dark. Don't tidepool by headlamp on this coast.**

### 9.3 Beach-by-beach
- **Carmel Beach:** "There are **no lifeguards** at Carmel Beach. Please remain aware of waves, undertow, riptides" [Official, City of Carmel]. Steep, with powerful shore break.
- **Monastery Beach / Carmel River SB:** "Swimming, wading or walking near the [surf] can be **extremely dangerous** at this beach". State Parks: "Stay high and dry (Stay on high, dry sand above the berm … as Monastery is a steep beach)", and "Never turn your back on waves" [Official, parks.ca.gov 567 and News Release 896]. **Look, don't touch the water.**
- **Garrapata / Soberanes Point:** the site of the two Nov 2025 incidents. Stay on the trail, off the wave-washed rocks.
- **Point Lobos:** cliff edges and coves (Weston Beach, China Cove). Stay behind the ropes.
- **McWay Falls:** the beach and cliff areas are "completely off limits"; trespassing means a citation and arrest [Official].
- **Asilomar / Pacific Grove / 17-Mile (Point Joe "Restless Sea"):** rocky shore with surge channels. Rogue sets reach far up the rocks.
- **Water temperature:** the Monterey gauge read an **unusually warm ~67 °F on 11/11/2026** (NDBC buoy 46042: 19.8 °C) [Official, NOAA]. It's still no swimming weather, and the problem here is waves and rip currents, not cold.

**Rules for the group:**
- Never turn your back on the ocean. Stay off wet or dark rocks and above the wet-sand line.
- Watch the waves for 10+ minutes before going closer.
- Use a phone strap near the water. **Don't chase a dropped phone, hat or drone into the surf.**
- If someone goes in, **call 911** and throw something that floats. Don't go in after them.
