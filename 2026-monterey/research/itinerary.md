## 0. Verdict

**Winner: candidate A's skeleton, with B's safety and verification fixes.** That means:
- Asilomar for 2 nights
- Thursday aquarium + low-tide sunset + Grotto Thanksgiving dinner
- **Friday Point Lobos at 8 AM, then Big Sur to Nepenthe and McWay**, back for the Carmel Beach sunset
- Saturday 17-Mile Drive → Carmel Mission → Phil's → home around midday

Why:
1. **Every stop falls on a day it's open.** Thanksgiving closes the Carmel Mission, Point Pinos Lighthouse, the PG Museum and Monterey's Fish House, but the aquarium and the whole coast stay open.
   - **Thursday** = the aquarium (11/26 programs are on the official schedule) plus the outdoor coast.
   - **Friday** = Point Lobos before the lot fills, Big Sur, the Cannery Row tree lighting (Friday only) and Fish House (closed on major holidays).
   - **Saturday** = the Mission (closed Thursday) and Phil's (eatery open Thu–Sat 10–8).
2. **The ocean timetable fits.** The trip's best low tide is **Thu 4 PM 0.0 ft → 5 PM −1.1 ft**, and sunset is 4:53. So the Asilomar tide pools and sunset go on Thursday, right across the street from the room. The king-tide highs fall in late morning (+6.8 ft Thu 10:26, +6.5 ft Fri 11:17), and the plan keeps people off the rocks then.
3. **Big Sur belongs in a first-timer "근본" course.** Bixby Bridge and McWay Falls are the MUSTs of a Big Sur day (sights §4E).
   - Friday is the only day that fits: Saturday is the drive home, and the AAA pattern has the worst traffic Sat 1–8 PM.
   - The day is shaped to be **north of Bixby by ~14:30**, well before 4:53 sunset and 5:21 dusk, with buffer for both signals.
   - B's "Bixby-only" version stays as a one-line swap (§8 variants) for a tired group or bad weather.
4. **Asilomar earns its ~$51/person premium over the Ramada:**
   - The Thursday sunset beach is across the street.
   - The 17-Mile PG gate is 3 min away and the aquarium 5.
   - Parking is free.
   - There are bonfires and live music every night 11/26–28, 6–9 PM.
   - It's refundable until 72 h before arrival.

   The Ramada is the #2 budget backup in the booking funnel.
5. **Two corrections from the 11/11 re-checks:**
   - **Toribashi** is dropped as a Thanksgiving fallback: its own site lists the Monterey location as "Thanksgiving Day: Closed".
   - **Aquarium hours** are planned as 9–5 (last entry 4 PM), the conservative reading (see §11). The plan works under either 9–5 or 10–6.
