How the prices below work:
- **Prices are the 2-night total for 1 room, before tax**, exactly as Booking's room table shows them. Booking lists mandatory property fees (service charge, resort/destination fee) under "Included" and taxes + per-night assessments under "Excluded".
- **"All-in" = room × (1 + tax %) + per-night flat charges × 2 + overnight parking × 2** [EST]. Flat charges = the "$X City tax / Tourism fee / Environmental fee per night" lines Booking lists as excluded.
- Rates are the **refundable "no prepayment / pay at property" (or "pay nothing until …") option** unless noted. See §1 for the trap rates to avoid.
- **"Free cancel before Nov 23"** is Booking's wording; the last *safe* day to cancel is the day before (Nov 22, property local time). I list Booking's date as shown.

## 1. TL;DR

### What matters most

- **Monterey on Thanksgiving weekend is not cheap, but it is not sold out.** On 11/10–11 Booking listed 58 properties in Monterey, 13 in Pacific Grove, 16 in Carmel (for 3 adults), 12 in Seaside, 11 in Marina [OBS]. Decent 2-queen rooms start at **≈$330–410 all-in for 2 nights** (Munras Ave / North Fremont motels) and **≈$540–565 at Asilomar** (was ≈$480–500 at midnight; it is rising).
- **Pacific Grove's Asilomar is the standout for this group.**
  - State-park lodge on the beach, run by Aramark for California State Parks [SITE].
  - Official engine: **Historic Room, 2 doubles, "Sleeps 4": $477.37 (3 adults) / $497.37 (4 adults)** for 2 nights before tax [OBS 09:27/09:31 PST 11/11]. It was $421.76 / $441.76 at 00:04, so it rose $55.61 overnight. All-in ≈ **$542 / $564** [EST].
  - **Free parking** [SITE]. **3 min to the 17-Mile Drive Pacific Grove gate, 5 min to the Aquarium** (OSRM, §3).
  - It solves Thanksgiving dinner: **Thanksgiving dinner in the Crocker Dining Hall, Thu 11/26, 2:00–4:30 pm, adults $95 + tax & service**, plus **bonfires with live music every evening 11/26–11/28, 6–9 pm** [SITE]. Reserve the dinner separately (831-372-8016 or the Eventbrite link on the page).
  - Trade-offs: rustic/historic rooms, **no telephones in guest rooms** [SITE]. Booking's listing says **"No telephones, TVs or microwaves are included in the guest rooms"** [BKG]; the official site mentions a TV only in the Forest Lodge suite. Booking guest score only 7.2/10 (597 reviews) [BKG]. A **1-night deposit + tax is charged at booking**; full refund if cancelled **72 h before the arrival date** [SITE/OBS].
- **Carmel is the most expensive and least practical base.**
  - Carmel rooms are small: at Hofsas House, Comfort Inn Carmel and Carmel Oaks, Booking could only fit 3 adults into **two rooms** [OBS].
  - The cheapest Carmel-village room for 3 is **Carmel Inn & Suites at $632 + $31/night fees + 12 % ≈ $770** (guest score 6.6) [OBS 09:3x/EST]. The $632 is after a 12 % "Bonus savings" discount; it was $718 (≈$866 all-in) at midnight.
  - **Better: sleep in Monterey/PG and drive to Carmel.** Carmel's Ocean Ave is **6–12 min** from Munras Ave/PG, and Point Lobos is 10–16 min (§3).
  - "2-night minimum": **not observed.** Booking sold 1-night (Fri 11/27) stays at 19 Carmel properties [OBS]. Direct-booking holiday minimums at individual inns are UNVERIFIED. Our stay is 2 nights anyway.
- **Parking is mostly free outside downtown/Cannery Row.**

### Booking strategy (short version; details in §7)

1. **Today–tomorrow (Nov 11–12):** hold **Asilomar direct** (deposit = 1 night + tax, refundable until 72 h before) **and/or** one free-cancel, pay-at-property Monterey motel as a backup. Booking's deadlines are Nov 23–25. Asilomar's direct price rose $56 between 00:04 and 09:27 on 11/11, and Booking no longer lists its standard 2-double room. If Asilomar is the plan, book it first.
2. **Only pick rates labelled "No prepayment needed – pay at the property" or "Pay nothing until …".** Never "Pay online · cancel within 24 hours" (nonrefundable from ~Nov 12) or "Partner Offer".
3. Book with the **booker's own credit card**. Everyone checks in with a photo ID + credit card; deposits run $50–250 [BKG].
4. **Decide 3 vs 4 by ~Nov 20** and update the occupancy (+$0–40 at most places).
5. **Re-shop Nov 18–21**, before the earliest deadlines (HGI Nov 22, Asilomar Nov 23, Days Inn DT / Lone Oak Nov 23). Cancel the extra booking.

## 7. Booking strategy (detailed)

1. **Hold refundable rooms now (Nov 11–12).** Supply exists, but some good-value rooms already show "We have N left" (e.g., Days Inn Downtown "We have 4 left"), and refundable rates on the property pages were often well above the search-list price (Victorian Inn $780 vs $848, Cannery Row Inn $609 vs $707) [OBS].
   - **Asilomar direct:** pays 1 night + tax now and is fully refundable until 72 h before arrival. It's also the cheapest way to get Asilomar: **$477.37 vs $577 on Booking for 3** (09:3x). The direct price was $421.76 at midnight, so don't wait.
   - **Backup:** one pay-at-property Monterey motel. Best deadlines:
     - Super 8 **Nov 25**
     - Ramada Monterey **Nov 25** ($341 / $358 with the Late Escape Deal at 09:3x)
     - Bayside **Nov 25**
     - Days Inn FW **Nov 24**
     - Arbor **Nov 24** (pay Nov 22)
     - Villa Franca **Nov 24**
     - Days Inn DT **Nov 23**
     - Lone Oak **Nov 23** (pay Nov 21)
2. **Rate types:**
   - ✅ **"No prepayment needed – pay at the property"** and **"Pay nothing until Nov X"**: refundable until the stated date.
   - ❌ **"Pay online · Cancel without fees within 24 hours of booking · Fully refundable during free cancellation"**: refundable only until 14 days before arrival (≈Nov 12), then **100 % charged** [OBS fine print].
   - ❌ **"Partner Offer"** (Monterey Bay Lodge $458): free only until 23:58 Nov 11, $154 fee after, full price from Nov 23, no modifications [OBS].
   - Booking's "**before** Nov 23" means cancel **by Nov 22** (property time) to be safe.
3. **Book under one person's own credit card.** Every listing requires "a photo ID and credit card at check-in" [BKG]. Debit cards may be refused or have larger holds (UNVERIFIED per hotel). Damage deposits [BKG]:
   - $50: Victorian, HIE Cannery Row, Hofsas
   - $100: Travelodge, Ramada, Lone Oak (**cash**), Super 8, Park Crest, Wilkie's, Carmel Inn & Suites
   - $150: Arbor, Monterey Bay Lodge, SureStay, Stage Coach
   - $200: Padre Oaks, El Castell, Cannery Row Inn, Bayside, Blu Pacific, Sand Dollar, Bay Breeze, HIE Marina
   - $250: Discovery Inn, Aura Inn
   - Some charge the full stay on arrival day: Arbor, Lone Oak.
4. **Mind age rules.** If the booker is under 21, skip HIE Cannery Row, Hampton Inn Monterey, Discovery Inn, Comfort Inn Marina and Hampton Marina [BKG].
5. **Thanksgiving-day arrival.** Leaving Berkeley Thursday morning puts you at the hotel by early afternoon; check-in starts 2–4 pm.
   - If arriving late, avoid or pre-arrange: Wilkie's (desk closes **9 pm**), Sea Breeze (9:30), Asilomar / Arbor / Villa Franca / Bayside (10 pm), Discovery (10:30), Lone Oak / Super 8 / Ramada (11 pm).
   - If doing the **Asilomar Thanksgiving dinner (2:00–4:30 pm)**, plan to arrive by ~1:30 pm and **reserve the dinner separately** (831-372-8016 / online) [SITE].
6. **Headcount.** Decide 3 vs 4 by ~Nov 20 and update the reservation occupancy.
   - Same price for 4: Villa Franca, Lone Oak, Bayside, Monterey Bay Lodge, Bay Breeze, Ramada Marina, Butterfly Grove, Marriott.
   - +$10–40: Asilomar (+$20), Days Inns (+$20), Super 8 (+$10), Arbor (+$30).
   - Unregistered extra guests can void a booking.
7. **Re-shop Nov 18–21,** before the earliest deadlines:
   - HIE Cannery Row / Butterfly Grove / Sea Breeze: Nov 19
   - Carmel Inn & Suites: Nov 21
   - HGI / Embassy: Nov 22
   - Asilomar / Days Inn DT / Lone Oak / Padre Oaks / Park Crest / Marriott: Nov 23

   Rebook first, then cancel the old reservation.
8. **Direct vs Booking.**
   - Asilomar direct was **$24 cheaper** at midnight and **≈$100 cheaper** at 09:3x.
   - Cannery Row Inn **waives parking** for direct bookings [SITE].
   - Chain sites (Wyndham, Choice, Hilton, Marriott, BW) weren't compared because they block automated access. Worth a 2-minute check for loyalty points and member rates.
9. **Car safety and parking.** Keep valuables out of sight and unload at the hotel (general advice; see `logistics.md` if written). Downtown/Cannery Row garages charge by the hour (rates UNVERIFIED here).
